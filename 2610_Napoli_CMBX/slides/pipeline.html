Catalogues to a blinded likelihood

The KP2-3 pipeline end to end. Euclid TR1 shear and positions are pixelised into maps with their masks and redshift distributions; the CMB-lensing inputs are the Planck PR4, ACT DR6 and SPT-3G Main convergence maps. NaMaster pseudo-$C_\ell$ turns them into bandpowers for all six families: the three Euclid 3×2pt spectra and the three involving CMB lensing.

Two steps run in parallel on the bandpowers. The covariance is analytic and Gaussian, in NaMaster's improved narrow-kernel approximation (iNKA), and covers every block including the cross-blocks between families. The blind is a parameter-shift blind in the style of Muir et al. (2019): a sealed shift in cosmology is turned into a $\Delta C_\ell$ added to the whole six-family data vector, so shapes, signal-to-noise, the covariance and the null tests stay visible while the inferred cosmology is hidden. We run it ourselves because the official pipeline blinds only the Euclid 2pt spectra.

The theory model computes the six families with CCL and passes them through the same bandpower windows as the data. The likelihood varies it with the nuisances: linear galaxy bias, photometric-redshift shifts, shear calibration $m$ and intrinsic alignments. The blinded TR1 likelihood input exists; the TR1 chains come next.

The covariance box shows both covariances: the analytic Gaussian one, in the improved narrow-kernel approximation (iNKA), the pipeline uses now, and the numerical one from FLASK lognormal simulations that the simulations workstream is building. How each is validated is on the next slide, with the mocks.

The simulations box: GLASS and FLASK lognormal mocks on the TR1 footprint with the delivered n(z), run through the same NaMaster step as the data. GLASS validates the pipeline (the mock-recovery slide); FLASK gives the numerical covariance.

Each box opens the workstream that owns it, with its scope and its people: hover a box (the + in its corner) for a popout, click to open it whole. Data vectors and systematics owns the inputs and the bandpowers, simulations and covariance the mocks and the covariance, likelihood and theory the theory model and the likelihood. The blind is the pipeline's own step, shared across workstreams. The workstream scopes and members are carried over from the Barcelona KP2-3 update by Legrand, Lembo and Fabbian.

Data vectors and systematics

  • Production of data vectors combining Euclid RR2 and TR1 (clustering and shear) with CMB lensing maps from ACT and SPT
  • Validation of the data vectors with systematics tests

Likelihood and theory

  • CMBX likelihood based on PyCCL + Cobaya
  • Implemented CMB lensing observable in cloe-lib, working to implement in cloe-like

Simulations and covariance

  • Flask simulations to obtain numerical covariances
  • Calibrated on DEMNUni N-body simulations
