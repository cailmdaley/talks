Open goals for KP2-3

  • Scale cuts and consistency: validated cut options; LensMC vs MetaCal, SPT-3G vs ACT DR6
  • Systematics: null tests calibrated on mocks, then deprojection
  • Likelihood: organise its development across the KP, building on the 3×2pt teams' models rather than rebuilding them
  • TR1 chains with the full nuisance model, then DR1

The pipeline runs end to end, so the open work is about choices and validation. Scale cuts are the largest: the data-vector slide shows every band, because where to cut depends on how far the linear-bias model and the nuisance treatment hold, per family and per bin, and that is a decision to make explicitly, with each option tested against perturbation-theory contamination on theory and on the mocks. The data vectors exist for two shear methods and two CMB lensing maps, and their agreement is a check in itself. The systematics battery crosses survey-property templates with the galaxy density and shear maps and measures what deprojection changes. For the 3×2pt side of the model (intrinsic alignments, redshift and shear-calibration nuisances, the 3×2pt likelihood itself), the aim is to rely on the work of the 3×2pt experts rather than rebuild it; organising the likelihood development across the key project is part of that. Chains on the TR1 data come after these, followed by DR1.
