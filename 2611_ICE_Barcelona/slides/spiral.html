Pipeline, simulations and data, held against each other

  • the pipeline recovers known inputs from simulations before it sees data
  • simulations are compared with the data and made more realistic where they fall short
  • the vetted pipeline meets the data, which shows what the simulations missed
  • Two arms — the analysis pipeline (red) and the simulations (teal), each turn held against the other and against the data (centre), tightening until they meet at the finished analysis.
  • In this talk — the GLASS mocks under both analyses, the mock calibration of UNIONS' two pipelines, the blinding rehearsals, the Euclid × CMB mocks being conditioned on the CMB experiments' own reconstruction simulations.
  • Why it suits agents — every step is checked against a known answer, so pipelines, simulations and comparisons can be built and run by agents while the scientist's attention goes to design, interpretation and judgment.
