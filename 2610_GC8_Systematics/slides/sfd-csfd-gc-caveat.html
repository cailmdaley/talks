CSFD's galaxy null is partly by construction

  • SFD: $\chi^2 = 27.7$–$72.5$ for 15 bands, $\rho \approx +0.03$ to $+0.06$
  • CSFD: $\chi^2 = 6.1$–$15.9$, PTE 0.39–0.98; $+2.7\sigma$ offset in bin 1
  • CSFD's template is built from WISE galaxies on TR1's sky: the null is partly by construction
  • Independent evidence: the CIB route (slide 5)

Chiang (2023) builds CSFD's extragalactic template from 180 WISE density maps (CatWISE2020 and WISE×SuperCOSMOS, $z \approx 0$–3), so its sky and redshifts overlap TR1's lens sample ($0.2 \lt z_\mathrm{ph} \lt 1.0$). Suppressing galaxy-correlated structure is the reconstruction's objective, and the paper itself treats stacks on its own training samples as a sanity check, not a validation (§4.2). Euclid objects were not named as inputs and no object-by-object overlap with WISE was tested, so a Euclid null is not guaranteed algebraically; it is a transfer check of Chiang's correction. The same holds for the strong negative CSFD − SFD correlation, which is the removed component and is negative in all 90 bin × band points. The CIB-route test (slide 5) rests on the Lenz CIB maps, which no galaxy survey built, and on shear as well as density.
