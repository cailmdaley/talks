  • Method — every row except clusters uses the Muir et al. (2020) data-vector shift: theory at a hidden cosmology minus theory at the fiducial, added to the measurement.
  • WL / 3×2pt — JC-1 generates shift files; OU-LE3 applies one after the LE3 pipelines run, and only BLINDED-tagged products reach the SAS.
  • GC spectro — runs its own shifts through the cloe-org blindspot notebooks; the broad-band amplitude is deliberately left unblinded.
  • Clusters — shift the posterior before saving it, DES-style.
  • CMBX — a self-blind of the whole 6×2pt vector, applied when each spectrum is written, so no unblinded spectrum reaches disk.
  • Bottom line — the official pipeline does not blind cross-correlations, catalogues, n(z) or the shear calibration; nothing blinds the CMB-lensing crosses unless CMBX does.
  • Separate blinds — "The actual blinding realization is different for each project. Unless a project explicitly involves a joint probe analysis, comparing results, even if based on the same data, should be avoided. By keeping the blinds separate, projects can unblind independently." (Redmine BlindingWL, v2, November 2023.)
  • JC-1 matrix — in JC-1's blinding deck (Marseille 2025, DMSF 26503) each column (a key project) has one consistent shift and each row (an observable) has different shifts across projects; "3x2pt and CMBX have inconsistent shifts".
  • Unblinding — "decided by the Euclid Science Coordination Group after being requested by the relevant key project leads" (consortium blinding-strategy draft, DMSF 19229). "The 3x2pt analysis cannot unblind before the individual probes" (BlindingWL).
  • Timing — LE3 coordination minutes, 30 September 2026. For CMBX: everything running by end of February, blinded data early April, chains and papers by end of May, unblinded rerun about a month later (Theory WG, CMBX telecon 12 November).
  • Conclusion — no group plans to share its blind with CMBX (also Guada Cañas-Herrera, WL-SWG meeting, Napoli, October 2026), so CMBX's blind is its own.
WL / 3×2pt

Flow from Redmine issue #25766 (3×2pt pipeline): SWG shift vectors, LE3 pipelines run as normal, a post-processing step applies the selected shift and tags the product BLINDED or UNBLINDED (in the data model since v10). The OU-LE3 Blinding Pipeline v1.0 passed the CCB in September 2026 (#36017). JC-1 delivered TR1 Cycle 1 shifts for $C_\ell$, $\xi$ and COSEBIs (#33161, resolved November 2025); blinded TR1 C1 products followed (#33337, April 2026) and TR1 C2 WL power spectra were blinded on 7 October 2026 (#35269). The JC-1 blinding deck (WL/SHE meeting, Marseille 2025, DMSF 26503) lists what is not blinded: "cross-correlations, 3-point statistics, catalog level, non-primary probes". n(z) is not blinded (WL-pipeline minutes, 2024).

The photometric shift cosmologies in blindspot's prototype notebook are 100 points drawn from a weak-lensing chain's posterior in $(\Omega_c, A_s)$, other parameters fixed. Whether the official TR1/DR1 3×2pt shifts used that notebook is not recorded in the repo.

GC spectroscopic

Blinded: the auto $\xi_\ell$ and $P_\ell$ multipoles and the 2D functions, before and after reconstruction; "no blinding on cross-correlation statistics" (Redmine BlindingGC). DR1 IT1 (#35938): 200 cosmologies uniform in $\omega_c$, $w_0$, $w_a$, kept only if the BAO and RSD parameters move by about 2σ of the expected DR1 errors. DR1 IT2 (#36596): $\omega_c$ ±0.0123, $w_0$ ±0.75, $w_a$ ±2.25, $h$ ±0.1, with per-bin 2σ tolerances. GC-1 is drafting written unblinding guidelines.

Clusters

CL-SWG Blinding Strategy page (v4, March 2025): run MCMC on unblinded data and shift the posterior before saving, with the shift parameters in a non-human-readable file. The page notes the con itself: the analysis team could unblind deliberately.

CMBX

Proposal: Redmine issue #31813 in dr1-kp-cmbx-3. A cosmology-shift blind over the full 6×2pt vector, drawn uniformly within ±0.1 in $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$ and ±0.075 in $S_8$ of Planck 2018 from a public list of 100 cosmologies, the chosen index sealed. Code: dr1_cmbx MR !9. Validation criteria and the unblinding procedure are open (#33511, #33512).

What this does to cross-group checks

The γγ, δγ and δδ spectra in the CMBX 6×2pt vector are the same statistics as the official 3×2pt ones, measured on the same galaxies. With different shifts, the two blinded copies differ by the difference of two hidden shifts, so a direct comparison is not meaningful while blinded. That is intended: it keeps each project's cosmology hidden from the other's. The cost is that a disagreement in measurement (masks, weights, estimator) cannot be told from a difference in blinds.

The other side of the trade-off

A shared shift preserves every relation between probes, so the combined vector stays one consistent cosmology. But the shift cancels in any cross-probe comparison: whether CMB lensing and 3×2pt agree on $S_8$ would then be visible while nominally blind. Whichever is chosen, a cross-probe consistency claim made after unblinding is protected only if the blinds were independent.
