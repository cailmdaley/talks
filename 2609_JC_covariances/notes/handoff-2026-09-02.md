## Engine (`nka_script.html`) — what slide 16 rests on

- `covariances()` fills three NK×NK matrices (NK = 150): `covE` exact 1D Gaussian
  covariance, `covN` NKA with C̄ = ½(C_ℓ+C_ℓ′), `covI` iNKA with ½(C̃_ℓ+C̃_ℓ′) against the
  same mask-product kernel. `Ctil` is stored already divided by `sumP` = ⟨w²⟩ (Parseval) —
  dividing by `MW2` again squares the mask fraction and saturates every panel.
- `projKernel()` / `project(cov)`: the 1D Legendre analogue, T[i][ℓ] = 2cos(2πℓθ_i),
  NTH = 22 bins over 0.05 of the box. Cov(ξ) = T · Cov(C̃) · Tᵀ.
- `projSet()` caches the nine panels. `normBy`/`diagOf` give correlation matrices
  normalised by the EXACT diagonal; `diffNoDiag` zeroes the diagonal.
- `drawProj(root)` paints canvases by class: `hFsky cFsky dFsky hInka cInka dInka cQuar dQuar`.
  `heatN(cv, mat, n, scale)` is `heat()` for any n; `heat()` stays NK-only.
- `PROJ = null` in `buildAll()` — slide 7's mask picker rebuilds slide 16 too.
- Ledger `hold:true` renders an entry one step early with `visibility:hidden`, so its box
  exists from the base state and nothing below the ledger moves when a fragment fires.
- `.caption` (28 px) is the one caption size in the deck.

## Pitfalls that cost time

- `.nka svg { display:block }` also hits MathJax's inline SVGs; the `mjx-container svg
  { display:inline }` exception is load-bearing.
- `drawMatrices()` grabbed every canvas on any slide; now scoped to `.mrow canvas`.
- 300 consecutive `mulberry` draws are sub-Poisson; catalogue positions use `xs128`.
- The figure-treatment helper multiplies against the PARENT's background, so an image wider
  than `.cardwrap` gets white bars. Card images cap at 1700 px.
- The Nagura card is a desaturated arXiv-page capture. The PDF cannot carry title and a
  ≥28 px abstract: at 300 dpi the abstract block alone needs 814 of the card's ~900 px.
- Slicing `custom.scss` between comment markers once deleted the paper-card block.
