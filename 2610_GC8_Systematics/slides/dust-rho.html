CSFD brings $\bar\rho$ close to zero

“Close to zero” describes the small correlation amplitudes, not every compressed mean lying within one or two errors of zero. The full 15-band zero-null tests use the complete Gaussian block. All thirteen CSFD full-spectrum PTEs exceed 0.27. The compressed δ1 mean is 0.0182 ± 0.0070, so an unqualified “all rho means consistent with zero” would be misleading. SFD is not zero either: its six density means span 0.0414–0.0575. These are uncalibrated marginal Gaussian diagnostics, not a simultaneous family test.

Each curve joins bins within one tracer family only; no line joins density, shear and kappa. The same full-block GLS compression and fixed fiducial signal normalization are used for each dust map; denominator uncertainty is not propagated. Only Cycle 2, LensMC E and SPT-3G have this matched saved three-map comparison. The density maps here include the delivered visibility correction, unlike the raw-density CIB-route slide. Verified against rho.json and the dust-comparison input: the six plotted VMPZ means are 0.09032945, 0.08604185, 0.09011848, 0.10622766, 0.12526974 and 0.13377997, exactly the c2/vmpz/g0–g5/vis rows in both files. The corresponding raw means are 0.09271624, 0.09604840, 0.09710744, 0.11068487, 0.12982566 and 0.13638149. Thus the C2 correction is small but not identically zero: reductions of 0.00239–0.01001 in rho-bar. Source: results/scratch/gc8/rho/rho_four_dust.json c2/{vmpz,sfd,csfd}/*/{vis,native}; rho/HEADLINE.md. All plotted means and one-error bars are inside the axis limits.

CSFD's LSS templates are built from WISE galaxies, constrained by spectroscopic galaxy/quasar correlations (Chiang 2023, arXiv:2306.03926, sections 3–5). A galaxy null is therefore partly by construction; it does not independently certify that every cosmological mode is preserved. The alternative-map comparison motivates a controlled visibility-input swap and mock recovery test, not an assertion that CSFD is perfect.
