Five templates are clean; extinction carries the CIB

Cycle 2: Euclid TR1 v2 on the official tomographic bins (26 Oct catalogues), LensMC, crossed with the SPT-3G Main bias+profile-hardened GMV κ. Template crosses are the production results/tr1_v2/systematics archives; σ_ab is the full NaMaster Gaussian covariance of the blinded Cycle-2 likelihood input (rerun5, 27 Oct). The builder checks that the two share the same field windows, hash for hash. MetaCal and ACT DR6 are not on Cycle 2 in this figure yet; their buttons say so.

Built in two steps. First one pair close up, $\gamma_6 \times \kappa$ against the extinction template: the card a click opens on the full triangle. Its top row is the template's measured cross with each of the pair's tracers, $C_\ell^{\gamma_6 S}$ and $C_\ell^{\kappa S}$, each scaled by the power of ℓ that evens its error bars, with its χ² over the 15 bands against zero and PTE (full Gaussian covariance of the null family). Its bottom panel is what that pair would pick up if both crosses were pure leakage, $X_\ell^{ab} = C_\ell^{aS} C_\ell^{bS}/C_\ell^{SS}$, in units of the pair's Gaussian error σ_ab. Then the view pulls back to all 90 pairs.

The five instrumental templates

Exposures, noise, PSF, zodiacal light and Gaia stars: no template cross fails (PTE above 10⁻⁴ everywhere), and over 5 × 1350 bands only three are resolved (|X| above twice its error), the largest 0.19 σ_ab. PSF shows edge triangles at ℓ ≈ 349 and 690: the template's own power dips about a hundredfold there, between its tiling harmonics, so X and its error both blow up (σ_X ≈ 12 and 2 σ_ab); none of those bands is resolved.

Extinction is not a null

The VMPZ extinction template is the Planck 2013 thermal-dust E(B−V) (R1.20), a far-infrared map that carries the cosmic infrared background. Its cross fails with every lens bin (χ² 154–311 on 15 bands), with SPT κ (59) and with the three highest source bins (65, 133, 313), rising with ℓ and positive. Dust obscuration would lower the counts and leave shear and κ alone; a positive cross with δ, κ and high-z shear is large-scale structure in the template. So X here is an upper bound on a contamination the data do not show, not an estimate of one: 257 bands resolved, up to 0.9 σ_ab in lens × high-z pairs at ℓ ≳ 2000. The template must not be deprojected; it would remove real clustering. GC-8 (28 Oct) carries the detail and the proposals for a dust map with less CIB.

Cells outlined in orange are those where both template crosses behind the cell fail (each PTE below 10⁻⁴). The PTEs are not calibrated on mocks yet (about a hundred correlated crosses per template), so the threshold is a guide. The errors on X propagate the two crosses' Gaussian errors with the template auto held fixed; the cross-cross covariance is left out.
