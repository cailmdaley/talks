Euclid DR1 overlaps every CMB lensing survey

The blue regions are the Euclid DR1 effective coverage: the DR1_R2 VMPZ weak-lensing effective-coverage map, built from 8964 wide-survey tiles in August 2025, seen around the south (left) and north (right) celestial poles. Each pixel holds the fraction of it that is effectively covered; the map needs VIS, NIR and ground-based bands together, so it is smaller than the area with VIS mosaics. The blue is drawn wherever the coverage is above zero. The map holds only wide-survey tiles, so all of it is shown: the two main regions plus the smaller fields around the north ecliptic pole and near the SPT field.

Every number on the slide is an effective area, the sum of coverage fraction times pixel area. DR1 covers 1647 deg², 1268 in the south and 379 in the north. The CMB lensing footprints arrive one at a time, and the legend gives each one's effective overlap with DR1: Planck PR4 1559 deg², nearly all of DR1 in both hemispheres; ACT DR6 (baseline lensing mask) 1094 deg², all in the south; SPT-3G Main, the 1500 deg² winter field, 369 deg² on the southern side of the DR1 south region, partly inside ACT. The north is seen by Planck alone.

Both panels are Lambert azimuthal equal-area projections centred on their pole, at the same scale, so drawn areas compare directly. Both have north up and east left, as on the sky, and share one vertical great circle (RA 30° above the south pole, RA 210° below the north pole), so RA increases to the left in both. Where a CMB mask boundary crosses DR1, its outline is drawn over the blue in that survey's colour; outlines follow only each mask's outer boundary, and holes smaller than 10 deg², such as point-source cuts, are not outlined.

Conventions: the drawn CMB footprints are mask pixels above 0.5 at the mask's native NSIDE 2048. An overlap sums the DR1 coverage fraction over pixels where the CMB mask is above zero, with the NSIDE 1024 coverage map upgraded to 2048. The maps are drawn at NSIDE 512.

On the tiles LE3-WL lists as valid for DR1 (8389 of the 8964 R2 tiles), the effective areas are 1589 deg² for Euclid and 1502, 1069 and 353 deg² for the overlaps with Planck, ACT and SPT-3G Main; the slide shows all R2 tiles.
