For DR1 it is even better: 6×2pt × Planck matches fixing all six mᵢ (σ₈ ×0.24 vs ×0.25, Σmν ×0.47 vs ×0.49) and shrinks σ(mᵢ) six-fold, 0.10 → 0.02.

On the contributed slide

ΛCDM · 13+13 bins, 14 700 deg² · pessimistic cuts (ℓ ≤ 750 GC, 1500 WL) · Gaussian + SSC · SO φ, ℓ 2–3000 · BBN prior · τ fixed

mᵢ = multiplicative shear bias: measured shear = (1 + mᵢ) × true shear in source bin i. Always marginalised, like the galaxy bias; “mᵢ fixed” = a perfect shear calibration.

Fisher forecast, no prior on the mᵢ in 3×2pt: a calibration prior on the mᵢ would make the 6×2pt gain smaller.

Fixing all 13 mᵢ gives σ₈ ×0.30 and Σmν ×0.57; 6×2pt × SO gives ×0.34 and ×0.60, almost the same, with no external calibration. Ωm (×0.91) is limited by galaxy bias instead.

Contributor's notes

The CMBX project: Fisher forecasts for Euclid photometric 3x2pt (galaxy clustering, weak lensing and their cross) alone and combined with CMB lensing (6x2pt), for LCDM, w0waCDM, IG, nmcxp-Delta, K-mouflage and TPM, with Planck and SO lensing, pessimistic and optimistic cuts, Gaussian plus super-sample covariance. The figure: grey dashed = 3x2pt with every nuisance marginalised; orange = 3x2pt with the 13 multiplicative shear biases fixed at their fiducial (a perfect shear calibration); blue = 6x2pt with SO, everything marginalised. The blue contours sit almost on the orange ones. Numbers (sigma ratios to 3x2pt, BBN prior): m_i fixed: sigma8 0.30, Omega_m 0.71, sum m_nu 0.57, n_s 0.63. 6x2pt x SO: 0.34, 0.91, 0.60, 0.69. Without the BBN prior: 0.31/0.71/0.61/0.72 against 0.38/0.94/0.66/0.78, so the message does not depend on the prior. Shear biases: sigma(m_i) from 3x2pt is about 0.02 in every bin; with SO lensing it drops to 0.017 in bin 1 and 0.004 in bin 13 (ratio 0.67 to 0.22). The gain grows with redshift because the high-z source bins overlap most with the CMB lensing kernel. Caveats: Fisher matrices; no prior on m_i in the 3x2pt case (a real Euclid analysis will have one, which reduces the gain); tau fixed; Omega_m gains little because its 3x2pt error is set by the galaxy bias, which CMB lensing calibrates only in part. DR1 (6+6 bins, 2500 deg2, Gaussian, Planck lensing, ell 8-400): m_i fixed gives sigma8 0.25, Omega_m 0.76, sum m_nu 0.49, n_s 0.56; 6x2pt x Planck gives 0.24, 0.73, 0.47, 0.55, slightly better than a perfect calibration. sigma(m_i) goes from about 0.10 to 0.027 (bin 0) - 0.015 (bin 5); |corr(sigma8, m0)| from 0.93 to 0.01.
