TR1 v1.1 LensMC × SPT-3GACT DR6

What is plotted

Blinded (cmbx_dr1_a) posteriors on Euclid TR1 v1.1 LensMC × SPT-3G (the variant control switches to ACT DR6), shown at their blinded values: every run fits the same blinded data vector (an additive cosmology-shift blind on the spectra), so absolute $S_8$ and $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$ carry the blind's unknown shift while the relative positions of the probe combinations are meaningful. Contours are 68 % and 95 %. Built up as κγ + κδ (κ-only), then + γδ, then + γγ + δδ. Sampler: nautilus nested sampling on the production Cobaya model, reweighted to the production Limber integration. 44 sampled parameters: five cosmological ($\ln 10^{10}A_s$, $\omega_c$, $\omega_b$, $h$, $n_s$), $\log T_\mathrm{AGN}$, NLA $A_\mathrm{IA}$ and $\eta_\mathrm{IA}$, per-bin source and lens n(z) shift and stretch, per-bin $m$ and linear bias, on the priors of the priors slide (the 3×2pt proposal, with the n(z) stretch and fixed magnification slopes).

Numbers

Blinded $S_8$, mean ± σ. SPT-3G: κ-only 0.841 ± 0.056, κ+GL 0.767 ± 0.028, full 0.826 ± 0.012 (κ+GL 0.059 below the full mean). ACT DR6: 0.895 ± 0.069, 0.820 ± 0.035, 0.829 ± 0.013. The σ(S₈) numbers on the slide are coloured as their contours. Nested shifts in units of $\sigma_n = \sqrt{|\sigma_\mathrm{sub}^2 - \sigma_\mathrm{ref}^2|}$ are in the chains fiber.

Caveats

  • The full vector fails goodness of fit at its best fit on v1.1 ($\chi^2$ 748/605), driven by δδ: autos high, distant crosses low, the v1.1 lens n(z) pattern. On Cycle 2 with the DR1 n(z) the lens n(z) stop narrowing, the γδ band-1 excess vanishes, and a δδ excess remains in the distant crosses.
  • The full-vector runs use a repartitioned proposal; validated on κ+GL, $S_8$ is exact and $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$ comes out about 8 % narrow, so the full contours' $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$ upper side may be short.
  • Exploratory runs outside lc; production chains on Cycle 2 (tr1_v2) go through lc next.
