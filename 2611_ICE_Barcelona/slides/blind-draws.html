The draw of a blinding shift sets how far $S_8$ moves

median shift in $S_8$
  • $(\omega_c, A_s)$ posterior: $3.0\sigma$
  • flat box in $(A_s, \omega_c, h)$: $12\sigma$
  • box kept within $3\sigma$: $1.3\sigma$
    (8 % of the draws survive)
  • $(\omega_c, A_s)$ set by $(S_8, \Omega_\mathrm{m})$: $0.8\sigma$
  • Each dot — one draw of a Muir-style blinding shift, placed at the size of the $S_8$ shift it means, in units of the DR1 South 3×2pt forecast $\sigma(S_8)=0.0105$. The black bar is the median, the dashed line $3\sigma$.
  • $(\omega_c, A_s)$ from the posterior — the pair drawn from its two-dimensional marginal with $h$ and $n_s$ held. Casper Vedder showed on 22 November that this moves $S_8$ by about $7\sigma$ (on COSEBIs); here the median is $3.0\sigma$, 13 % of the draws pass $7\sigma$ and the largest of 400 is $14.8\sigma$. The marginal is as wide as it is because $h$ and $n_s$ are free; with them held, the draw runs far off the $S_8$ degeneracy.
  • Flat in $(A_s, \omega_c, h)$ — Martin Crocce's proposal of 24 November: a flat box $\pm3\sigma$ of the 3×2pt posterior wide, then a cut in $(S_8, \Omega_\mathrm{m})$. The raw box has a median of $12\sigma$; keeping draws within the $3\sigma$ ellipse of the 3×2pt leaves 311 of 4000, with a median of $1.3\sigma$ and none above $3\sigma$.
  • Set by $(S_8, \Omega_\mathrm{m})$ — the CMBX draw: the $(\omega_c, A_s)$ covariance is $J^{-1} C J^{-T}$, where $C$ is the 5×2pt posterior's $(S_8, \Omega_\mathrm{m})$ covariance and $J$ the derivative of $(S_8, \Omega_\mathrm{m})$ with respect to $(\omega_c, A_s)$ at fixed $h$ and $n_s$. Median $0.75\sigma$, 98 % within $3\sigma$.
Consequence for the draw

Crocce's recipe gives shifts of the wanted size once the $(S_8, \Omega_\mathrm{m})$ cut is applied; the cut does nearly all the work, since 92 % of the box is thrown away. The $J$ recipe needs no rejection and gives a size that is chosen in advance. The 30 draws on the next slide are the first 30 kept from the flat box.
