SPT-3G is tighter than ACT DR6 at small scales

  • TR1: SPT-3G tighter from $\ell \approx 200$, ×2.4 at 2700
  • DR1: from $\ell \approx 330$, ×1.8 at 2700
  • ACT gains more of DR1: 1069 deg², against 353

Euclid TR1 v1.1 LensMC; SPT-3G Main GMV; ACT DR6 baseline

The ratio of error bars, $\sigma_\mathrm{ACT}/\sigma_\mathrm{SPT}$, in the CMB-lensing cross-spectra, in the 15 NaMaster bandpowers from $\ell = 100$ to 3000. On TR1 it is measured: each σ is the square root of the diagonal of that survey's full NaMaster Gaussian covariance, the error bars of the data-vector slide. The twelve crosses (shear in six source bins × κ, galaxy density in six lens bins × κ) give the same ratio to within a few per cent, at most 10 % apart in one low-$\ell$ bandpower, because the CMB-lensing noise rather than the Euclid tracer sets it; the solid line is their mean. Only error bars enter, so the figure carries no measured amplitude.

The dashed DR1 line scales the TR1 ratio by the overlap areas, with σ ∝ (area)$^{-1/2}$ for each survey: ratio$_\mathrm{DR1}$ = ratio$_\mathrm{TR1}\times\sqrt{(A^\mathrm{TR1}_\mathrm{ACT}/A^\mathrm{DR1}_\mathrm{ACT})(A^\mathrm{DR1}_\mathrm{SPT}/A^\mathrm{TR1}_\mathrm{SPT})} = 0.751\times$ ratio$_\mathrm{TR1}$. ACT DR6 gains more of DR1 than SPT-3G Main does: its effective overlap grows from 222 to 1069 deg², against 130 to 353 deg² for SPT-3G Main (effective areas: the TR1 weak-lensing coverage the data vectors are measured on, and the DR1 VMPZ coverage of the footprint slide). The scaling assumes the same noise per mode and ignores mode coupling. It uses SPT-3G Main alone: the Summer fields add 814 deg² of DR1 overlap (Main and Summer together 1104 deg², more than ACT DR6), at higher lensing noise than Main, so they are not in this projection.

ACT DR6 is tighter at the largest scales, where its larger overlap gives more modes: by up to about 7 % below $\ell \approx 180$ on TR1, and by about 30 % below $\ell \approx 250$ on DR1, where the crossover moves from $\ell \approx 200$ to about 330. Above it SPT-3G is tighter, by about 1.5 (TR1) and 1.1–1.2 (DR1) from $\ell \approx 550$ to 1700, and 2.4 (TR1) and 1.8 (DR1) at $\ell \approx 2700$, where the depth and resolution of the Main field dominate. On TR1 the κ-only blinded posteriors of the results slide give $\sigma(S_8)$ 0.056 with SPT-3G and 0.069 with ACT DR6.
