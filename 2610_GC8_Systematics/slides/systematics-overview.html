Fills in when systematics.gaussian_null_family lands.

Each cell is a null test: one Euclid tracer (lens bin $\delta_i$, source bin $\gamma_i$, the rows) or the SPT-3G CMB lensing map $\kappa$, crossed with one VMPZ systematics template (the columns). The number is the $\chi^2$ probability to exceed over 15 bands, $100 \lt \ell \lt 3000$, against zero. The density rows (boxed) are the ones for galaxy clustering to validate. Values below $10^{-6}$ are written as such. These template crosses are blinding-independent. The Cycle toggle switches the data cycle; both cycles show LensMC shear and SPT-3G $\kappa$ (the MetaCal and ACT DR6 versions of Cycle 1 are in the KP2-3 deck).

Cycle 1 (TR1 v1.1)

Covariance: the full NaMaster Gaussian covariance of each cross for $\delta$ and $\gamma$; the diagonal only for $\kappa$. Shear is clean against every template (lowest PTE 0.07 in this variant; across all 72 shear tests of the four KP2-3 variants the lowest is 0.03, about what the smallest of 72 independent tests gives), and so is $\kappa$ (lowest 0.17).

Galaxy density is divided by the visibility weights. Each lens bin's $\delta$ is counts divided by the VMPZ v1.1 test visibility weight, $w = (1 + \mathrm{WEIGHT}) / \langle 1 + \mathrm{WEIGHT} \rangle$ (Odier, Alguero, Macías-Pérez; January 2026, flagged TEST, not yet validated), which is what the visibility maps are for: removing the depth, exposure and extinction modulations of the selection by construction. Same templates, masks, estimator and bands as the production crosses; the Gaussian covariance is recomputed. Without the weights $\delta$ shows two signatures: exposures and noise in lens bin 1 (PTE $3\times10^{-12}$ and $2\times10^{-9}$, one depth dependence of the faintest lens sample, mirror images because pixel noise falls as exposure$^{-1/2}$), and extinction across lens bins 2–6 ($10^{-4}$ to $10^{-11}$). With them, $\delta_1 \times$ exposures and noise go to 0.03 and 0.10, and extinction in bins 2, 3, 4 and 6 to 0.12–0.97. What remains is $\delta_5 \times$ extinction ($9\times10^{-8}$; $+3.5\sigma$ at $\ell \approx 1090$), and $\delta_2$, $\delta_3 \times$ exposures and noise at 0.01–0.03, driven by $\pm 3$–$4.5\sigma$ single bands at $\ell \approx 440$–550, the tiling harmonic, where a Gaussian $\sigma$ for a template cross is least reliable.

Two caveats on the weights. The visibility model is a SOM fit to five survey-property maps that the headers do not name; a template among them is removed partly by construction, so its clean cell checks that the correction was applied, and only templates outside the model are independent tests. And the WEIGHT convention is this pipeline's: the official randoms follow $1 + \mathrm{WEIGHT}$ with slope 0.52, not 1, so the producers need to confirm it. The correction leaves $\delta \times \kappa$ within $1\sigma$ of the unweighted measurement in every band (largest change $0.96\sigma$, bin 3), so it does not visibly remove signal; an injected-signal mock test would settle that.

The PTEs are not yet trustworthy as significances: with about a hundred template crosses per variant, correlated through shared tracers and templates, and an analytic covariance not yet checked against mocks, their distribution under the null is not known. The plan is to run the same null tests on the GLASS mocks and calibrate them, as for the UNIONS B-mode null tests. Read the dark cells as clear detections and the 0.01–0.1 cells as unknown.

Cycle 2 (tr1_v2)

What changes: the six templates are the DR1 R2 products (same six survey properties), the lens and source samples are the tr1_v2 selection, and the density maps are visibility-corrected at the source (the pipeline's VMPZ visibility footprint), so no after-the-fact weight is applied. The covariance is one joint NaMaster Gaussian covariance for all 78 template $\times$ tracer tests, conditional on the fixed templates (template–data Wick terms set to zero), and each cell's $\chi^2$ uses that test's own block, $\kappa$ included. Producer: workflow/scripts/systematics_cycle2.py (family), lc output results/tr1_v2/systematics/gaussian_null_family.tar, which also carries a Gaussian-draw calibration of the whole family. Until it lands the Cycle-2 panel is empty.

Is the extinction signal CIB? The extinction template is documented as Planck 2013 radiance-based $E(B-V)$, which carries cosmic infrared background; of the six templates it is the only one that correlates with the Planck GNILC CIB maps. The next two slides test the CIB reading. The template-cross estimator leaks the large-scale ($\ell \lt 100$) modes of steep-spectrum maps like dust into the $\ell \ge 100$ bands, so a template cross at a given $\ell$ is not a measurement at that $\ell$; the null tests stand as nulls (an uncorrelated template gives zero in every band whatever leaks). Scratch analyses: results/scratch/napoli/visibility_check, results/scratch/napoli/cib_extinction.
