TR1 × CMB lensing blinded 5×2pt data vector

  • Steps — one row of the data vector, the crosses with SPT-3G κ: shear × κ in six source bins, then panned to galaxy density × κ in six lens bins, then pulled back to all 90 spectra with that row at the base.
  • Black curve — the best fit of the full 5×2pt chain to these blinded data, through the bandpower windows: $\chi^2 = 577$ for 651 bands inside the scale cut and 38 parameters (SPT-3G; 576 with ACT DR6). The strip under each panel is $(\text{data} - \text{best fit})/\sigma_G$ over $\pm 3$, shaded at $\pm 1$, a triangle on its edge marking a band beyond it.
  • Pale points — bands outside the scale cut ($k_\mathrm{max} = 0.2\,h$/Mpc for δδ, 0.15 for γδ and δκ), which the fit is not asked to match; there the clustering spectra rise above the linear-bias curve.
  • Blinded — the data carry the blind's shift, so the fit's parameter values say nothing about cosmology; what the slide shows is that one model describes all five families at once.
  • 5×2pt — the κ auto-spectrum, the sixth family, is not in this vector.
  • Controls — a click on any panel opens it in detail (Escape closes); the variant control at the bottom right switches SPT-3G and ACT DR6, here and on every slide with both versions; MetaCal opens with ACT DR6.
What is plotted

Euclid TR1 v2 LensMC shear and galaxy density on the official tomographic bins, crossed with the SPT-3G Main winter CMB lensing map, bias- and profile-hardened GMV (PH-GMV, kGMVbhTTprf), or with the ACT DR6 baseline map. NaMaster bandpowers, $\ell C_\ell$ in 15 bands from $\ell = 100$ to 3000, blinded (cmbx_dr1_a). The data are the likelihood product the production chains fit (6 Nov). Error bars are $\sigma_G$, the square root of the diagonal of the NaMaster Gaussian covariance; the covariance the chains use (Gaussian plus super-sample) has σ 0.87–1.65× $\sigma_G$, median 1.05, widest in the lowest bands.

The overview holds 90 spectra and 1350 bandpowers as a lower triangle of panels. Rows and columns run over the twelve Euclid tracers, source bins $\gamma_i$ then lens bins $\delta_i$, and the CMB lensing row is the base, in the order panned along. Every panel shows $\ell C_\ell$; the panels of a row share one vertical scale (its shear columns and its density columns separately). The scale cut drawn is the production linear-bias cut, $k_\mathrm{max} = 0.2\,h$/Mpc for δδ and 0.15 $h$/Mpc for γδ and δκ: 651 bands kept. Outside the cut the points are pale and the curve is still drawn: the small-scale δδ autos and adjacent crosses sit above it by up to several σ per band, the nonlinear bias the cut exists to exclude. Inside the cut no pair departs from the fit.

The best fit

For each CMB map, the highest-posterior sample of the full 5×2pt nested-sampling chain (pure CLOE through the JAX adapter, 15 Nov), polished with a gradient optimizer on the same likelihood and priors. At the unpolished sample the recomputed $\chi^2$ equals the chain's stored likelihood (585.3 for SPT-3G, 584.2 for ACT DR6); polishing lowers it to 577.0 and 575.8.

Per family with SPT-3G, $\chi^2$ over bands: γγ 287.0/315, γδ 89.8/132, δδ 66.7/92, γκ 92.8/90, δκ 19.1/22. One amplitude per family against the curve: γγ 1.00 ± 0.03, γδ 0.99 ± 0.06, δδ 1.00 ± 0.03, γκ 1.00 ± 0.09, δκ 1.22 ± 0.21. With ACT DR6 the families agree within about 1 in $\chi^2$ except γκ, 98.5/90 with amplitude 1.18 ± 0.10.

Against a fixed Planck 2018 cosmology with placeholder biases and no intrinsic alignments, the curve this slide used to show, the same data give γγ 0.79 and SPT-3G γκ 0.81 with $\chi^2 = 726$. The fit absorbs that difference. Where it does so: the intrinsic-alignment amplitude sits on the lower edge of its prior, U(0.3, 1.3), and its redshift slope near its lower edge; the shear calibrations stay within 1σ of zero; two lens-bin redshift shifts move by about 1.3σ of their priors.

SPT-3G against ACT DR6 in γκ

With the shear amplitude set by γγ, the SPT-3G γκ agrees with the fit (1.00 ± 0.09) and the ACT DR6 γκ is 18 % high (1.18 ± 0.10, 1.8σ). The two crosses share most of their sky: of the 118 deg² where SPT-3G overlaps TR1 shear, 95 also lie under ACT DR6 (ACT ∩ TR1 is 201 deg²). The test that separates a κ-side cause from a fluctuation is γκ on the common 95 deg² with both maps.

MetaCal × ACT DR6

No MetaCal chain exists, so the MetaCal variant shows the LensMC × ACT DR6 best fit through the MetaCal product's own windows, labelled as such on the figure. Against it MetaCal shear runs high, γγ 1.12 and γκ 1.20, the coherent shear-amplitude excess of about 7 % per shear field seen before; neither method carries a multiplicative calibration here. The MetaCal product is the 29 Oct one and its windows and covariance are of a different date from the LensMC product's, so its $\chi^2$ against this curve (779.5/651) is not comparable with the LensMC ones.
