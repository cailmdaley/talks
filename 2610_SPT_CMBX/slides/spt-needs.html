Data products needed from SPT

  • Mock lensing reconstructions with their input κ
  • Lensing noise curves (RDN0, N1)
  • The response, for the cosmology and systematics modelling
  • Mean fields

These products would feed the mock-based covariance efforts.

  • Mock reconstructions — the first need: with their input κ they give the SPT block of the mock covariance and realistic noise for the GLASS mocks; a transfer is tracked in Euclid Redmine issue #35882.
  • Noise curves — N0 (and the RDN0 recipe for the data) and N1, for each estimator variant used.
  • Response — the released map is already normalized; the response models the normalization away from the fiducial cosmology and the calibration and beam uncertainties.
  • Mean fields — or confirmation of how the released maps and simulations are mean-field subtracted.
The response

The D1 lensing analysis (SPT-3G D1: CMB lensing reconstruction and cosmology, sec. 4, “Debiasing and normalization”) divides the released κ map by the Monte-Carlo-corrected response, about 20 % of which is mode loss from the filtering. So the map needs no further normalization for the crosses. The response is still needed to model how the normalization changes with cosmology away from the fiducial, and to propagate the temperature and polarization calibration and beam uncertainties, which the D1 likelihood treats through changes in the response.

Why the mocks come first

The Euclid side holds the SPT-3G Main winter lensing maps for each estimator and one simulated reconstruction per estimator. With the input κ and the matched reconstruction for each simulation, the SPT block of the mock-based covariance can be built on the constrained route of the covariance slides, and input minus reconstruction gives noise realizations that carry the real reconstruction's structure, for the GLASS validation mocks too. Until then the CMB-lensing noise in the mocks and the covariance is a stopgap: the observed κκ minus theory. A transfer of about 500 input + reconstruction pairs is tracked in Euclid Redmine issue #35882.
