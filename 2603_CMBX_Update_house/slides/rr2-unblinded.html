Unblinded RR2: we build the full pipeline while we can poke around

385 deg², ~30% of DR1, unblinded

$\kappa$ from ACT DR6 and the SPT-3G winter field

$\delta_g \kappa$ and $\gamma \kappa$ in 6 tomographic bins

Pseudo-$C_\ell$ with NaMaster, Gaussian covariance

Euclid RR2 is unblinded, so we are building a full pipeline on it while we can still poke around. It covers 385 square degrees, about 30% of the DR1 area. We cross-correlate it with CMB lensing convergence from ACT DR6 and from the SPT-3G winter field, using two tracers, galaxy density and cosmic shear, each in six tomographic bins. Bandpowers are pseudo-C_ℓ estimates from NaMaster, with a Gaussian covariance.
