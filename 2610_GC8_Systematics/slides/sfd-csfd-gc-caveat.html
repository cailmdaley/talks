Chiang (2023) builds CSFD's extragalactic template from 180 WISE density maps (CatWISE2020 and WISE×SuperCOSMOS, z ≈ 0–3), so its sky and redshifts overlap TR1's lens sample (0.2 < z < 1.0). Suppressing galaxy-correlated structure is the reconstruction's objective, and the paper itself treats stacks on its own training samples as a sanity check, not a validation (§4.2). Euclid objects were not named as inputs and no object-by-object overlap with WISE was tested, so a Euclid null is not guaranteed algebraically; it is a transfer check of Chiang's correction. The same holds for the strong negative CSFD − SFD correlation, which is the removed component and is negative in all 90 bin × band points. The CIB-route test (slide 5) and the κ cross (slide 6) rest on tracers that no galaxy survey built.
