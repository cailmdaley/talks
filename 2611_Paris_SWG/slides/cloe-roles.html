Row 1 to row 2 changes only sampler · row 2 to row 3 changes only likelihood
  • Cobaya — a framework for Bayesian inference in cosmology. It holds the model (which parameters are sampled, fixed or derived, and their priors), connects theory codes to likelihoods, and ships its own samplers: a Metropolis–Hastings MCMC, an interface to PolyChord, and a minimizer. So "running Cobaya" usually means both: Cobaya's model and Cobaya's MCMC.
  • eDR1like — a likelihood written for Cobaya's framework, with CCL as its theory code. dr1_cmbx's configurations run it with Cobaya's MCMC (row 1).
  • nautilus — a separate nested sampler. It needs only a function from parameters to log-likelihood, so it can sample Cobaya's model (row 2) or a JAX function (row 3). It proposes points in batches, which is what lets the JAX likelihood evaluate 1000 points per call.
  • What each change gains — on a GLASS mock, full vector (measured 17–18 November 2026). Row 1: Cobaya's MCMC ran 23.0 node-hours and stopped at its 12 h limit with $R-1 = 0.059$, short of its stopping criterion of 0.02; its posterior was by then within 0.08σ of row 2's. Row 2: 16.6 node-hours, finished. Row 3: 0.45 node-hours. The sampler change gains a modest factor (1.4 or more if MCMC is held to its criterion) and the evidence; the likelihood change gains 37.
What my chains used before

The first TR1 chains of this pipeline used Cobaya's MCMC; from 19 October 2026 they use nautilus on the same Cobaya model (row 2), and since 15 November nautilus on CLOE in JAX.
