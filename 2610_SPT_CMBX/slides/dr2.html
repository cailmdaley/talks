Euclid DR2 in the south follows DES, where SPT-3G is

  • DR2 south: 3570 deg², 3037 with DES
  • the survey plan now stays on DES in the south
  • internal DR2: June 2028

SPT-3G Main sits inside DES

Where this comes from

Hervé Aussel, “Toward DR2”, Euclid WL-SWG × OU-SHE meeting, Napoli, 16 October 2026: Euclid observed 7234 deg² for DR2; the southern area is 3570 deg², of which 3037 deg² have DES coverage, and the northern 3664 deg², of which 3177 deg² have delivered UNIONS photometry. DR2 data acquisition runs to June 2027; the internal DR2 release is planned for June 2028. Euclid needs ground-based photometry for its photometric redshifts, which in the south is DES, and the reference survey has been modified to stay as close as possible to DES in DR2.

For SPT × Euclid

DES was laid out to overlap the SPT-SZ survey, and SPT-3G Main lies inside the DES footprint, so a DR2 southern area that stays on DES keeps Euclid where SPT-3G is. The DR1 analysis is the pipeline DR2 would run. The DR2 × SPT-3G overlap area has not been computed yet; this slide claims only the direction.
