Keep CIB out of the GC visibility input

  1. The VMPZ / Planck extinction map contains CIB.
  2. Correcting clustering against it removes some real LSS.
  3. Proposal: use CSFD to construct the visibilities (a swap of input maps).
  4. Proposal: switch VMPZ extinction maps to CSFD for DR2.
  5. DES / DESI have studied this deeply; HI maps are alternatives. Input welcome.

Cail Daley · CEA Paris-Saclay

The template carries CIB-correlated large-scale structure, supported by density, shear E and CMB-lensing crosses and the 857-GHz CIB route. Any nonzero correction fitted against that extragalactic component removes some real structure; this is a mechanism, not a measured cosmological-parameter bias. The loss depends on the fitted correction and selection; these slides do not establish its size in the production science vector. A template swap cannot undo photometry or selection already corrected upstream.

The immediate proposal is to change the dust input to the offline GC visibility construction, then rerun controlled data-vector and mock-recovery checks. The delivered VMPZ InfoMaps are frozen for DR1; the separate proposal for DR2 is to replace the systematic extinction-map input with CSFD, subject to VMPZ and GC review. Source for the operational distinction: the 26 October discussion with Sam Farrens, recorded in gc8-systematics-update/meetings/2026-10-26-1532-sam-vmpz-csfd.

DES Y6 adopted CSFD after screening dust templates against ACT kappa and high-redshift shear (arXiv:2601.14484, section III A). DESI uses HI information and excludes a CIB-bearing SFD residual from the BGS/LRG imaging weights (arXiv:2411.12020, section 6). Those analyses are more developed than this TR1 diagnostic; input on alternative dust tracers and validation is welcome. Details and citations are in the literature backup and gc8-systematics-update/literature-cib-dust.md.

All quoted errors and PTEs use Gaussian NaMaster blocks, never Knox diagonals. These covariances are provisional and not mock-calibrated; GLASS null-PTE calibration follows the talk. Fixed fiducial normalizations omit denominator scatter, and shared-map cross-cycle covariance is unavailable. No cross-cycle difference significance is claimed.
