UNIONS covers the northern sky that Stage III lensing left out

  • little overlap with the other Stage III lensing surveys
  • different telescope, different systematics, independent sky
  • DESI and SPHEREx redshifts on the same sky
  • Globe — the Stage III weak-lensing footprints (DES, KiDS, HSC) and UNIONS: the orange outline is the final UNIONS footprint, the filled region UNIONS-3500, the first shear release; violet is Euclid DR1.
  • Overlap — small: HSC's HectoMap field (about 100 deg² at Dec +42.5° to +44.5°) lies inside UNIONS, and a few DR6 tiles fall inside DES (Stripe 82, CFHTLS-W1).
  • Why it matters — a cosmic-shear measurement with its own instrument and its own sky is an independent check on the Stage III results, and the northern sky is where DESI and SPHEREx spectroscopy overlap it.

What is drawn

DES Y6 outline; KiDS-1000 and HSC-SSP PDR3 Wide drawn as their published field limits, good to a degree or two; UNIONS-3500 shear footprint; the final footprint from the DR6 tiles (about 5,800 deg²). Euclid DR1 at nside 512, pixels at least half covered.
