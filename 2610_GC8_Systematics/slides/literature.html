Screening dust maps against large-scale structure

  • DES Y6 screened with ACT $\kappa$ and high-$z$ shear.
    Planck13 flagged 4/5; SFD 1/5. Final library uses CSFD.
  • DESI DR1 used HI for BGS/LRG dust weights;
    excluded CIB-bearing SFD $-$ stellar residuals.
  • Chiang & Ménard: IR dust maps carry LSS;
    Planck maps have stronger high-$z$ imprints.

arXiv:2601.14484 §III A · 2411.12020 §6 · 1808.03294 §4

These are published precedents, not numerical calibrations of the Euclid tests.

DES Y6 MagLim++ (Weaverdyck, Rodríguez-Monroy et al., arXiv:2601.14484, §III A, Fig. 7, Appendix A) tests five LSS maps, including ACT kappa and the two highest-redshift DES Y3 shear mass maps. Its threshold is based on pixel-level Spearman correlation with jackknife uncertainties, not our Gaussian spectrum statistic. Planck13 is flagged by four of five tracers, SFD by one. The final library includes EBV_CSFD. Appendix A warns that fitting differences of individually plausible templates can remove real LSS.

DESI DR1 sample definitions (Ross et al., arXiv:2411.12020, §§6.1–6.4) uses HI for baseline BGS/LRG dust weights and excludes SFD-minus-stellar residuals because they contain CIB. CSFD is also used for validation; this is not a claim that every DESI selection or weighting method switched to CSFD. QSO and ELG inputs differ.

Chiang & Ménard (2019), arXiv:1808.03294, §§4.1–4.4 and Fig. 7, detects extragalactic structure in IR dust maps, with stronger high-z imprints in Planck-based products. The redshift dependence is map-dependent; it is not universally rising for every CIB-contaminated map.
