Euclid GC-8 call

28 October 2026

A preliminary CMBX KP2-3 template-fidelity test using OU-VMPZ survey-property maps, TR1 density, LensMC/MetaCal shear and SPT-3G/ACT DR6 CMB lensing. TR1 is not a blinded release. Template crosses do not by themselves establish contamination of the science vector.

Cycle 1 is the default throughout. Cycle 1 uses TR1 v1.1 products and legacy windows; Cycle 2 uses the tr1_v2 selection and DR1 R2 products. Cycle 1 density visibility weighting is applied after map-making; Cycle 2 correction is built into the maps. The raw Cycle-2 comparison removes visibility corrections on the already-supported catalogue pixels, without restoring excluded pixels. This is a release comparison, not a controlled change of dust map.

Verified LensMC input changes: Cycle 1 is catTR1v1p1_WL_260623 (TR1 v1.1); Cycle 2 is catTR1v2_localbins_WL_261016, with locally reconstructed bins and inherited LensMC shears according to the release declaration. Both archived map recipes use the same South WL coverage file and weights-in-mask policy at NSIDE 2048, but the binned maps and weights differ: per-bin support is 319.28–319.68 square degrees in Cycle 1 versus 319.24–319.72 in Cycle 2; bin-6 total shear weight rises by 6.90 percent. These nearly equal areas do not imply identical source selections or shear weights. Sources: astra.yaml data_release/shear_method; data/releases/tr1_v2.yaml; results/{tr1,tr1_v2}/map_products/.wl_shear_maps_coverage.manifest.json; exploratory rho/shear_input_audit.json. The dominant template-window effect on the cross errors is tested separately in the cycle ladder; these input differences are not a causal attribution of the cycle change.

GLASS calibration of the null PTEs is planned after the talk; the Gaussian covariances are not yet trusted as a calibrated significance scale. The new shear-B, MetaCal, ACT-window, rho and window controls are exploratory talk-only measurements. They do not replace the production data vector.
