SPT-3G is tighter than ACT DR6 above $\ell \approx 200$

  • ×1.5 from $\ell \approx 500$, ×2.4 at $\ell \approx 2700$
  • on 149 deg² of TR1, against ACT's 345
  • κ-only $\sigma(S_8)$: 0.056 vs 0.069

Euclid TR1 v1.1 LensMC; SPT-3G Main GMV; ACT DR6 baseline

The ratio of error bars, $\sigma_\mathrm{ACT}/\sigma_\mathrm{SPT}$, for each of the twelve CMB-lensing cross-spectra in the TR1 5×2pt data vector: shear in six source bins × κ (blue) and galaxy density in six lens bins × κ (gold), in the 15 NaMaster bandpowers from $\ell = 100$ to 3000. Each σ is the square root of the diagonal of that survey's full NaMaster Gaussian covariance, the error bars of the data-vector slide. Only error bars enter, so the figure carries no measured amplitude.

ACT DR6 is tighter by up to about 10 % in the lowest three bandpowers ($\ell \lesssim 180$), where its larger overlap with TR1 gives it more modes. From $\ell \approx 200$ SPT-3G is tighter: by about 1.4 at $\ell \approx 350$, 1.5 from $\ell \approx 550$ to 1400, 2.0 at $\ell \approx 2150$ and 2.4 at $\ell \approx 2700$, where the depth and resolution of the Main field dominate. The twelve curves nearly coincide because the CMB-lensing noise, not the Euclid tracer, sets the ratio.

Overlaps are TR1 areas from the RR2 photometric footprint at NSIDE 512: 149 deg² with SPT-3G Main (121 deg² of it also inside ACT DR6) and 345 deg² with ACT DR6. The κ-only $\sigma(S_8)$ values are the blinded TR1 posteriors of the results slide (κγ + κδ alone, full nuisance model); the same comparison was made at the June summer meeting on the earlier binning, with a crossover near $\ell \approx 200$ as here.
