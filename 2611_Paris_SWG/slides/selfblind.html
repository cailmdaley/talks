CMBX today: one hidden cosmology shift

  • The blind — when the pipeline writes a data vector, each bandpower is shifted by the theory at a hidden cosmology minus the theory at the fiducial (after Muir et al. 2020, the method of blindspot). Shapes, signal-to-noise, the covariance and the null tests stay visible; the inferred cosmology does not.
  • The plot — blinded κ-cross bandpowers, as $\ell C_\ell$, in the six tomographic bins: galaxy clustering × κ above, cosmic shear × κ below, with the error bars of the covariance, which the blind does not touch. Dashed: the fiducial theory through each bandpower window, which is not shifted. The data are an earlier catalogue, as shown in June 2026.
  • The draw — the hidden cosmology is one entry of a public list of 100, uniform within ±0.1 in $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$ and ±0.075 in $S_8$ of the Planck 2018 fiducial; the index is sealed and only its hash is shown.
