Cosmic shear benefits from independent measurements

$S_8$ seems to converge as systematics improve; consensus matters

UNIONS-3500 non-tomographic release
  • I shape catalogue
  • II B-mode validation
  • III real space
  • IV harmonic space
  • Whisker — published Stage III cosmic-shear $S_8$, oldest at the top; the band is the CMB. The newer analyses (KiDS-Legacy, DES Y6) moved towards the CMB as systematics control improved.
  • The release — four companion papers from one blinded catalogue: I, the shape catalogue (Hervas-Peters et al.); II, B-mode validation (Daley et al.); III, real-space constraints (Goh et al.); IV, harmonic-space constraints (Guerrini et al.).
Numbers

KiDS-1000 $0.759^{+0.024}_{-0.021}$; DES Y3 $0.759^{+0.025}_{-0.023}$; HSC Y3 $0.769^{+0.031}_{-0.034}$; KiDS-Legacy $0.815^{+0.016}_{-0.021}$; DES Y6 $0.798^{+0.014}_{-0.015}$; CMB $0.836^{+0.012}_{-0.013}$.
