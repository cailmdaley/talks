  • Lightcone Research — an open ecosystem for reproducible, composable, verifiable science, based at CNRS AISSAI and BIDS / UC Berkeley; Cail is a core member.
  • Two parts — ASTRA specifies the analysis and validates the specification; the Lightcone stack (the lc command line, the Studio viewer and the agent skills) executes it with whichever agent you bring and exposes the artifacts each result came from. The next slide is the Studio, live.
