SFD correlates with every lens bin; CSFD does not

What is plotted

The correlation coefficient $r_\ell(\delta,S) = C_\ell^{\delta S}/\sqrt{C_\ell^{\delta\delta}C_\ell^{SS}}$ between the TR1 Cycle-2 (tr1_v2) galaxy density in each of six lens bins (δ1 to δ6, photometric redshift 0.20–1.00) and three dust-map templates: SFD (blue), CSFD (orange) and CSFD − SFD (green), the component Chiang (2023) removes. The denominators are the total observed autos, including shot and reconstruction noise, so $r$ is not an intrinsic signal-only correlation. Errors are the NaMaster Gaussian covariance on the observed signal plus noise, propagated through all three spectra. Bands: the production 15 logarithmic bins, ℓ = 100–3000; bands below ℓ = 100 were not used, since their Gaussian covariance is not validated on this footprint. This is an exploratory measurement made for this call, not a pipeline (lc) product. The six lens bins and the three maps are correlated, so none of the numbers below combine as independent tests.

The numbers (χ² over 15 bands against zero, Gaussian covariance)

  • SFD: χ² = 72.5, 31.0, 27.7, 40.1, 36.7, 52.3 in bins 1–6 (27.7–72.5 for 15 bands); coherent amplitude $\bar r$ = +0.03 to +0.06 (+0.056 ± 0.007 in bin 1).
  • CSFD: χ² = 13.0, 8.9, 6.7, 11.3, 6.1, 15.9 (6.1–15.9 for 15 bands), PTE 0.39–0.98. A coherent offset of +2.7σ remains in bin 1 ($\bar r$ = +0.018 ± 0.007) and +2.0σ in bin 6; the worst single CSFD point is δ6 at ℓ = 112 (−2.3σ; SFD is −2.2σ there).
  • CSFD − SFD is negative in all 90 bin × band points. Its $\bar r$ is −0.19 to −0.25, but the amplitude is scale dependent (about −0.5 at ℓ ≈ 200 to −0.15 at ℓ ≈ 2500), so a constant $r$ fits it poorly (χ²/14 of 90–202) and the band average is not a model.

Where SFD and CSFD differ

At ℓ ≲ 300 SFD and CSFD agree point by point: Galactic dust dominates there and the error bars are large. The separation is at ℓ ≳ 400.

Caveats

  • The CSFD template is built from WISE galaxies. See the next slide, and its Chiang (2023) quotation below. The galaxy null and the strong CSFD − SFD correlation are partly by construction; the independent evidence that the dust map carries LSS is the CIB route (slide 5) and the κ cross (slide 6).
  • No matched-mock calibration of the Gaussian PTEs yet.
  • Footprint: all measured pixels lie in CSFD's LSS-correction region; 93.9 % lie in its most reliable cosmology area (the remaining 6 % were kept for comparability with the production window; no sensitivity test).
  • The CSFD map's reconstruction uncertainty is not propagated: the Gaussian errors condition on the delivered map. In this footprint the difference map's pixel scatter is 2.9 times the published reconstruction uncertainty.
  • This slide uses the Cycle-2 GC maps (tr1_v2); the other result slides are Cycle 1 (TR1 v1.1).

What CSFD's LSS template is built from

Chiang (2023) §3: “For the LSS templates T_LSS,i(φ) used in the field-level CIB reconstruction, we build 180 sky maps of density contrasts from 604 million galaxies in Wide-field Infrared Survey Explorer (WISE) from the CatWISE2020 and WISE×SuperCOSMOS catalogs.” The templates are designed to span z ≈ 0–3; the 2.7 million SDSS and eBOSS spectroscopic objects (0 < z < 3, about 10,000 deg²) set the target statistics, not the sky phases. §4.2 calls the SDSS MAIN stacks “a sanity check”, as those galaxies are part of the reference sample. TR1's lens bins (0.2–1.0) lie inside the template redshift range and its sky inside the WISE coverage.
