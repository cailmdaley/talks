Euclid × SPT-3G

A CMBX update: papers, products and access

Cail Daley

SPT main call

22 October 2026

An update from the Euclid CMBX working group to SPT-3G, following the status report at the SPT summer collaboration meeting in June 2026. It covers the DR1 papers the working group will write and the one built on SPT-3G lensing, where the TR1 × SPT-3G data vectors stand, what we need from SPT to finish them, and Euclid external-collaborator access for the SPT members of the MoU. All data shown are blinded.
