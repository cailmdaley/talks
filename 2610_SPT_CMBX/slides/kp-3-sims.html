The red box: these simulations are not yet in the SPT × Euclid pipeline. The posteriors shown earlier use the Gaussian NaMaster covariance; the next slides are the plan for bringing SPT-3G into the simulated covariance.

The method paper: Vielzeuf, Haridasu, Calabrese, Cuozzo, Carbone, Baccigalupi, Legrand, Fabbian, "Euclid: N-body-calibrated log-normal covariances for Euclid 6×2pt cross-correlations with CMB lensing" (internal draft 17 Sep 2026, Euclid PUB 741775, in review). It targets DR3 with Simons Observatory-like lensing reconstruction: 2500 FLASK log-normal realisations calibrated on DEMNUni-Cov, plus 1000 Gaussian ones for comparison, with constrained ISW–RS. Non-Gaussian effects are modest for the κκ auto-covariance but significant for the full Euclid × CMB covariance, especially for crosses with κ at high ℓ. Davide's DR1 simulations follow that framework.

DR1-KP-CMBX-2-3: Simulations and Covariance

Luchina, Baccigalupi, Calabrese, Carbone, Haridasu, Vielzeuf

  • Following the draft from pre-launch Vielzeuf+: we begin by simulating logNormal, full-sky, mocks of Euclid 3×2 fields correlated with KCMB; marginal distribution calibrated through the DEMNUni-COV simulations. Also possible to constrain logNormal 3×2 fields, anchored to gaussian simulations of KCMB from CMB-collaborations. (3×2) have magnification bias and intrinsic alignment, together with Poisson sampling of galaxies. Concerning KCMB it is possible to either perform lensing reconstruction (if using the fully logNormal mocks) or use the reconstructed KCMB fields from CMB-collaborations (matching their sims). The final covariance is then obtained by computing spectra (with NaMaster) for each simulated sky, and for each field combination on the appropriate footprint (DR1 / DR1×CMB / CMB). Currently investigating “resampling” of mock skies to increase the sample size.
  • Current results: 400 realizations constrained on ACT gaussian KCMB simulations & 400 fully logNormal realizations with a Planck-like configuration for lensing reconstruction. Soon to be added: constraint realizations on Planck simulations.
  • Parameters: nside=4096, [4.1, 4.1, 4.2, 4.3, 4.4, 4.6] gal/arcmin2 , (23,42) μK noise levels in (T,P) and 7’ beam for Planck-like CMB. Output binned in 20 log-spaced ℓ bins in the range [10, 3000].
