The γκ amplitude moves with the cosmology

The γκ amplitude per source bin: a GLS fit of one amplitude to each $\gamma_i\kappa$ spectrum over all 15 bands, $100 \lt \ell \lt 3000,$ against the fiducial theory, with the full Gaussian covariance of the blinded TR1 × SPT-3G data vector. Unlike the lens ratio, nothing cancels the cosmology here: the amplitude scales as $S_8^{2.6},$ and the pale bars show the range a cosmology shift the size of the blind spans (×0.75 to ×1.40). The points sit inside that range, so at this stage they say nothing about $m$ or n(z) that the blind does not hide; that is why the main slides use the ratio $\gamma\delta/\kappa\delta,$ in which the cosmology divides out.

Source bin 1 is drawn open: on the ten GLASS mocks the same estimator returns $1.25 \pm 0.12$ there, 2σ high, for a reason we have not found (possibly the low-z shear excess or source clustering; not established). Bins 2 to 6 recover 0.96 to 1.02 on the mocks, with 70 % coverage. These are exploratory estimators (results/scratch/napoli/crosses), not an lc output.

The variant control switches the shear method and the CMB lensing map; the description above is for SPT-3G. With ACT DR6 the amplitudes are 0.30 ± 0.40, 0.46 ± 0.35, 1.16 ± 0.29, 1.04 ± 0.21, 1.05 ± 0.15 and 0.87 ± 0.10 in bins 1 to 6, all inside the blind range except bins 1 and 2, whose errors reach it. With MetaCal shear the amplitudes are 0.75 ± 0.35, 0.49 ± 0.32, 1.03 ± 0.26, 0.91 ± 0.20, 0.95 ± 0.14 and 0.92 ± 0.10 (SPT-3G) and 0.55 ± 0.44, 0.39 ± 0.40, 1.29 ± 0.33, 1.05 ± 0.24, 1.15 ± 0.18 and 0.92 ± 0.13 (ACT DR6). All variants are drawn on the same axes; they share galaxies or κ, so their differences are not calibrated significances.
