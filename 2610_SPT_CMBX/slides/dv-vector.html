TR1 × CMB lensing blinded 5×2pt data vector

Cycle 2. Data: Euclid TR1 v2 LensMC shear and galaxy density on the official tomographic bins (26 Oct catalogues), and the SPT-3G Main winter CMB lensing map, bias+profile-hardened GMV (kGMVbhTTprf); NaMaster bandpowers, 15 bands from $\ell = 100$ to 3000; full NaMaster Gaussian covariance; blinded (cmbx_dr1_a). Data and σ_G are the rerun5 likelihood input (27 Oct). The black curve is the Cycle-2 run's windowed reference (its datavector_vs_theory, 28 Oct): a fixed Planck 2018 cosmology with placeholder linear biases 1.13 to 1.71, no intrinsic alignments and no photo-z or shear-calibration nuisances. It is a reference, not a fit. The scale cut drawn is the production linear-bias cut, k_max = 0.2 h/Mpc for δδ and 0.15 h/Mpc for γδ and δκ (arXiv:2203.12439’s method): 651 bands kept. It is the cut the chains use. The error bars stay σ_G of the data-derived Gaussian covariance; the covariance the chains use (theory-fiducial Gaussian plus super-sample) has σ 0.88–1.64× this, median 1.05, widest in the lowest bands.

Against Cycle 1 the blinded data barely move. The shear errors are unchanged too: shear noise and σ_G agree with the Cycle-1 product to 0.94–1.01 in every shear auto, and both cycles pass the B-mode null. The clustering errors grow, following the data: the measured galaxy auto-spectra are 1.17× (first lens bin) to 1.35–1.6× (last) Cycle 1's, and the Gaussian covariance is built from the measured spectra. The ACT DR6 variant is on Cycle 2 as well, from the same rerun, with its own reference curve and the same cut.

From here on the plots are measured on data: the Euclid TR1 release crossed with the SPT-3G and ACT DR6 lensing maps. With CMB lensing the 3×2pt data vector gains a row, shear × κ and galaxy density × κ: 5×2pt (the κ auto-spectrum, the sixth, is not in this vector). These are blinded data, built in three steps along one row of the data vector: the row of cross-spectra with the SPT-3G Main-field CMB lensing map. First its source-bin half: Euclid TR1 shear in six source bins against $\kappa$, $\ell C_\ell^{\gamma\kappa}$ in 15 NaMaster bandpowers from $\ell = 100$ to 3000. Error bars are $\sigma_G$, the square root of the diagonal of the full NaMaster Gaussian covariance. The black curve is the fiducial theory pushed through the same bandpower windows as the data: a fixed reference, not a fit. The strip under each panel is $(\text{data} - \text{fiducial})/\sigma_G$ over $\pm 3$, shaded at $\pm 1$; a band beyond it is marked by a triangle on its edge.

The view then pans along the same row to its lens-bin half, the galaxy-density cross $\delta\kappa$, on one shared vertical scale.

Finally the view pulls back to the whole data vector: 90 spectra and 1350 bandpowers, as a lower triangle of panels. Rows and columns run over the twelve Euclid tracers, source bins $\gamma_i$ then lens bins $\delta_i$, and the CMB lensing row is the base, in the order just panned along. Every panel shows $\ell C_\ell$; the panels of a row share one vertical scale (its shear columns and its density columns separately), so shapes compare along a row. The overview carries no y-axis labels; the close-ups and the detail cards do. Fitting one amplitude per pair against the reference (full covariance, fit bands only) gives, for SPT-3G: δδ autos 0.76 to 1.21 (weighted 1.04 ± 0.03), adjacent δδ crosses 1.14 ± 0.06, the non-adjacent δδ crosses unconstrained (their reference signal is now tiny), γδ 0.90 ± 0.03, δκ 1.21 ± 0.15; shear sits uniformly low, γγ 0.67 to 0.87 per pair (weighted about 0.8) and γκ 0.81 ± 0.05. With ACT DR6, γκ is 0.99 ± 0.06 and δκ 1.23 ± 0.15. The two crosses use the TR1 shear inside each CMB footprint, which overlap only in part, so the γκ difference mixes sample variance with any κ normalization; the κ responses measured so far (ACT about 0.92 on TR1, SPT-3G about 0.95 from mask power alone) do not account for it. The test that separates them is γκ on the common SPT-3G ∩ ACT DR6 ∩ TR1 area with both maps. Each pair is flat in ℓ. These amplitudes are blinded data against a fixed reference with no intrinsic alignments and placeholder biases, so they carry the blind's unknown cosmology shift and the missing IA; they say nothing about cosmology. The Cycle-1 pattern, δδ autos high (1.0 to 1.7) and non-adjacent crosses low (0.4 to 0.6), which pointed at too much inter-bin n(z) tail overlap, is gone on the official Cycle-2 bins and n(z).
