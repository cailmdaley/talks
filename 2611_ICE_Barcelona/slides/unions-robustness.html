$S_8$ is robust to analysis choices but sensitive to the IA prior

  • most tests shift $S_8$ by less than $0.4\sigma$
  • no IA: $-0.7\sigma$
  • Robustness — scale cuts, non-linear model, covariance and PSF-leakage correction all move $S_8$ little.
  • The exception — intrinsic alignments: with one redshift bin $A_{\rm IA}$ follows the $S_8$ degeneracy; no IA moves $S_8$ by $-0.71\sigma,$ a flat prior by $+0.38\sigma.$ Tomography resolves it.
