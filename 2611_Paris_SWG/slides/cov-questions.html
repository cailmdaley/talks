Open choices for the κ blocks

  • ~400 realizations are too noisy to invert directly
  • Shrinkage to an analytic target: target lacks reconstruction
  • Jackknife: biased, even delete-two (DES)
  • Own sims with reconstruction: a very large set
  • JC-1: run synthetic chains jointly with the covariance group

Ask: reconstructions on sims for every CMB field, and how many by February?

  • Too noisy — the options are those listed at the CMBX telecon of 5 November 2026 (Davide Luchina's covariance update and the discussion after it).
  • Shrinkage — toward an analytic target; the analytic target does not know the reconstruction noise, which is the term the simulations are needed for.
  • Jackknife — biased even with delete-two, as found in DES.
  • Own simulations — with reconstruction run on each; needs a very large set.
  • Joint synthetic chains — the JC-1 voice at that telecon: the 6×2pt and covariance groups must run synthetic chains together; JC-1's flagship paper is asked to include primary CMB and possibly κκ without the crosses, so coordinating here helps.
A hybrid, as one option

Keep the analytic Gaussian + SSC covariance where it is validated (the 3×2pt blocks, against the GLASS ensemble) and use simulations only to calibrate the κ-reconstruction terms of the κ blocks, which need far fewer realizations than inverting the full matrix. This is a suggestion for discussion, not a group decision.
