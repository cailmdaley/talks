CMB lensing adds a row to the 3×2pt data vector

Data: Euclid TR1 LensMC shear and galaxy density, and the SPT-3G Main winter CMB lensing map; NaMaster bandpowers, 15 bands from $\ell = 100$ to 3000; full NaMaster Gaussian covariance; blinded (cmbx_dr1_a).

These are blinded data, built in three steps along one row of the data vector: the row of cross-spectra with the SPT-3G Main-field CMB lensing map. First its source-bin half: Euclid TR1 shear in six source bins against $\kappa$, $\ell C_\ell^{\gamma\kappa}$ in 15 NaMaster bandpowers from $\ell = 100$ to 3000. Error bars are $\sigma_G$, the square root of the diagonal of the full NaMaster Gaussian covariance. The black curve is the fiducial theory pushed through the same bandpower windows as the data: a fixed reference, not a fit. The strip under each panel is $(\text{data} - \text{fiducial})/\sigma_G$, with bands at $\pm 1$ and $\pm 2$. Every $\gamma\kappa$ band is inside the scale cut.

The view then pans along the same row to its lens-bin half, the galaxy-density cross $\delta\kappa$. The $\ell$ range outside the scale cut is shaded grey, and its bands are drawn grey but kept. The cut is the validated linear-bias cut: $\ell_{\max} = k_{\max} \chi$ at each lens bin's mean redshift, with $k_{\max} = 0.15\,h\,\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}$ for $\delta\kappa$ and $\gamma\delta$ and $0.2\,h\,\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}$ for $\delta\delta$, chosen so that perturbation-theory contamination shifts $S_8$–$\Omega_\mathrm{m}$ by less than $0.3\,\sigma_{2D}$. It keeps the lowest one to five bands of each $\delta\kappa$ spectrum, from $\ell \approx 110$ in lens bin 1 to $\ell \approx 280$ in lens bin 6. It is a choice about modelling, not a limit of the data.

Finally the view pulls back to the whole data vector: 90 spectra, 1350 bandpowers, 605 of them inside the scale cut, as a lower triangle of small panels. Rows and columns run over the twelve Euclid tracers, source bins $\gamma_i$ then lens bins $\delta_i$, and the CMB lensing row is the base, in the order just panned along. Each small panel has its own vertical scale, set by the fiducial and the bands inside the cut, so excluded bands far from the fiducial run off the panel. The galaxy-clustering amplitudes vary pair by pair: fitting one amplitude per pair against the windowed fiducial (full covariance), the δδ autos sit high (about 1.0 to 1.7), the crosses between non-adjacent lens bins sit low (about 0.4 to 0.6), and δκ sits near 1. Each pair is flat in ℓ; a constant offset only looks like an excess growing with ℓ because σ shrinks. The pattern points at less inter-bin n(z) tail overlap than the delivered n(z) predict, with intrinsic alignments contributing on the source side; magnification at the measured flux slopes does not explain it. Under investigation. In the deck, a click on any small panel opens it in detail; Escape closes it.

The toggles at the top right switch the shear method and the CMB lensing survey. Only the variants that have been measured and blinded are live; the others (MetaCal, and ACT DR6 until its TR1 likelihood product lands) are shown dashed and do nothing, so the audience sees where the analysis is going.
