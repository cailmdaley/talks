Beyond single agents

  • Single-agent horizon grew from minutes to hours on software and ML tasks
  • Navier–Stokes: ~10,000 concurrent agents for 88 h wall time
  • Hugging Face: ~700 attack participants across 108 h recovered campaign
  • Left axis — METR Time Horizons v1.1: the human-expert duration of software, ML and cybersecurity tasks that a single agent completes at 50% reliability (not agent wall-clock runtime). Doubling time from 2023 onward is 128.7 days (CI 104–158 d), excluding points above 16 hours where the suite saturates.
  • Upper-right axis — agent wall-clock time for two coordinated multi-agent demonstrations, explicitly separate from METR's single-agent human-time metric: Navier–Stokes (88 h with ~10,000 concurrent agents; 88 × 10,000 = 880,000 is a constant-concurrency illustration) and the Hugging Face intrusion (107.77 h recovered campaign, ~700 participants).
  • Bridge — individual horizons are already hours long; the frontier now also includes coordinated swarms.
