3×2pt with SPHEREx lenses expected in summer 2027

  • lenses: Legacy imaging × SPHEREx redshifts on 4,099 deg² of v2
  • first chain on GLASS mocks (stand-in lens bins) recovers $\Omega_{\rm m}$
  • next: real lens bins in the mocks, a blind, then data

  • Figure — redshift from 0 to 1.6, each $n(z)$ scaled to a peak of one. In rose, the six lens bins of the SPHEREx all-sky sample (LSx v0.9), redrawn from Calum Murray's plot. Behind them in blue, the source redshift distribution of the first UNIONS release (ShapePipe v1.4.6.3, one bin, no tomography): the published $n(z)$, blind B of the three that analysis carried, mean redshift 0.60. 47 % of the sources lie beyond $z = 0.55$, behind every lens bin.
  • Lens sample — Calum Murray's LSx v0.92, Legacy Surveys imaging with SPHEREx photometric redshifts: 8.53 million galaxies on the 4,099 deg² it shares with the v2 tomographic footprint. Each bin's clustering-redshift mean, measured against DESI DR1, is good to 0.005–0.011 and within 1.1σ of the photo-z expectation.
  • Status — a first 3×2pt chain (shear, galaxy–galaxy lensing, clustering) runs on GLASS mocks and recovers $\Omega_{\rm m}$ and the lens biases; $S_8$ comes out $1\sigma$ low, half from prior volume and half from a 2 % excess in the mock's $w(\theta)$. It used seven stand-in lens bins and no nuisance parameters; the mock with the LSx bins, the blind and the data chain come next, from code that is part-built. No data shear has been measured for it.
Numbers

Mean of 20 mock skies: $\Omega_{\rm m} = 0.3096 \pm 0.021$ against a truth of 0.3097, lens biases within 0.3σ, $S_8$ at −1σ, about half from prior volume and half from a 2 % excess in the mock's $w(\theta)$. Source-bin clustering means: 0.293, 0.428, 0.583, 0.924 (±0.021–0.026); the working $n(z)$ on the cosmic-shear slide, a different estimate, gives 0.29, 0.45, 0.61, 0.93.

Still missing

The Gaussian covariance is 5–20 % tight on the mocks, $n_s$ needs an external prior, and intrinsic alignments, shear calibration, redshift shifts, redshift-space distortions, magnification and nonlinear bias are not yet in the mock or the model.
