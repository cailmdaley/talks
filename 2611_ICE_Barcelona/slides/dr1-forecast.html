Assumptions

Euclid DR1 South from the Nowcast v2 kit: its 1151 deg² mask, per-bin number densities, shape noise and South n(z). Noiseless theory vectors, the cmbx likelihood (pure CLOE through the JAX adapter, nested sampling), priors of the 15 November production chains. Gaussian plus super-sample covariance from our own code, on coupled pseudo-$C_\ell$ in the inference group's 50 square-root-spaced bands, $10<\ell<2000$.

κ maps: SPT-3G Main (bias- and profile-hardened GMV, 2019–20), SPT-3G Summer (TT-only) and ACT DR6 baseline, noise from the delivered maps (SPT) or the published curve (ACT). The κ noise is a single realization at $L\lesssim100$ for SPT. No κ reconstruction systematics or foreground parameters are marginalised.

Scale cuts: shear spectra ($\gamma\gamma$, $\kappa\gamma$) to $\ell=2000$, the upper edge of the bands; galaxy spectra to $k_\max=0.2$ (GG) and $0.15$ (GL, Gκ) $h$/Mpc; $\kappa\kappa$ from $\ell=40$ to 763 (ACT) or 2000 (SPT); other κ legs above $\ell=40$. The SPT-3G D1 lensing likelihood itself has 17 bandpowers over $L = 30$ to 2999 (Omori & Wu et al. 2026), so the forecast, whose bands end at 2000, leaves out its last bin and most of the one before; ACT DR6's 763 is its own baseline range.

Different from the inference group's run

South only; our lens $k$ cuts; our covariance rather than Spaceborne; one mask for positions and shear rather than per-bin matrices; the 15 November CLOE through the JAX adapter. The Euclid-only 3×2pt is the cross-check, but I have no published widths from the group to set beside it.

κκ through candl, and its covariance with the crosses

ACT DR6's published $\kappa\kappa$ likelihood, run through candl as an independent term on a noiseless vector, gives $\sigma(\sigma_8)=0.0204$ (3×2pt + ACT crosses + candl $\kappa\kappa$) against 0.0187 for our joint vector; the published $\kappa\kappa$ covariance is wider than ours ($S/N$ 39 against 46). Dropping the covariance between $\kappa\kappa$ and everything else in our joint vector changes $\sigma(\sigma_8)$ by 4 % with ACT (0.0187 to 0.0195) and 2 % with SPT-3G (0.0145 to 0.0148). SPT's own D1 $\kappa\kappa$ candl likelihood is withdrawn by its authors for a known bug until spt_candl_data 3.1.0, so it is not used here.
