δκ and δδ measure galaxy bias without $\sigma_8$

  • in δδ / δκ, $\sigma_8$ cancels but $\Omega_{\rm m}$ doesn't: $b$ stays blinded
  • bins 3 and 6 match the fiducial within 1σ
  • more bins with ACT and a less conservative cut

Galaxy bias per lens bin from the ratio of two amplitudes, $b = (C^{\delta\delta}/T^{\delta\delta}) / (C^{\delta\kappa}/T^{\delta\kappa}),$ each fitted against a unit-bias template with the full Gaussian covariance of the blinded TR1 × SPT-3G data vector. On linear scales $\delta\delta \propto b^2\sigma_8^2$ and $\delta\kappa \propto b\,\sigma_8^2,$ so $\sigma_8$ cancels, without needing galaxy–galaxy lensing. $\Omega_{\rm m}$ does not cancel: the CMB-lensing kernel carries a factor $\Omega_{\rm m}$ that the clustering auto-spectrum does not, so $b \propto \Omega_{\rm m}^{-(0.5\text{–}0.8)},$ and a cosmology shift the size of the blind moves it by ×0.80 to ×1.41 at fixed true bias. So this bias is blinded along with the cosmology, and the black lines are the fiducial biases (the GLASS mock inputs), not a fit.

Scale cut: $\delta\delta$ at $\ell \lt k_{\rm max}\chi$ with $k_{\rm max} = 0.2\,h/$Mpc and $\delta\kappa$ at $0.15\,h/$Mpc, the split that a nonlinear-bias contamination test validates (a $0.22\,\sigma_{\rm 2D}$ shift in $(S_8, \Omega_{\rm m})$ against 0.52 with 0.2 everywhere). That leaves one to five $\delta\kappa$ bands per bin.

Only bins 3 and 6 (filled) are measurements: 1.09 and 1.89 against fiducial 1.32 and 1.71, within 1σ. The open points are not. Lens bin 1 is not measured at this cut: its $\delta\kappa$ keeps a single band at $\ell \approx 112,$ where the mock S/N is about 1, so its point at 0.49 has a deceptively small and heavy-tailed error bar (on the mocks, $b/b_{\rm true}$ in that bin ranges from −1.3 to 7.7 over ten seeds). Bin 2 ($\delta\kappa$ S/N 1.9) is unconstrained. Bins 4 and 5 come out high, 1.76 and 3.08 against 1.37 and 1.50. Part of that is the estimator: a ratio with a noisy denominator is biased high, and on the mocks it returns $b$ 15–21 % high in bins 4 and 5 at their S/N of 3–4. The rest tracks the $\delta\delta$ auto-spectra, which sit high against the fiducial by a constant factor in ℓ (one fitted amplitude per pair: autos 1.0 to 1.7), while crosses between non-adjacent lens bins sit low (0.4 to 0.6) and $\delta\kappa$ sits near 1. That auto-high, cross-low, $\delta\kappa$-normal pattern points at less inter-bin n(z) tail overlap than the delivered n(z) predict, not a higher bias (which would raise $\delta\kappa$ and the crosses too); magnification at the measured flux slopes does not explain it, and it is under investigation. Because this ratio estimator assumes the fiducial n(z), it reads an n(z) error as bias, which is also why bins 4 and 5 come out high. The mocks are lognormal with linear bias, so they validate the estimator, not the scale cut.

More bins will come from ACT (larger overlap, lower κ noise in the south) and from a less conservative cut once a nonlinear bias model is in the likelihood. These are exploratory estimators (results/scratch/napoli/crosses), not an lc output.
