Simulating surveys from catalogs to cosmology with Euclid, SPT, and UNIONS

Cail Daley

CosmoStat, CEA Paris-Saclay

ICE-CSIC seminar, Barcelona

November 2026

  • The talk — mocks that reproduce a survey's observational properties, and forward models built from the same techniques, in two analyses: a 6×2pt cross-correlation of Euclid and SPT-3G, and a new UNIONS shape catalog over more than 5,000 deg².
  • Blinded — every Euclid cosmological result in this talk is blinded, and so is the UNIONS v2 data vector placed against mocks.
