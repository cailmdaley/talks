What CMB lensing can do for weak lensing

  • Source n(z) × m: the lens ratio checks each bin's $(1+m)$ × n(z) efficiency without image simulations; TR1 is consistent with 1 to about 15 %
  • Lens n(z) and bias: δκ against δδ separates bias from $\sigma_8$; TR1's distant δδ crosses point at the lens n(z) tails
  • Intrinsic alignments: γκ weights IA differently from γγ; the blinded TR1 joint fit prefers $A_{\rm IA} \approx 0.4$–$0.6$
  • Foreground maps: the extinction template carries the CIB, structure in a “systematics” map

Four places where the CMB-lensing crosses touch the weak-lensing and OU-SHE work in this room, offered as openings for collaboration rather than results.

Source redshifts and shear calibration: the lens ratio $C^{\gamma_i\delta}/C^{\kappa\delta}$ measures each source bin's $(1+m_i)$ times its lensing efficiency against the CMB's, with galaxy bias and $\sigma_8$ cancelling. On TR1 the shared amplitude is consistent with 1 in every variant: 0.83 to 0.94, with errors of about 15 %, so 0.3 to 1.1σ low. The four variants share galaxies (and, per CMB map, the same κ), so sitting low together is one fluctuation counted several times, not four. Per bin the errors are 15 to 40 %, against the 0.3 to 1 % the Euclid requirements on $m$ and the bin mean redshifts allow (the lens-ratio slide); it is a consistency check now, independent of image simulations, and its precision grows with area and with the CMB map's depth.

Lens redshift distributions and bias: on linear scales δδ scales as $b^2\sigma_8^2$ and δκ as $b\,\sigma_8^2,$ so the pair measures bias without galaxy–galaxy lensing. On the blinded TR1 data vector, the galaxy-clustering crosses between distant lens bins sit well below the fiducial while δκ sits near it; a joint fit reads this as more overlap between the lens bins' n(z) tails than the delivered n(z) carry, not as bias or magnification.

Intrinsic alignments enter γκ through the source bins' own tidal alignment, weighted differently from cosmic shear. In the same joint fit, adding NLA intrinsic alignments improves the fit by $\Delta\chi^2 \approx 13$–20 with $A_{\rm IA} \approx 0.4$–0.6 (blinded data; about 1–2σ above the mock baseline).

Survey-property maps: the Planck E(B−V) extinction template correlates positively with every TR1 tracer, the opposite of dust obscuration. It reads as cosmic infrared background leaking into the far-infrared dust map, so the template carries large-scale structure and must not be used to deproject the galaxy maps.
