Resolved contamination stays under half a σ

Built in two steps. First one pair close up, $\gamma_6 \times \kappa$ against the extinction template: the card a click opens on the full triangle. Its top row is the template's measured cross with each of the pair's tracers, $\ell C_\ell^{\gamma_6 S}$ and $\ell C_\ell^{\kappa S}$, each with its χ² over the 15 bands against zero and PTE (full Gaussian covariance; κ diagonal only). Its bottom panel is what those two crosses imply for the pair if they were all leakage, $X_\ell^{ab} = C_\ell^{aS} C_\ell^{bS} / C_\ell^{SS}$, in units of $\sigma_{ab}$, the Gaussian error of $C_\ell^{ab}$ in the blinded data vector. Then the card shrinks into its cell and the view is the whole data vector, laid out cell for cell as on the data-vector slide.

Every panel spans $\pm 0.75\,\sigma_{ab}$, with $\pm 0.5\,\sigma_{ab}$ shaded; a band beyond the scale is a triangle on the edge. That range holds 99.6 % of all bands over every template and variant. The bars are $\sigma_X/\sigma_{ab}$, first-order error propagation that ignores the covariance between the two crosses and treats the template's auto-spectrum with a Gaussian error, which is least reliable at a tiling-periodic template's harmonics. Their size varies with ℓ because the template's own power does; a factor of ℓ would not even them out, since it cancels in the ratio.

Cells outlined in orange are those where at least one of the two template crosses behind the cell fails: its χ² PTE over the 15 bands is below $10^{-4}$ (full Gaussian covariance; κ diagonal). The PTE statistics are not right yet: there are about a hundred correlated template crosses per variant and the PTEs are not calibrated on mocks (the plan is to run the null tests on the GLASS mocks, as for the UNIONS B-mode null tests), so the threshold is a guide, not a significance. In every variant the flagged crosses are all galaxy density: extinction in lens bins 2, 4, 5 and 6, which is its CIB signal, and exposures and noise in lens bin 1, a depth effect. No shear or κ cross falls below the threshold. Inside a card, the failing cross carries its PTE in orange.

A PTE for the pair

Above its bottom panel each card carries one verdict for the pair: is $X^{ab}$ nonzero at all? $X^{ab}$ vanishes when either template cross does, so the null hypothesis is composite, "$C_\ell^{aS} = 0$ or $C_\ell^{bS} = 0$", and its likelihood-ratio test is the intersection-union test: the pair's PTE is the larger of the two crosses' PTEs, $\max(\mathrm{PTE}_a, \mathrm{PTE}_b)$. This test is exact at the boundary of the null and valid whatever the other cross is. Two tempting alternatives are not. A $\chi^2$ of $X/\sigma_X$ with first-order errors is not $\chi^2_{15}$ under the null: for two independent null crosses with pulls $p_a, p_b$ the band statistic $p_a p_b/\sqrt{p_a^2 + p_b^2}$ has variance $1/4$, not 1. And once rescaled to be correct when both crosses are null, it fails whenever one cross is a strong detection, even if the other is pure noise. For an auto-pair the verdict is the single cross's PTE. In every variant the pairs that fail ($< 10^{-4}$) are exactly the pairs of two flagged tracers: under extinction, every pair among lens bins 2, 4, 5 and 6, and under exposures and noise, δ1δ1 alone. No pair involving shear or κ fails. Every caveat of the cross PTEs carries over: κ uses the diagonal only, and the PTEs are not yet calibrated on mocks.

The dashed lines in exposures, noise and PSF cards mark where each template's own power peaks: the bands at ℓ ≈ 438, 551 and 1088, the same in all three. A fundamental between the first two bands (ℓ ≈ 400–620) is a pattern repeating every 360°/ℓ ≈ 0.6–0.9°, consistent with the ≈0.7° Euclid field of view stepping across the survey, and ℓ ≈ 1088 is its second harmonic. The bands are too coarse to pin the period more tightly. At these multipoles the template is far from a Gaussian field, so the Gaussian σ of its crosses is least trustworthy there.

The buttons at the top right pick the template; the variant control picks the shear method and CMB map; on the full triangle a click on a cell opens its card. Where X is resolved, nothing exceeds about 0.4 $\sigma_{ab}$: the largest are exposures in δ1δ1 ($0.35 \pm 0.15$) and extinction in δ5δ5 ($0.24 \pm 0.12$; δκ at most $0.18 \pm 0.10$). The nominal maxima above 1 (exposures δ6δ6 at $\ell \approx 349$, PSF δ4γ1 at $\ell \approx 691$, and with MetaCal PSF in source bin 2 up to about 2) sit at dips of the template's own power, where $\sigma_X$ is as large as X; they are the edge triangles. The lens-bin-1 depth signature costs δ1δ1 about a third of $\sigma_{ab}$ per band. All four shear × CMB variants are measured; the δ crosses are the same in every variant.
