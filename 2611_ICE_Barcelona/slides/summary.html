Summary

  1. Euclid × SPT-3G: the blinded 5×2pt analysis runs end to end on TR1 and one model fits all of it
  2. UNIONS: 190 million galaxies over 5000 deg² for cosmic shear and 3×2pt with SPHEREx in summer 2027
  3. Simulations test each pipeline and give agents a known answer to check their work against

Cail Daley · CosmoStat, CEA Paris-Saclay

  • Euclid × SPT-3G — the blinded TR1 analysis runs from catalogs to posteriors, and the chain's best fit describes the 651 bands inside the scale cut with $\chi^2 = 577$; at DR1 the forecast figure of merit in Ωm–S8 is 2.4 times the 3×2pt's with SPT-3G CMB lensing, nearly all of it from the CMB lensing auto-spectrum; the κ legs of SPT-3G and ACT DR6 differ by 0.07–0.08 in $S_8$ while the full vectors, dominated by the shared galaxy spectra, agree.
  • UNIONS — the UNIONS-3500 release (Papers I–IV), the v2 catalog and its mock checks, Klara Bertmann's tomographic cosmic shear and the 3×2pt with SPHEREx lenses led by Calum Murray and Cail Daley.
  • Simulations — mocks that reproduce a survey's footprint, noise, redshifts and systematics check each step of an analysis; made fast and faithful enough, they serve explicit (field-level) and implicit (simulation-based) inference.
  • Agents — agents check their own work against mocks whose answer is known, while scientists set the requirements. Public data and agent-assisted analysis may change how collaborations divide their work; this is a question, not a forecast.
