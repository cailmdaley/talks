A blinding shift is recovered when its reference is near the truth

  • The test — Martin Crocce's of 24 November: add 30 shifts to a noiseless Euclid DR1 South vector, run the full chain on each (38 parameters, nuisances sampled, as he asked on 20 November), and plot recovered minus injected $(\Omega_\mathrm{m}, S_8)$. The dashed circle is $0.3\sigma$, the pass level registered for the CMBX blind. 90 shifted chains, 30 per set of points.
  • First step — the truth sits at the blind's reference, as in the group's plan. All 60 chains (3×2pt, and 5×2pt with SPT-3G Main and Summer) are within $0.12\sigma$, and $\sigma(S_8)$ keeps its size within 9 %. This case passes by construction: $T(\mathrm{ref}) + T(\mathrm{shifted}) - T(\mathrm{ref})$ is exactly $T(\mathrm{shifted})$, so the chain is the forecast at another cosmology. It tests projection effects and numerics, not whether the added shift is the right one.
  • Second step — the truth sits off the reference and the blind does not know it: galaxy biases 10 % higher, $A_\mathrm{IA}$ 0.55 for 0.85, $\log T_\mathrm{AGN}$ 7.9 for 7.7, and $S_8$ 0.777 for 0.839. The 5×2pt misses by a scatter of $0.29\sigma$ in $S_8$ and in $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$, up to $0.6\sigma$; 10 of 30 stay inside the circle. The mean miss is zero ($-0.04$ and $+0.01\sigma$), so an ensemble average hides it.
  • Third step — the same 30 shifts, best fits instead of chains, on two noisy vectors, for five probe sets. With one fixed reference (red) the $S_8$ miss scatters by 0.24, 0.49, 0.25, 0.29 and $0.50\sigma$ for cosmic shear, 2×2pt, 3×2pt, 5×2pt and 6×2pt. With the reference set to the best fit of the vector being blinded (blue) it is 0.06, 0.05, 0.10, 0.04 and $0.15\sigma$, and at most $0.4\sigma$.
One shift for every probe

Crocce's plan applies the same shift to the 3×2pt, 2×2pt and 1×2pt. With a fixed reference off the truth, each probe moves by a slightly different amount, so one blind separates cosmic shear from 2×2pt: the false $S_8$ difference has a scatter of $0.4\sigma$ over the 30 shifts and reaches $0.8\sigma$. Referenced to the best fit it stays below $0.2\sigma$. A blinded internal-consistency test would see the first as a mild tension that is not in the data.

Where the miss comes from

The miss is the product of the shift and the offset of the reference, so it is linear in both: at half the offset the 3×2pt $S_8$ miss halves ($0.11\sigma$ against $0.24\sigma$). By source, for the 3×2pt: cosmology alone $0.19\sigma$, galaxy bias alone $0.08\sigma$ ($0.18\sigma$ in $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$), intrinsic alignments $0.02\sigma$, baryons $0.02\sigma$. The offsets chosen are large on purpose: $S_8$ is at the level where weak lensing measures it low.

The alternative of scaling the vector

Multiplying by $T(\mathrm{shifted})/T(\mathrm{ref})$ recovers to $0.3\sigma$ on a noiseless vector, but Muir et al. (2020, arXiv:1911.05929, footnote 1) warn that it rescales the noise as well as the signal, and it does: the best-fit $\chi^2$ changes by a factor 0.82 to 1.21 for cosmic shear and 0.84 to 1.18 for the 5×2pt, and for cosmic shear it follows the hidden $S_8$ shift with a correlation of 0.98. A blind that shows the shift in the goodness of fit is not usable. The added shift, with either reference, leaves $\chi^2$ unchanged within 1 %.

What the reference at the fit would need

The blinding pipeline would fit the unblinded vector and keep the result sealed with the shift. The full best fit takes about 3 minutes on one node with all 38 parameters, so it is cheap; the cost is that the pipeline then holds an unblinded fit. It has not been tested on mocks or applied to any blind. $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$ still moves by 0.1 to $0.3\sigma$ with noise, up to $0.65\sigma$ for the 2×2pt, where the best fit is a poorer summary than a posterior mean.

Best fits against chains

The recovery test at the posterior's maximum, all 30 shifts batched, takes 3 minutes where a chain takes 10 to 25 minutes for each shift. On the 30 off-reference 5×2pt chains the two agree to $0.05\sigma$ in $S_8$ and $0.06\sigma$ in $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$ (correlation 0.99).

Setup

The DR1 forecast's vector, covariance, priors and scale cuts (the DR1 forecast slide), with the 15 November CLOE through its JAX adapter, $n_s$ held in the shift. Widths on a shifted vector are not the unshifted widths: $\sigma(\Omega_\mathrm{m})$ changes by a factor 0.75 to 1.34 with where the shift lands. An earlier rehearsal on one GLASS mock, with two throwaway blinds and eight parameters, recovered its shifts within $0.75\sigma$ (the Napoli 2610 deck). The real TR1 blind was never opened.
