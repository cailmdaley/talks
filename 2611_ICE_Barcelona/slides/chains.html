The shared Euclid spectra carry most of that constraint, so the agreement says little about the CMB maps (on shared-galaxy mocks without κ reconstruction noise, the full-vector difference scatters by 0.001).
  • SPT-3G normalization — the lensing reconstruction's normalization on our footprint is checked on 250 SPT simulations to a fraction of a percent: the κ cross response of the baseline map on the TR1 windows is 0.9925 ± 0.0019, flat in $\ell$ to 3500. Applying it would raise the SPT-3G × Euclid amplitudes by 0.75 %, 0.075σ on shear × κ; it is not applied in these chains. The SPT-3G against ACT DR6 difference in shear × κ is twenty times larger, so it is not a normalization error of the kind simulations can see. The κ auto-spectrum's normalization was not part of this check.
  • Same galaxies — both runs use the same Euclid TR1 catalogs; only the CMB-lensing map, its footprint and its noise differ.
Numbers

Blinded $S_8$, mean ± σ. SPT-3G (bias- and profile-hardened GMV): κ crosses 0.822 ± 0.047, + GGL 0.770 ± 0.031, 5×2pt 0.782 ± 0.017. ACT DR6: 0.901 ± 0.053, 0.841 ± 0.034, 0.786 ± 0.016. $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$ in the full vector: 0.301 ± 0.040 and 0.296 ± 0.041.

Size of the κ split

On 20 GLASS mocks that share their galaxies between the two CMB footprints, the SPT−ACT difference in the κ + GGL $S_8$ scatters by 0.038, so the data's 0.071 would be about 1.9σ. Those mocks carry no CMB-lensing reconstruction noise, so the yardstick is too narrow and 1.9σ overstates the split; mocks conditioned on the experiments' own reconstruction simulations are the fix in progress.

Switching off temperature does not close the gap: the polarization-only and temperature-hardened SPT-3G reconstructions give κ + GGL $S_8$ of 0.774 ± 0.038 and 0.759 ± 0.037, against 0.770 ± 0.031 for the baseline, none moving toward ACT DR6. That rules out a temperature foreground as the whole explanation, not as a contribution; foreground mitigation is still being worked through. Open causes: the different TR1 sub-areas under each CMB footprint, the cross response near the ACT mask edge, and the absence of CMB-side nuisance parameters in the likelihood.

The model

Euclid TR1 Cycle 2 (official tomographic bins, DR1 n(z)), Gaussian + super-sample covariance, theory from CLOE with its emulators through a JAX adapter (checked against CLOE to below $10^{-3}\sigma$ per band and on the posteriors), nested sampling with nautilus. The priors on the nuisance parameters are placeholders with a cited source for each width; the scale cuts are an engineering profile until the Euclid 3×2pt group fixes its own.
