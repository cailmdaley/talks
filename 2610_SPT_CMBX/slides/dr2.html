Euclid DR2 will overlap well with SPT-3G

  • DR2 south: 3570 deg²
  • internal DR2: June 2028
  • Map — Hervé Aussel's DR2 input southern coverage in Mollweide projection: the Euclid Wide southern area in blue, DES in green, UNIONS in orange.
  • Why it follows DES — Euclid needs DES for its photometric redshifts in the south, and SPT-3G Main lies inside the DES footprint.
  • Not yet computed — the DR2 × SPT-3G overlap area; the slide claims only the direction.
Numbers

Euclid observed 7234 deg² for DR2, 3570 deg² of it in the south and 3664 deg² in the north. DR2 data acquisition runs to June 2027; the internal DR2 release is planned for June 2028 and the public one for June 2029.

For SPT × Euclid

The DR2 southern area grows Euclid's overlap with the SPT-3G fields well beyond DR1's, and the DR1 analysis is the pipeline DR2 would run.
