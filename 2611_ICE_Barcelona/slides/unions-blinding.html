Analysis choices were frozen before unblinding

  • blind in $n(z)$: three versions, one true
  • catalog version, scale cuts and robustness criteria frozen before collaboration-wide unblinding
  • Curves — the three redshift distributions A, B and C every analysis was run on; one is the measured $n(z)$, the other two shifted. B turned out to be the real one.
  • What was frozen — the catalog version, scale cuts and robustness criteria were fixed before collaboration-wide unblinding.
  • A leak — the authors of the cosmology papers were inadvertently unblinded about two weeks early: the maximum-likelihood $S_8$ was the same for all three blinds, because $A_{\rm IA}$ absorbed the $n(z)$ shift, which showed which blind was physical. No analysis choice changed after that; a $\Delta z$ fix after unblinding is documented in Papers III and IV.
