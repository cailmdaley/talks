Euclid–SPT MoU project: “Cross-correlations between galaxy density, shear and CMB lensing”

  • Joint project approved June 2025,
    part of the Euclid DR1 key project KP-CMBX-2/3
  • Scope: proposed as 3×2pt (γκ, δκ, κκ);
    6×2pt now looks feasible
  • Participants with data access: Cail Daley, Marco Gatti, Louis Legrand, Margherita Lembo
  • Other participants: Marina Migliaccio, Abhi Maniyar, Yuuki Omori, Aaron Ouellette, Srini Raghunathan, Kimmy Wu
  • The MoU — the Euclid–SPT memorandum of understanding, signed November 2024; under it, mm-wave analysis is led by SPT members and optical/IR analysis by Euclid Consortium members.
  • What it measures — Euclid galaxy density and shear × SPT-3G CMB lensing, plus the CMB-lensing auto-spectrum: the CMB-lensing block of the Euclid 6×2pt data vector, on the SPT-3G Main field.
  • Participants — the two lists of the 3 June 2025 proposal, verbatim: those requiring data access (Cail Daley, Euclid & SPT; Marco Gatti, Euclid & SPT; Louis Legrand, Euclid; Margherita Lembo, Euclid) and the initial list of other participants (Marina Migliaccio, Euclid; Abhi Maniyar, Yuuki Omori, Aaron Ouellette, Srini Raghunathan, Kimmy Wu, SPT). The SPT members are the ones the external-collaborator request is for.
  • Why CMB lensing — a high-redshift source plane with independent systematics, which helps calibrate the shear multiplicative bias and the galaxy bias and tightens the $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$–$\sigma_8$ constraint.
The project

The joint project “Cross-correlations between Galaxy Density, Shear, and CMB Lensing with Euclid & SPT-3G” was proposed on 3 June 2025 and approved as part of the Euclid DR1 key project DR1-KP-CMBX-3/2.

It builds on the first Euclid Q1 galaxy density × CMB lensing measurement (Fabbian et al., in Euclid collaboration review) and on DES Y3 shear × SPT-3G D1 CMB lensing (Ouellette, Omori et al., arXiv:2606.26223).
