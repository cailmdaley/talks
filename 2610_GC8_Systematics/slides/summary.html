Summary

  1. No template moves the data vector beyond $\sim 0.4\,\sigma$ (Cycle 1)
  2. The Planck $E(B-V)$ template carries LSS, through the CIB
  3. SFD correlates with every lens bin; CSFD's null is partly by construction
  4. Regressing $\delta$ on SFD removes $\approx 0.1$–$0.3\,\%$ of its power per bin
  5. VMPZ visibility draws on the $E(B-V)$ map: is it in the TR1 v2 ISD set, with what weight?
  6. Cycle 2 follows the 857 GHz CIB route; template nulls pending

Cail Daley · CEA Paris-Saclay

Six VMPZ templates crossed with TR1 density, shear and CMB lensing (Cycle 1, TR1 v1.1): shear and $\kappa$ are clean against every template; with the visibility weights, density fails only against extinction in lens bin 5. Where the implied contamination is resolved, no template moves any band of the data vector by more than about 0.4 of its Gaussian error $\sigma_{ab}$ (…/six-template-scorecard; the contamination's own error bar uses a Knox error for the template auto-spectrum, see slide 3).

The extinction template, documented as Planck $E(B-V)$, correlates positively with every density bin, the highest-redshift source bins and $\kappa$, rising with $\ell$ and with redshift, which dust obscuration cannot do. The CIB route reproduces the high-signal crosses with no free parameter, best with the 857 GHz CIB (…/extinction-template-carries-lss, …/lenz-cib-route).

Against the Cycle-2 lens bins, SFD correlates at $\bar\rho = +0.031$ to $+0.056$ ($\chi^2$ = 27.7–72.5 for 15 bands), and CSFD, which subtracts a WISE-galaxy-built LSS template, is consistent with zero (PTE 0.39–0.98) except a $+2.7\sigma$ offset in bin 1; since the template was built from galaxies, that null is partly by construction (slides 6–7, …/sfd-csfd-gc-cross).

The consequence for galaxy clustering. A weight, regression or deprojection built on a far-infrared dust-emission map (SFD, the Planck $E(B-V)$ map behind the VMPZ template) also removes the clustering that the map's CIB traces. Regressing $\delta$ on a map it correlates with at $\rho$ removes a fraction $\rho^2$ of $\delta$'s observed power: with the per-bin constant-$\rho$ fits to $\delta \times$ SFD ($\bar\rho$ = 0.056, 0.031, 0.031, 0.038, 0.039, 0.042, each ± 0.007; science/cmbx/gc8-systematics-update/sfd-csfd-gc.json), that is 0.31 % in bin 1 and 0.10–0.17 % in bins 2–6, about 0.1–0.3 % per bin. That is a fraction of the observed power, shot noise included, so the fraction of the clustering signal is larger. A CIB-corrected map (CSFD), an HI-based map, or stellar reddening avoids it. The visibility maps. The VMPZ visibility map is the density contrast "assumed to be related to systematic effects" (DPDD, VMPZ-ID HEALPix visibility card), and its processing element, VMPZ_Visibility, consumes the VMPZ info maps (DPDD, HEALPix info-map card). Those info maps include "Galactic Extinction / band … from Planck E(B-V) map". The decontamination runs as ISD (DES-Y3-like, official) and SOM (KiDS-like) regressions on the templates (GC-8 status, Napoli, 13 Oct). A visibility model that regresses on this template therefore also removes the CIB-carried clustering. The Cycle-1 CIB-route test shows the visibility weights removing more of that correlation than predicted in lens bin 6 (measured/predicted 0.22 ± 0.16, PTE 0.013 at 857 GHz), which fits this reading. With the Cycle-2 selection and visibility it does not persist: δ6 reads 0.93 ± 0.06 (PTE 0.48; slide 5, Cycle 2). So the over-removal is a Cycle-1 observation, and the selection, templates and windows all changed, which isolates no cause. Still open: whether extinction was in the TR1 v2 ISD template set, and with what weight.

Cycle 2. The independent CIB-route rerun on the tr1_v2 maps and DR1 R2 extinction is filled on slide 5: at 857 GHz all 12 diagonal-Gaussian PTEs are 0.30–0.99, and the Cycle-1 visibility-weighted $\delta_6$ deficit does not persist (measured/predicted 0.22 ± 0.16 → 0.93 ± 0.06). The lower-frequency routes underpredict; the worst point is $\delta_1$ at $\ell=2695.5$ and 545 GHz, $+4.44\sigma$ (see slide 5 for the trend and covariance caveats). The pipeline template null family, extinction-amplitude panel and contamination triangle remain pending on slides 2–4; no Cycle-2 extinction-null or depth-null verdict is asserted without those outputs. The SFD and CSFD crosses (slides 6–7) also use the Cycle-2 density maps.
