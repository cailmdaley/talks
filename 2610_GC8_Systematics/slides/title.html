Systematics-template cross-correlations

TR1 template nulls, and the CIB in dust maps

Cail Daley

Euclid GC-8 call

28 October 2026

An update for the galaxy-clustering group on validating the TR1 tracers with cross-correlations against the VMPZ systematics templates. The template crosses were made for the CMBX 3×2pt $\times$ CMB-lensing analysis (KP2-3), which needs to know whether any survey property contaminates its data vector; the galaxy-density rows are the ones of most interest here. Template $\times$ data crosses are nulls: their expectation is zero whatever the cosmology, so they are shown as measured, with no blinding.

Two data cycles

The result slides on the template nulls (slides 2–5) carry a Cycle toggle in the bottom-right corner; the choice holds across the deck. Cycle 1 is TR1 v1.1: the v1.1 selection, the VMPZ v1.1 templates, and density maps that are either coverage-only or divided by the VMPZ test visibility weights after the fact (each slide says which). Cycle 2 is tr1_v2: the DR1 R2 templates, the tr1_v2 selection, and density maps that are visibility-corrected at the source, so its $\delta$ rows compare with the Cycle-1 visibility-weighted ones, not the coverage-only ones. The Cycle-2 CIB-route test is filled on slide 5; the template null family, extinction amplitudes and contamination triangle still await their pipeline outputs, so slides 2–4 retain "Cycle 2 pending" panels on the same axes. Both cycles show TR1 LensMC shear and SPT-3G CMB lensing; the MetaCal and ACT DR6 versions of the Cycle-1 nulls are in the KP2-3 deck. Slides 6–7 (SFD and CSFD) are Cycle 2 only.

Earlier versions of these slides were shown at the OU-SHE × WLSWG meeting in Napoli (deck) and at the CMBX KP2-3 telecon of 22 October (deck).
