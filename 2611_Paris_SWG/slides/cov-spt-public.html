SPT-like mocks: inputs from public sources

  • A generic quadratic estimator, run by the covariance team
  • Masks: SPT-3G Main, Summer A and B
  • Noise levels per band, temperature and polarization
  • Noise maps: 500 per band in the public D1 release
  • Unconstrained, so any number can be made
  • What it is for — a generic quadratic-estimator κ reconstruction on simulated CMB with SPT-3G's footprint and noise, so that covariance mocks for the κ blocks can start before SPT's own reconstructions can be shared. Every number comes from a public paper or data release; nothing derives from internal SPT products.
  • Masks — RA/Dec boxes from the published field boundaries, HEALPix $N_\mathrm{side} = 2048$, RING, equatorial, binary and apodized. Overlap with the observed DR1 footprint: Main 423 deg², Summer A 948 deg², Summer B 137 deg²; Summer C has none and is left out.
  • Noise — white level, atmospheric knee and slope, and beam per band (95, 150, 220 GHz), with beam-deconvolved noise curves. Summer polarization noise and Summer beams are proxies, labelled as such in the table.
  • Public D1 release — the SPT-3G D1 Main-field release has the survey pixel masks and 500 sign-flip noise realizations per band; for Main-field mocks these are better than the package's boxes and noise model.
  • Any number — these mocks are not conditioned on an experiment's own simulated reconstructions, so their number is not limited by how many simulations SPT has.
How this differs from constrained realizations

Constrained realizations on SPT's own lensing reconstructions of its simulations carry the real estimator, filtering and masks, and there are as many as SPT has simulations. The public route needs none of SPT's products and is not limited in number.

Drawn from the package's own files by make_cov_figures.py in the deck directory, in a hand-drawn style. Left: the Main, Summer A and Summer B box masks (ochre) over the observed Euclid DR1 wide footprint (blue: the union of DR1_R1 and DR1_R2 MER VIS tiles, 2341 deg², not an analysis mask), in an equal-area view about the south celestial pole; outlines are lightly smoothed, and the numbers are each field's overlap in deg², recomputed from the same maps. Right: the beam-deconvolved noise model of the D1 Main field per band, temperature solid and polarization dashed.
