Euclid DR1 overlaps every CMB lensing survey

The blue regions are the two wide areas of Euclid DR1 that a cosmology analysis will use, seen around the south (left) and north (right) celestial poles. Both panels are Lambert azimuthal equal-area projections centred on their pole, at the same scale, so drawn areas compare directly across the slide. Both have north up and east left, as on the sky, and share one vertical great circle (RA 30° above the south pole, RA 210° below the north pole), so RA increases to the left in both. The north pole is the point where the spokes meet in the upper left of the north panel.

DR1-wide comes from the DR1 observed footprint: the union of the MER VIS tiles in the DR1_R1 and R2 releases, as listed in the archive in October 2025 (2341 deg² in all). That is where VIS mosaics exist, not a shear mask: there are no effective-coverage, star or bright-object cuts yet, and DR1 shear processing is not finished, so the analysis areas will be smaller. DR1-wide keeps, in each hemisphere, the largest connected region of that footprint and any piece within 1° of it: 1648 deg² in the south and 389 deg² in the north, 2037 deg² together. The 304 deg² left out are isolated fields: the patches near the equator, the fields around the north ecliptic pole, and small pieces in the far south.

The CMB lensing footprints arrive one at a time, and the legend gives each one's overlap with DR1-wide, labelled “∩ DR1” on the slide; the slide's “Euclid DR1” area is DR1-wide. Where a CMB mask boundary crosses DR1, its outline is drawn over the blue in that survey's colour. Outlines follow only the outer boundary of each mask: holes smaller than 10 deg², such as point-source cuts, are not outlined. Planck PR4 covers 1930 deg² of it, nearly all of both regions. ACT DR6 (baseline lensing mask) covers 1446 deg², all in the south. SPT-3G Main, the 1500 deg² winter field, covers 423 deg² on the southern side of the DR1 south region, partly inside ACT. The north region is seen by Planck alone.

Conventions: the drawn CMB footprints are mask pixels above 0.5 at the mask's native NSIDE 2048. The overlap with DR1-wide is binary, mask above zero and DR1-wide, with DR1 upgraded from NSIDE 1024 to 2048. The maps are drawn at NSIDE 512.
