The first half brings back what from the Napoli WLSWG × OU-SHE week bears on KP2-3: the DR1 calendar, a DR2 survey change that keeps the south on DES and the EXT-DES photometry problems found there, the TR1 Cycle 2 and Cycle 3 data, the 3×2pt discussion of scale cuts and priors, the first DR1 shear calibration and a test showing shift + stretch n(z) may not be enough, and CLOE's readiness for CMBX, with the emulator question. The second half repeats the TR1 results Cail showed in Napoli, marked preliminary until this group has discussed them, plus two pieces made since: the extinction template's CIB test, made during the week, and the first blinded TR1 posteriors. These results come from the pipeline Cail runs (dr1_notebooks, scratch/cdaley/, built on dr1_cmbx); they are not yet group products.
