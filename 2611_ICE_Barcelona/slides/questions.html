Open questions

  • How do we train and mentor young people while using AI?
  • How does collaborating change when labor is nearly free?
  • What are collaborations for, and which of their assumptions still hold?
  • Euclid runs through 2031. Can we imagine 2031?
  • Spoken — these are questions we need to answer together: experimental answers, worked out together, with open hearts.
  • Mentoring — the first and most important: how students still struggle, stay curious and learn the craft when an agent can do the task.
  • Collaborating — what changes between people when labor stops being the scarce thing, and how people who use AI a lot and people who use it little work on the same analysis.
  • Collaborations — the large ones already exist and run for years more on contracts and assumptions written for a world where labor was scarce.
  • Experimental answers — nobody has these worked out; the proposal is to try things together and see.
