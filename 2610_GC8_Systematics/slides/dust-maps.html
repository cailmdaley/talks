VMPZ is Planck13 reddening; CSFD passes the tracer nulls

  • VMPZ = $2.108\times$ Planck R1.20 $E(B-V)$
  • Identical values in both cycles
  • SFD: $\bar\rho\approx0.04$–$0.06$ in density
  • CSFD: all 13 spectrum nulls pass

This comparison exists only for Cycle 2; no Cycle-1 dust-map comparison is implied. The means and errors use full-range GLS compression, visibility-corrected density, LensMC E and SPT kappa, with the same fixed fiducial-signal normalization as slide 3.

The provenance audit identifies the source as Planck 2013 thermal-dust-model R1.20 EBV, converted to VIS extinction in magnitudes as $3.1\times0.68=2.108$. Every original source value is identical in the two cycles on the audited common pixels; coverage and window preparation changed, not the dust values. Source: private felt gc8-systematics-update/extinction-provenance/findings.md and stats.json.

All thirteen CSFD full-spectrum zero-null PTEs exceed 0.27. That does not make every GLS mean consistent with zero at one or two errors: for example density bin 1 has mean 0.0182 ± 0.0070, while its full-spectrum null passes. The headline refers to the spectrum nulls, not an assertion of exactly zero LSS. SFD density means span 0.0414–0.0575, apart from rounding.

CSFD's LSS template uses WISE galaxies, constrained by spectroscopic galaxy/quasar correlations. A galaxy null is partly by construction; it is not independent certification that all cosmological signal is preserved.
