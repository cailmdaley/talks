Summary

  1. The VMPZ / Planck extinction map contains CIB.
  2. Correcting clustering against it removes some real LSS.
  3. Proposal: use CSFD to construct the visibilities.
  4. Proposal: switch VMPZ extinction maps to CSFD for DR2.
  5. DES Y6 (Weaverdyck+ 2026) and DESI (Ross+ 2025, Zhou+ 2023) have tackled this; Sofia Chiarenza is testing it for DESI and has thought about it far more deeply than I have.

  • 1–2 — the template carries CIB-correlated LSS (slides 3–6); any correction fitted against that component removes real structure. The size of the loss in the science vector is not yet measured.
  • 3 — DR1: change the dust input to the offline GC visibility construction, then rerun data-vector and mock-recovery checks.
  • 4 — DR2: the VMPZ InfoMaps are frozen for DR1, so replacing them with CSFD is a DR2 request.
  • 5 — DES Y6 screened dust templates against ACT κ and high-z shear and adopted CSFD (arXiv:2601.14484); DESI DR1 uses HI for BGS/LRG dust weights and drops the CIB-bearing SFD residual (arXiv:2411.12020); Zhou et al. release LRG weights without E(B−V) because CIB in SFD removes real LSS (arXiv:2309.06443).
The mechanism, and its limits

Supported by density, shear E and CMB-lensing crosses and the 857 GHz CIB route. Any nonzero correction fitted against the extragalactic component removes some real structure; this is a mechanism, not a measured cosmological-parameter bias. The loss depends on the fitted correction and selection. A template swap cannot undo photometry or selection already corrected upstream.

DR1 vs DR2

The delivered VMPZ InfoMaps are frozen for DR1; GC corrects through the offline visibility map, so the DR1 remedy is keeping LSS out of the map it uses. A request to VMPZ needs a GC-8 justification and must land before the end-February 2027 freeze. Source: discussion with Sam Farrens, 26 October (gc8-systematics-update/meetings/2026-10-26-1532-sam-vmpz-csfd).

DES and DESI

DES Y6 adopted CSFD after screening dust templates against ACT κ and high-redshift shear (arXiv:2601.14484, section III A). DESI uses HI information and excludes a CIB-bearing SFD residual from the BGS/LRG imaging weights (arXiv:2411.12020, section 6). Those analyses are more developed than this TR1 diagnostic. Details: literature backup and gc8-systematics-update/literature-cib-dust.md.

Errors

All quoted errors and PTEs use Gaussian NaMaster blocks, never Knox diagonals. They are provisional and not mock-calibrated; GLASS null-PTE calibration follows the talk. Fixed fiducial normalizations omit denominator scatter, and shared-map cross-cycle covariance is unavailable, so no cross-cycle difference significance is claimed.
