Hands-on tutorial: dark energy from 580 supernovae

35–40 minutes · real data

  1. Specify & run flat $\Lambda$CDM in ASTRA + lc
  2. Verify quote against Suzuki et al. (2012) PDF
  3. Diagnose systematics and record a decision

Runs in seconds on any laptop; no HPC needed.

  • Dataset — Union2.1 compilation (580 Type Ia supernovae, $z = 0.015\text{–}1.414$, calibrated to $h = 0.7$), Suzuki et al. 2012 (arXiv:1105.3470).
  • Punchline — Step 1 recovers $\Omega_\Lambda = 0.722 \pm 0.013$ (statistical only); Step 2 catches that our error bar is 3× tighter than Suzuki et al.'s $0.705^{+0.040}_{-0.043}$; Step 3 switches to the full systematic covariance matrix, moving $\Omega_\Lambda$ to $0.714 \pm 0.030$ (within $0.2\sigma$ of the paper).
