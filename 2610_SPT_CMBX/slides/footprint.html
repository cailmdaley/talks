Euclid DR1 overlaps Planck, ACT and SPT-3G

The blue regions are the Euclid DR1 effective coverage: the DR1_R2 VMPZ weak-lensing effective-coverage map (August 2025), restricted to the 8389 tiles LE3-WL lists as valid for DR1 (of 8964 wide-survey tiles), seen around the south (left) and north (right) celestial poles. Each pixel holds the fraction of it that is effectively covered; the map needs VIS, NIR and ground-based bands together, so it is smaller than the area with VIS mosaics. The blue is drawn wherever the coverage is above zero. The map holds only wide-survey tiles, so all of it is shown: the two main regions plus the smaller fields around the north ecliptic pole and near the SPT field.

Every number on the slide is an effective area, the sum of coverage fraction times pixel area. DR1 covers 1589 deg², 1229 in the south and 360 in the north. The CMB lensing footprints arrive one at a time, and the legend gives each one's effective overlap with DR1: Planck PR4 1504 deg², nearly all of DR1 in both hemispheres; ACT DR6 (baseline lensing mask) 1069 deg², all in the south; SPT-3G Main, the 1500 deg² winter field, 353 deg² on the southern side of the DR1 south region, partly inside ACT; SPT-3G Summer 814 deg². The north is seen by Planck alone.

Both panels are Lambert azimuthal equal-area projections centred on their pole, at the same scale, so drawn areas compare directly. Both have north up and east left, as on the sky, and share one vertical great circle (RA 30° above the south pole, RA 210° below the north pole), so RA increases to the left in both. Where a CMB mask boundary crosses DR1, its outline is drawn over the blue in that survey's colour; outlines follow only each mask's outer boundary, and holes smaller than 10 deg², such as point-source cuts, are not outlined.

Conventions: the drawn CMB footprints are mask pixels above 0.5 at the mask's native NSIDE 2048. An overlap sums the DR1 coverage fraction over pixels where the CMB mask is above zero, with the NSIDE 1024 coverage map upgraded to 2048. The maps are drawn at NSIDE 512.

On all 8964 R2 tiles the effective areas would be 1647 deg² for Euclid and 1559, 1094 and 369 deg² for the overlaps with Planck, ACT and SPT-3G Main; the valid-tile restriction costs about 4 %.

SPT-3G Summer

The last frame adds the SPT-3G Summer fields (summer-a and summer-b, the apodised boundary mask, 1812 deg² above 0.5): 814 deg² of effective overlap with DR1, more than twice SPT-3G Main's. Main and Summer together overlap DR1 by 1104 deg², more than ACT DR6. The lensing map used in this analysis is from Main alone. Main and Summer share mostly an apodised edge near dec −42°.

TR1, the data analysed now

The dark region is Euclid TR1, the release the data vectors in this talk are measured on: its weak-lensing effective coverage, 286 deg², all inside DR1. Its effective overlap is 130 deg² with SPT-3G Main, 222 deg² with ACT DR6 (105 deg² with both) and 192 deg² with SPT-3G Summer; 281 of its 286 deg² lie inside Main or Summer, and all of the part ACT DR6 sees is also seen by SPT-3G.
