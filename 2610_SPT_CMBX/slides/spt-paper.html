Scope

The paper is the SPT entry in the KP3 list on the previous slide: the Euclid photometric probes (shear and galaxy density) crossed with SPT-3G CMB lensing, as a 6×2pt analysis. It shares the KP2-3 pipeline with the Planck + ACT 6×2pt paper (Lembo, Legrand, Fabbian), so the same data vectors exist for ACT DR6 and SPT-3G and their agreement is a test in itself. The SPT-3G map is the Main winter-field lensing reconstruction (GMV, profile-hardened).

Status

The Overleaf project exists on the Euclid ECEB template, linked from the KP2-3 wiki (Redmine #36759); the paper leads agreed at the 8 October KP2-3 call to set out each paper's structure and contributors next. Paper leads are coordinators, not necessarily first authors. Euclid DR1 products are final in March 2027 and public in June 2027; papers on DR1 data may be submitted before the public release, provided they match the data frozen in the internal archive.
