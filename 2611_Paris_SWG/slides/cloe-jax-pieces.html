CLOE with its slow pieces in JAX

  • Top — plain CLOE with the Theory WG emulator for $P(k)$: 8.0 core-s per full-vector evaluation, of which the Limber projection alone is 5.3 s (a nuisance-only step, $P(k)$ not recomputed). The 2.7 s for background and $P(k)$ is the difference. With CAMB for $P(k)$ an evaluation is 22.6 core-s. So emulating $P(k)$ gains only about 3×: the projection sets the cost.
  • Bottom — the same theory with three pieces ported to JAX: the background (closed-form $H(z)$, with CAMB's radiation and neutrino density tables computed once), the CosmoPower-JAX emulator call and the $P(k)$ table built from it, and the projection, which is CLOElib's own tracers and Limber integrals compiled under jit and vmap. The Gaussian $\chi^2$ is the same formula. The whole likelihood is one compiled function that nautilus calls on 1000 points at a time.
  • Right — time per point on one 112-core Leonardo DCGP node. Plain CLOE: 8.0 core-s ÷ 112 = 71 ms, assuming perfect parallel efficiency. JAX: 4 ms, measured in the blinded TR1 × SPT-3G full chain (247 000 points in 986 s of likelihood time); at most 0.45 core-s per point if it used every core.
  • Agreement — over the fiducial point and 24 prior draws, 90 pairs and 651 bands, the largest difference from plain CLOE is $3.8\times10^{-4}\sigma$ of the Gaussian bandpower error (gate 0.1σ).
Numbers

Theory gate. TR1 with ACT DR6 κ: worst band $3.8\times10^{-4}\sigma$ in a clustering auto-spectrum at ℓ ≈ 349; the SPT-3G configuration has the same worst band, and its κ × shear bands stay ≤ $2\times10^{-4}\sigma$.

Posterior check. Re-scoring the points of the blinded TR1 chain with plain CLOE moves no parameter mean by more than $1.3\times10^{-4}\sigma$ with ACT DR6 κ (8437 points) and $8.2\times10^{-5}\sigma$ with SPT-3G κ (8307 points). Widths agree to $3\times10^{-5}$ and the effective sample size is unchanged (ratio 0.9999–1.0002).

Plain CLOE timing. Medians over 5 prior draws on one core of a DCGP node (Sapphire Rapids), the first of 6 excluded as warm-up.

Where the port differs from CLOE

Numerically, in the interpolation of $P$ onto the Limber wavenumbers, the quadrature of the comoving distance and $z_* = 1089$ in the CMB-lensing kernel; and the emulator is clamped to its training box, where CLOE raises. Together these stay below the agreement quoted above.

Local patches to CLOElib

Three: power-law extrapolation of $P(k)$ beyond the $k$ table, radiation in the background, and the continuation above $z = 5$.

On the GLASS mocks

The mocks use dark-matter-only HMCode-2020, which the emulators do not provide, so there CLOE in JAX uses a correction table fixed at the reference cosmology and agrees with plain CLOE + CAMB to 0.038σ per band at prior draws. The posterior check (re-scoring a mock chain with plain CLOE) has not been run on a mock.
