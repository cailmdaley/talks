Shear calibration (SHECAL, Nicolas Martinet, day 2 afternoon)

  • Deck p. 10: "LensMC: m = −0.046 +/− 0.004; MetaCal: m = −0.018 +/− 0.007". Preliminary, 17 MER tiles (step 1b), "with EXT-DES bypass"; TR1 v2 quality cuts except VMPZ (pp. 11–12).
  • From the talk (fresh that morning, includes selection): Martinet's provisional reading is ~2 % selection bias, MetaCal measurement bias ~0, and a ~2 % LensMC model bias. Not yet verified. DR1 requirement in SIM: σm ~ 1.5 × 10⁻³ (deck).
  • The likelihood takes a single m (one σ) and c₁, c₂ per tomographic bin. Colour gradients add Δm ~ 2 × 10⁻³ per bin (Csizi+26). The deep-sim factor-2 gap may be folded in as a ~3 × 10⁻³ m uncertainty.
  • The southern calibration is blocked on reduced EXT-DES data, with no bypass possible (backup slide "EXT photometry in the south").
  • Martin Kilbinger's reading at the CosmoStat group meeting (20 Oct): the requirement is on the uncertainty of m, not its value, and the sims use one PSF for both methods with no PSF error, so the quoted errors are a lower limit.
  • Day 3, late session: the 3×2pt prior table has $m \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 0.01)$, called "quite pessimistic"; ~0.007 was already being reported.

Redshift parametrisation (William d'Assignies, day 4; from the deck, not transcribed)

  • Compared shift-and-stretch (n(z) moments 1–2) with DES Y6-style n(z) modes. For cosmic shear the first mode is essentially a mean-z shift.
  • Forecasts with Dumerchat's calibration sims: in a KiDS-like case (sub-1 % systematic error) shifts suffice. In the "DES-like (Euclid DR1)" case, systematic error of order 5 %, "correcting the estimated n(z) by the difference in moments is not enough to recover the true cosmology, even with broad priors on shift and stretch" (p. 14). In the figure, $S_8$ moves from about 0.83 to about 0.89.
  • Next steps on the deck (p. 15): first check the realism of the simulation (origin of the tails problem); if confirmed, either reweight the wide sample, or go beyond moment-1 and 2 corrections, where "with the current pipeline there is no straightforward solution such as 'let's use DESY6 modes method'".
  • Day 3, late session: the 3×2pt baseline is shift only, no stretch (contested); one participant said LSST preparation work showed shift-only is "very, very optimistic" and that one needs to go beyond shift + width.

Why it matters to KP2-3

κ × γ is linear in $(1+m)$, which the lens ratio checks (later slide), and it sets how the κ crosses' m prior is centred. κ × δ depends on the lens n(z) width, so the stretch is not optional there; the d'Assignies result says the stretch may not be enough at DR1 n(z) errors. The TR1 chains in this talk use shift + stretch per bin.
