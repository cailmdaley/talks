Implied contamination stays under half a $\sigma$Cycle 2 contamination triangle: inputs pending

Built in two steps. First one pair close up, $\gamma_6 \times \kappa$ against the extinction template: the card a click opens on the full triangle. Its top row is the template's measured cross with each of the pair's tracers, $C_\ell^{\gamma_6 S}$ and $C_\ell^{\kappa S}$, each scaled by the integer power $\ell^n$ that makes its error bars most nearly equal across the bands ($\ell^2$ for most crosses, $\ell$ for $\kappa$ and for some source bins, as each axis label says), each with its $\chi^2$ over the 15 bands against zero and PTE (full Gaussian covariance; $\kappa$ diagonal only). Its bottom panel is what those two crosses imply for the pair if they were all leakage, $X_\ell^{ab} = C_\ell^{aS} C_\ell^{bS} / C_\ell^{SS}$, in units of $\sigma_{ab}$, the Gaussian error of $C_\ell^{ab}$ in the blinded data vector. Then the card shrinks into its cell and the view is the whole data vector, one cell per pair of tracers.

Every panel spans $\pm 0.75\,\sigma_{ab}$, with $\pm 0.5\,\sigma_{ab}$ shaded; a band beyond the scale is a triangle on the edge. That range holds 99.6 % of all bands over every template and variant. The bars are $\sigma_X/\sigma_{ab}$, first-order error propagation that ignores the covariance between the two crosses and gives the template's auto-spectrum $C_\ell^{SS}$ a Knox error (it treats a fixed map as a Gaussian field), which is least reliable at a tiling-periodic template's harmonics. So the contamination is in units of the data's Gaussian $\sigma_{ab}$, but its own error bar is not purely Gaussian. Their size varies with $\ell$ because the template's own power does; no power of $\ell$ evens them out, since it cancels in the ratio.

The lens-bin crosses use $\delta$ divided by the VMPZ test visibility weights, as on the previous slide. Cells outlined in orange are those where both template crosses behind the cell fail, so the pair shares a detectable correlation with the template: each cross's $\chi^2$ PTE over the 15 bands is below $10^{-4}$ (full Gaussian covariance; $\kappa$ diagonal). The PTEs are not calibrated on mocks (about a hundred correlated template crosses per variant), so the threshold is a guide, not a significance. The one flagged cross is $\delta_5 \times$ extinction, so the one outlined cell is $\delta_5\delta_5$ under extinction. Without the visibility weights, extinction also flagged lens bins 2, 4 and 6, and exposures and noise lens bin 1. Inside a card, the failing cross carries its PTE in orange. A shared correlation with the template needs both crosses to be nonzero, so the pair's PTE is the larger of the two crosses' PTEs on its card.

The buttons above the contamination panel pick the template; on the full triangle a click on a cell opens its card. Where $X$ is resolved (above twice its error), nothing exceeds about $0.4\,\sigma_{ab}$: the largest are noise and exposures in $\delta_2\gamma_3$ at $\ell \approx 440$ (MetaCal, $0.37 \pm 0.16$) and in $\delta_3\delta_3$ at $\ell \approx 550$ ($0.30 \pm 0.14$), both at the tiling harmonic. The nominal maxima above 1 (PSF at $\ell \approx 690$ in pairs with $\delta_1$, and with MetaCal in source bin 2 up to about 2) sit at a dip of the template's own power, where $\sigma_X$ is as large as $X$; they are the edge triangles. The figure here is LensMC $\times$ SPT-3G; the KP2-3 deck has all four shear $\times$ CMB variants, from which the MetaCal numbers above come.
