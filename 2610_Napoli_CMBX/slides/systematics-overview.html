Shear passes every template; galaxy density does not

Each cell is a null test: one Euclid tracer (lens bin δ, source bin γ) or the SPT-3G CMB lensing map κ, crossed with one VMPZ systematics template. The number is the χ² probability over 15 bands, $100 \lt \ell \lt 3000$, expressed as a Gaussian σ (0 when the PTE is above one half), with the full Gaussian covariance; κ uses the diagonal only. These template crosses are blinding-independent.

Shear passes against every template. Galaxy density has two signatures. Extinction fails across the lens bins (χ² 70–80 over 15 in lens bins 4–6), but its sign is positive in every tracer, the opposite of dust obscuration, and it reaches high-z shear and κ too: the Planck E(B−V) map behind the template carries cosmic infrared background from the same large-scale structure. It is structure in a foreground map, not contamination of ours. Exposures and noise fail in lens bin 1 (χ² 87 and 72 over 15), mirror images of each other because pixel noise falls as exposure$^{-1/2}$: one depth dependence of the faintest lens sample, counted once. PSF, zodiacal light and stars are clean.

Detected in a cross does not mean large in the data vector: the next slide puts each template's implied contamination in units of the data's own error bars.
