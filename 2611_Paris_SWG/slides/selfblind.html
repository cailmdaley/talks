CMBX today: one hidden cosmology shift

  • The blind — when the pipeline writes a data vector, each bandpower is shifted by the theory at a hidden cosmology minus the theory at the fiducial (after Muir et al. 2020, the method of blindspot). Shapes, the covariance and the null tests stay visible; the inferred cosmology does not.
  • The plot — that shift for one throwaway blind on a GLASS mock (seed 3000), revealed for the purpose: $\Delta S_8 = -0.029$, $\Delta\Omega_\mathrm{m} = -0.089$. Each line is one spectrum, coloured by tomographic bin; the vertical axis is blinded minus true in units of the Gaussian error of that bandpower. Theory: the likelihood's own theory vector of dr1_cmbx, with the HEALPix pixel windows and the ACT κ filter.
  • Reading — one cosmology shift is a very different number of σ per probe: at most 0.3σ per bandpower in κγ and 0.7σ in κδ, but up to 22σ in δδ. The table gives the median and largest shift per family and the joint significance on that family's covariance block (κγ 1.0, κδ 2.2, γγ 7.7, δγ 18, δδ 91).
