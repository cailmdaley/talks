Cail Daley · CEA Paris-Saclay · for the Euclid CMBX SWG

The talk in five lines. The CMBX working group's DR1 key projects cross Euclid with the secondary anisotropies of the CMB: lensing (KP1-2, KP2-3), the thermal Sunyaev–Zel'dovich effect (KP4), the kinetic one through peculiar velocities (KP5), and further probes including the cosmic infrared background. The KP2-3 pipeline goes from catalogues to a blinded likelihood and is validated on GLASS 6×2pt mocks stage by stage. The lens ratio $C^{\gamma\delta}/C^{\kappa\delta}$, with intrinsic alignments in the templates, checks shear calibration times source lensing efficiency at about 15 % and is consistent with 1 in all four data variants (0.85 to 0.95). On TR1 data, no shear or CMB-lensing cross with any of six survey-property templates stands out, while galaxy density correlates with extinction (most likely the CIB in the dust map) and with exposure and noise in the first lens bin (a depth effect). Next come chains on TR1, the numerical covariance from the FLASK simulations, and the move to DR1, starting with galaxy clustering × CMB lensing.
