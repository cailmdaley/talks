Same chain, two exact likelihoods

  • TR1 5×2pt: 90 pairs, 651 bands, no κκ
  • Identical data, Gaussian + SSC covariance, priors, cuts
  • eDR1like + CCL against CLOElib 0.11 + CLOElike 0.3
  • GLASS mock (seed 3004) and blinded TR1 × SPT-3G
  • JAX explorer, then reweighted to each exact likelihood

Not a native CLOE sampler run: CLOE scores every posterior point

  • Vector — shear × shear, shear × positions, positions × positions, κ × shear and κ × positions after scale cuts. κκ is excluded because CLOElike has no CMB-lensing likelihood yet.
  • Inputs — covariance, priors and cuts are the same files, checked by SHA256: the production Gaussian + SSC covariance, the DR1-document nuisance priors, 37 sampled parameters on the mock and 38 on data ($T_\mathrm{AGN}$ also sampled).
  • dr1_cmbx — eDR1like with CCL theory and CAMB HMCode-2020 $P(k)$.
  • CLOE — CLOElib v0.11 theory (CAMB background and HMCode-2020 $P(k)$) scored by the CLOElike v0.3 Gaussian likelihood. One change: CLOElib extrapolates $\log P(k)$ beyond its $k$ table with a cubic, which is not grid-convergent in the top shear × shear band; a power law is used instead (worst band 0.34σ → 0.02σ), to be proposed upstream.
  • Sampler — nautilus with 2000 live points explores a fast JAX emulation of the theory; every posterior point is then importance-reweighted to the exact likelihood. The two chains use different random seeds, so their difference includes Monte Carlo noise.
Theory parity before the chains

The two theory vectors were compared point by point at the fiducial and at 12 prior draws: the largest band difference is 0.067σ on the data configuration and 0.008σ on the mock, in units of the Gaussian bandpower error.
