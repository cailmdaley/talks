SPT × Euclid 6×2pt: a Euclid DR1 key-project paper

  • Euclid shear and galaxy density × SPT-3G lensing
  • TR1 now, DR1 for the paper (public June 2027)
  • Same pipeline as the Planck + ACT 6×2pt paper
  • Overleaf set up on the Euclid template

Scope

The paper is one of the CMBX KP3 papers in the Euclid DR1 key-project plan: the Euclid photometric probes (shear and galaxy density) crossed with SPT-3G CMB lensing, as a 6×2pt analysis. It shares the KP2-3 pipeline with the Planck + ACT 6×2pt paper (Lembo, Legrand, Fabbian), so the same data vectors exist for ACT DR6 and SPT-3G and their agreement is a test in itself. The SPT-3G map today is the Main winter-field lensing reconstruction (GMV, profile-hardened). The full KP2-3 paper list is in backup.

Status

The Overleaf project exists on the Euclid template. Paper leads are coordinators, not necessarily first authors. Euclid DR1 products are final in early 2027 and public in June 2027; papers on DR1 data may be submitted before the public release, provided they match the data frozen in the internal archive.
