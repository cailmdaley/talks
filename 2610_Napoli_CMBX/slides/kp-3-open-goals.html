Open goals for KP2-3

  • Scale cuts: still to validate per probe; only roughly done for GCphoto
  • Consistency: LensMC vs MetaCal, SPT-3G vs ACT DR6
  • Systematics: null tests calibrated on mocks; deprojection
  • Likelihood: one shared development, on the 3×2pt teams' models
  • Chains: TR1 with the full nuisance model, then DR1

The pipeline runs from catalogues to a blinded likelihood, so the open work is about choices and validation. Scale cuts are the largest: the data-vector slide shows every band, because where to cut depends on how far the linear-bias model and the nuisance treatment hold, per family and per bin, and that is a decision to make explicitly, with each option tested against perturbation-theory contamination on theory and on the mocks. The data vectors exist for two shear methods and two CMB lensing maps, and their agreement is a check in itself. The systematics battery crosses survey-property templates with the galaxy density and shear maps and measures what deprojection changes. For the 3×2pt side of the model (intrinsic alignments, redshift and shear-calibration nuisances, the 3×2pt likelihood itself), the aim is to rely on the work of the 3×2pt experts rather than rebuild it; organising the likelihood development across the key project is part of that. Chains on the TR1 data come after these, followed by DR1.

Where these meet the 3×2pt work (from this meeting, day 2)

  • Likelihood: the CMB-lensing theory is merged in cloelib; the pseudo-$C_\ell$ branch needs mixing matrices, and cloelike has no CMBX data products yet. Raphaël Kou is converting the GLASS-mock output, which carries the mixing matrices, to CLOE format. The κκ noise-bias treatment is open.
  • Covariance: Spaceborne's mask-coupled Gaussian covariance is the JC2 standard; it is the natural route if κ joins the 3×2pt covariance, and a cross-check for our NaMaster Gaussian covariance. JC1 treats the full-shape × 3×2pt cross-covariance as negligible for the DR1 headline, and its jackknife-against-analytic method is a template for κ × 3×2pt.
  • Scale cuts: JC4 finds, in Cosmo-OWLS, that a $k = 0.2\,h$/Mpc cut gives more $S_8$ bias than full scales, so cutting scales does not by itself protect κ × γ against baryons. JC4 also uses SPT and ACT SZ as external baryon constraints, which overlaps KP4.
  • Consistency: the probe-consistency metric is still to be fixed before unblinding; ours (LensMC vs MetaCal, SPT-3G vs ACT DR6) should use the same one.
  • Timeline: DR1 shear-calibration ($m$) correction November to February; masks, selection and data vectors by November.
