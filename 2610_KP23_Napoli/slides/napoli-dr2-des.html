DR2 south follows DES

  • Rubin off-sky for 3+ months; official DR2 has no Rubin data
  • survey plan now sticks to DES in the south
  • DR2 south: 3570 deg², 3037 with DES

SPT-3G Main sits inside DES: good for SPT × Euclid DR2

Where this comes from

  • Hervé Aussel, Napoli day 5 (16 Oct), “Toward DR2”, slide 4: Euclid observed 7234 deg² for DR2; southern 3570 deg², of which 3037 with DES coverage; northern 3664 deg², 3177 with delivered UNIONS (UNIONS is observing the rest); anticipated DR2 input ~6700 deg². DR2 data acquisition runs to June 2027.
  • Euclid Slack, #ask-me-anything, 19 Oct: Knud Jahnke asked how the Rubin/LSST downtime (off-sky for more than three months) affects DR2. William Hartley: official DR2 does not include Rubin data, so no SGS impact for DR2; the loss is for the derived data products. Pierre Casenove: the reference survey has been modified to stick as closely as possible to DES and to favour the North in DR2; Rubin's DR1 timing could start to matter for DR3.
  • Euclid needs ground-based photometry (EXT) for photo-z; in the south that is DES. The thread raised the survey change in the context of Rubin's downtime; it does not state that as the cause.

For SPT × Euclid

DES was laid out to overlap the SPT-SZ survey, and SPT-3G Main lies inside the DES footprint, so a DR2 southern area that stays on DES keeps Euclid where SPT-3G is. The DR2 × SPT-3G overlap area has not been computed yet; this slide claims only the direction.
