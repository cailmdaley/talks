  6. To participate: external-collaborator status, please contact me

Cail Daley · CEA Paris-Saclay

  • The paper — the Euclid DR1 key-project paper SPT × Euclid 6×2pt: Euclid shear and clustering crossed with SPT-3G CMB lensing, submitted with the public DR1 release around June 2027.
  • TR1 × SPT-3G — the pipeline is validated on GLASS mocks and blinded as in the Euclid 3×2pt analysis; the posteriors use the analytic Gaussian + SSC covariance.
  • Small scales — SPT-3G's error bars in the κ crosses are smaller than ACT DR6's from $\ell \approx 200$ on TR1; projected to DR1 the two are comparable over most of the range, ACT DR6 tighter below $\ell \approx 300$ and SPT-3G 1.1–1.2× tighter from $\ell \approx 550$, more at the smallest scales (Cycle 1, where both covariances follow the same recipe).
  • DR2 — its southern area will overlap SPT-3G far more than DR1 does.
  • Open — the covariance plan needs SPT's mock reconstructions with their input κ; the scope (cosmologies, κκ, foreground mitigation) is open.
  • Participation — Euclid external-collaborator status, for people who will commit time before the DR1 papers in June 2027.
