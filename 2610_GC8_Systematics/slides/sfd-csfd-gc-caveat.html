CSFD's galaxy null is partly by construction

  • CSFD subtracts an LSS template built from WISE galaxies
  • A galaxy null checks transfer; it does not independently prove signal preservation
  • Compare dust-map alternatives one at a time
  • Use independent stellar/HI maps and mock recovery to test the correction

Chiang 2023 · arXiv:2306.03926 §§3–5 · Cycle-2 comparison on slide 5

CSFD uses WISE density templates and an AKARI point-source template, constrained by SDSS galaxy/quasar tomographic correlations. Euclid was not an explicitly named training set; its null is not guaranteed algebraically, and no object-level overlap was tested here. The construction nevertheless suppresses galaxy-correlated structure by design. The independent CIB route and shear/kappa tests therefore matter.

CSFD corrects SFD's reconstructed CIB, not every dust calibration/selection error, and it is not a direct correction for the different Planck R1.20 map. All thirteen Cycle-2 CSFD spectrum nulls pass; some compressed means are nonzero, as explained on slide 5. References: Chiang 2023 §§3.1, 3.5, 4.2, 5.1–5.2; DES Y6 Appendix A.
