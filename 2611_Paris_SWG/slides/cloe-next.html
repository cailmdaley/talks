Where this leaves the CMBX likelihood

  • cmbx main path: CLOElib + Theory WG emulators + CLOElike
  • κκ is not in CLOElike yet (?)
  • SPT, Planck and ACT κκ likelihoods as external CLOE modules (?)
  • Fast path: a JAX port of CLOElib's projection and emulator call
  • Main path — decided for the cmbx pipeline on 12 November 2026 and in production since 15 November: CLOE computes the theory (CLOElib with the Theory WG emulators) and the likelihood. The chain driver still builds a Cobaya model to hold the parameters and priors, and CCL still supplies the intrinsic-alignment constant, the distance for the linear-bias lens cut and the neutrino masses. eDR1like survives as adapters for what CLOE lacks.
  • Above z = 5 — the emulators are trained to $z = 5$; above it CLOElib continues $P(k)$ as a power law in $1+z$. Against CAMB-HMCode to the last-scattering surface, every variant is within 0.04σ (SPT and ACT, production Gaussian covariance) for κκ, κ×γ and κ×δ at the fiducial; the power law is −1.15 % on $C_\ell^{\kappa\kappa}$ at ℓ = 2696. The crosses see nothing above $z = 5$ (≤0.001σ). At prior corners the difference reaches 0.26σ against the fiducial covariance, which is too small there; in relative terms ~1–1.5 %, where every simple continuation misses. Not a blocker; $z > 5$ is 18–30 % of $C_\ell^{\kappa\kappa}$.
  • External κκ — adding SPT likelihoods to CLOE as an external module looks feasible (CMBX telecon, 5 November 2026); JC-1 welcomed it.
  • Fast path — the production chains sample CLOE through a JAX port of its projection and emulator call, checked against CLOE band by band and at the posterior (the JAX slide in this section). Packaged on its own for the SPT × Euclid analysis, it reproduces the production mock chain point for point. What CLOElib lacks for it (radiation in the background, the $z > 5$ continuation, the high-$k$ tail) is carried as local patches.
