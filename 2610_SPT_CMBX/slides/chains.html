Why σ(S8) = 0.012 is optimistic

Three plausible sources, each to be fixed before the full rerun on Cycle 2: scale cuts, n(z) priors, and missing covariance terms.

For scale: DES Y3 3×2pt gives $S_8 = 0.776 \pm 0.017$ and DES Y3 × SPT/Planck 6×2pt $0.792 \pm 0.012$, on a footprint of nearly 5000 deg² (arXiv:2206.10824, Table 1 and §2). TR1's WL area is a few hundred deg² (258 deg² binary-occupied), with several times DES's source density.

  • Covariance: Gaussian only, with neither the super-sample nor the connected non-Gaussian term. The super-sample term, computed and cross-checked against Spaceborne but not yet in production, widens σ(S8) by 6.6 %. On GLASS mocks in this configuration the parameter scatter is ~1.25× the Fisher σ. So the covariance accounts for tens of per cent, not a factor of two.
  • Model choices carry the rest: linear bias to $k_\mathrm{max} = 0.2\,h$/Mpc for δδ and 0.15 for γδ and κδ (arXiv:2203.12439's method); fixed magnification slopes; n(z) shift priors (σ 0.013–0.028) from the 3×2pt proposal, not calibrated for TR1; $m_\nu$ fixed; shear to ℓ = 3000 with baryons only through $T_\mathrm{AGN}$.
  • The model does not fit (Caveats), so σ belongs to a model the data reject.

The mock gate (four GLASS seeds, the same cosmology, no blind) recovers the truth within ~1σ, and the four seeds give a first measure of the scatter: ~1.25× the Fisher σ.

The data want an n(z) stretch

With the full vector, the lens n(z) stretches move far from their $\mathcal{N}(1, 0.1)$ prior and are measured by the data: bins 2–5 at 0.61–0.68 (posterior σ 0.03–0.05), bin 1 at 1.40 ± 0.07, bin 6 at 1.0 (SPT-3G and ACT DR6 alike; the source stretches stay prior-dominated). κ-only and κ + γδ leave them at the prior, so the pull comes from δδ. On an earlier blinded TR1 × SPT-3G product a shift-only lens model was rejected outright (best-fit χ² 778 with shifts only, 588 with the stretches freed), which is why the stretch stays despite the 3×2pt proposal's shift-only baseline. On Cycle 2 with the DR1 n(z) the lens n(z) stop narrowing.

Caveats

  • The full vector fails goodness of fit at its best fit on v1.1 ($\chi^2$ 748/605), driven by δδ: autos high, distant crosses low, the v1.1 lens n(z) pattern. On Cycle 2 with the DR1 n(z) the lens n(z) stop narrowing, the γδ band-1 excess vanishes, and a δδ excess remains in the distant crosses.
  • The full-vector runs use a repartitioned proposal; validated on κ+GL, $S_8$ is exact and $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$ comes out about 8 % narrow, so the full contours' $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$ upper side may be short.
  • Exploratory runs outside lc; production chains on Cycle 2 (tr1_v2) go through lc next.
