What the week means for KP2-3, and the TR1 results shown there

Cail Daley

CMBX KP2-3 telecon

22 October 2026

A report to the CMBX KP2-3 group after the joint OU-SHE / WLSWG meeting in Napoli, 12–16 October 2026. The first part collects what from the week bears on KP2-3. The second repeats the KP2-3 results shown there, on TR1 v1.1 and marked preliminary until this group has discussed them, plus the extinction template’s CIB test and the first blinded TR1 posteriors. The full Napoli talk, on behalf of the whole CMBX SWG, is at cailmdaley.github.io/talks/2610_Napoli_CMBX.
