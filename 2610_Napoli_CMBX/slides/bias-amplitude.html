Bias and the γκ amplitude stay blinded

Two estimators from the same crosses that, unlike the lens ratio, carry the blind; both panels are TR1 × SPT-3G under blind cmbx_dr1_a, and the pale bars show how far a blind-sized cosmology shift (the public envelope, $\pm 0.075$ in $S_8,$ $\pm 0.1$ in $\Omega_{\rm m}$) moves each estimator at fixed truth. Where the pale bars are as wide as the error bars, the number tells us nothing we may read.

Left: galaxy bias per lens bin, $b = (C^{\delta\delta}/T^{\delta\delta}) / (C^{\delta\kappa}/T^{\delta\kappa})$ against unit-bias templates, on linear scales only ($\ell \lt k_{\rm max}\chi,$ $k_{\rm max} = 0.2\,h/$Mpc, declared before looking). On linear scales $\delta\delta \propto b^2\sigma_8^2$ and $\delta\kappa \propto b\,\sigma_8^2$, so $\sigma_8$ cancels, but the CMB-lensing kernel carries a factor $\Omega_{\rm m}$ that the clustering auto-spectrum does not: $b \propto \Omega_{\rm m}^{-(0.5\text{–}0.8)},$ and a blind-sized shift moves it by ×0.80 to ×1.39. So the bias is blinded too, and the pitch slide's “bias free of $\sigma_8$” is as far as the claim goes. In the data, lens bins 1 and 2 have $\delta\kappa$ signal-to-noise of only 2.3 and 2.5 inside the cut, so their ratios are heavy-tailed and essentially unconstrained (open). Bins 4 and 5 come out about 2σ above the fiducial (2.2 and 3.5 against 1.37 and 1.50), but that is driven by the $\delta\delta$ auto-spectrum, whose residual against the fiducial rises steadily with ℓ inside the cut (in one of them from +1.4σ to +4.8σ by ℓ ≈ 280). A scale-independent blind cannot make a trend; nonlinear bias or an estimator effect reaching below ℓ ≈ 300 is the likely cause, so those two are not results either (open). Bins 3 and 6 (filled) sit within 1.4σ of the fiducial. On ten GLASS mocks the estimator recovers the input bias to 0.97–1.13 in bins 3 to 6, with a hint of a 5–10 % ratio bias at S/N ≈ 5; the mocks are linear by construction, so they cannot validate the scale cut.

Right: the γκ amplitude per source bin over $100 \lt \ell \lt 3000$, relative to the fiducial. It scales as $S_8^{2.6},$ so a blind-sized shift moves it by ×0.75 to ×1.40: as an $m$ or n(z) handle on its own it is useless before unblinding, which is why the lens ratio divides the cosmology out. Source bin 1 is drawn open: on the mocks it comes out $1.25 \pm 0.12,$ 2σ high, for a reason we have not found (possibly the low-z shear excess or source clustering, not established). Bins 2 to 6 recover 0.96 to 1.02 on the mocks.
