  2. SPT-3G κ crosses are tighter than ACT DR6 above $\ell \approx 200$
  3. Open: covariance with SPT mocks, κκ, foregrounds, inference
  4. DR2 in the south stays on DES, where SPT-3G is
  5. To take part: external-collaborator status, by June 2027

Cail Daley · CEA Paris-Saclay

On Euclid TR1 the pipeline produces a blinded 5×2pt data vector with SPT-3G and first blinded posteriors, validated on GLASS mocks and blinded as in the Euclid 3×2pt analysis. SPT-3G's error bars in the κ crosses are smaller than ACT DR6's from $\ell \approx 200$, by 1.5 and more. The covariance plan needs SPT's mock reconstructions with their input κ; the scope (cosmologies, κκ, foreground mitigation, inference) is open. Working on the paper requires Euclid external-collaborator status, for people who will commit time before the DR1 papers in June 2027.
