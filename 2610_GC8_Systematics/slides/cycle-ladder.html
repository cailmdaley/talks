C1’s template window suppressed $\bar\rho$

  • Rewindowing explains 77% of the κ rise.
  • Density theory normalization changed too.

The C1 template support was fragmented; low-ell dust leaked into its high-ell auto. Rewindowing uses C2 template support, retaining C1 tracer fields, template preparation, mean and n_iter=1. The window did not merely hide significance through larger error bars: it lowered the model-normalized compressed correlation itself. This is a window-dependent compression, not proof that the cosmology data vector was biased.

SPT kappa rises from 0.0815 ± 0.0251 to 0.1504 ± 0.0238, versus 0.1712 ± 0.0267 in C2. This explains 77% of the total shift. An exact ordered decomposition changes the template denominator at fixed C1 weights (+0.0551), then changes GLS weights (+0.0022), then the cross (+0.0115). The denominator dominates this tracer. The weights do not universally increase the mean: their contribution is negative for raw density bin 1. A Shapley decomposition averaging all six orders is saved too.

Release-specific tracer theory autos changed. For raw density bin 6, C2/C1 theory auto is 1.54–1.60 across the bands; changing only that normalization takes rewindowed rho from 0.1702 to 0.1364, matching C2 0.1364. Shear E6 theory auto changes by 11.5–12.5%; its rewindowed mean falls from 0.1600 to 0.1512 (C2 0.1470). Kappa theory is identical. Thus a same-kappa-map argument identifies a template/estimator effect, not a changed kappa sky; changing cross windows and estimator conventions can also change the cross.

The C1 vis baseline uses the saved matched-weight c1_vis_original control, not rho.json’s legacy unweighted correction. All rewindowed errors are full Gaussian blocks on the rewindowed footprint with zero template-data Wick. The legacy raw/shear/kappa originals and C2 rho.json rows use observed Wick; this small policy mismatch is retained and explicitly included in the covariance contribution, not silently called pure window weighting.

Residual significance is not measured: the cycles share maps and their cross-cycle covariance is absent. Before theory matching, raw density bins 5/6 and corrected bin 5 retain residuals at least about 2 Gaussian marginal-error units for any covariance (Cauchy-Schwarz bound). Do not claim every rewindowed point agrees with C2. Theory matching removes much of these density differences; catalogue, selection and estimator differences remain possible. No independent-error pull is presented as a detection significance.

Prediction before computing: rewindowing would raise C1 toward C2 but might leave a normalization-dependent residual. It survives the saved-product test.
