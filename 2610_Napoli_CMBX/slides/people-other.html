The DR1 key projects of the CMBX working group and their people. Each column is a key project with its topic, as listed in the CMBX splinter introduction in Barcelona: KP1-2, CMB ISW and lensing cross-correlation with Euclid observables; KP2-3, cosmology with the combination of Euclid photometric probes and CMB lensing; KP4, hot gas in the cosmic web via tSZ cross-correlation with GC, WL and clusters; KP5, peculiar velocities from CMB-Euclid cross-correlation. The additional probes (cross-correlations with AGNs, voids, the FIR CIB and cluster masses) are listed under the portraits. Someone working on two key projects appears in both. KP1-2 is led by Carlo Baccigalupi. In KP2-3 the first row is the KP leads (Carlo Baccigalupi, Giulio Fabbian, Louis Legrand, Margherita Lembo), then the data-vectors and systematics, likelihood and theory, and simulations and covariance workstreams in turn. KP4 is led by Nabila Aghanim and Laura Salvati, with the team shown on the title slide of the KP4 update in Barcelona. KP5 is led by Carlos Hernández-Monteagudo; the names under him are the team listed on the first slide of the KP5 update in Barcelona, which may be incomplete: G. Aricò, R. Bean, J. Chaves-Montero, H. Cortois, G. Fabbian, M. Fernández-Tonreiro, R. Génova-Santos, M. Migliaccio, M. Pérez-Sar, R. Rebolo, J.A. Rubiño-Martín.
