CLOE is ready for CMBX; the chains need emulators

  • κκ, κ × GC, κ × WL in cloelib
  • GLASS mocks going into CLOE format
  • open: κκ noise bias, mixing matrices
  • TATT-M ~4× NLA cost: emulators

Left half of the "Final Words" slide of the CLOE-org DR1-readiness talk (day 2, 13 Oct; speaker not recorded on the deck). Source: slides/27893.pdf, p. 18.

What was said

  • Day 2: "cloelib: the κκ, κ×GC and κ×WL spectra are merged." A branch, feature/cmb-lensing-pseudo-cl, computes pseudo-$C_\ell$ through mixing matrices and works, "but no pipeline produces CMB-lensing mixing matrices yet". "cloelike: not yet implemented, because no CMBX data products are cloe-compatible." Still open: how to handle the κκ noise bias. cloelib v0.11 is being frozen and is "almost the DR1 pipeline".
  • Day 2: Cail pointed Raphaël Kou at the GLASS-mock full output product, and he is converting it to CLOE format.
  • Day 3: TATT-M parameters and priors available by Christmas. Late session: TATT-M currently takes about 4× longer than NLA; emulators already exist that convert $A_s \leftrightarrow \sigma_8$ in fractions of a millisecond. DR2 session (Navarro-Gironés): chains are slow, NLA ~40 h, TATT ~142 h on the deck.
  • Emulators: Cail spoke with Ivan Sladoljev on 14 Oct; he is keen to build emulators for CMBX. Marina Migliaccio proposed he speak at the CMBX call on 29 Oct to decide how to proceed, possibly with a group working with him directly.

Why it matters to KP2-3

The KP2-3 likelihood plan layers on CLOE and euclidlib. The CMB-lensing theory is in cloelib, and the CMBX side is ready to be tested against CMBX data products, starting with the GLASS 6×2pt mocks in CLOE format. The full-nuisance nested runs on TR1 shown later take hours to a day per vector, so a move to TATT-M without an emulator multiplies an already long run. Likelihood design is Louis and Margherita's.
