  • Plotted — posterior mean minus the GLASS input value, in units of the posterior σ, for seed 3004 with each likelihood; grey squares: mean over 20 GLASS seeds of the production (eDR1like) chain with its standard error; crosses: a chain on the noiseless mock.
  • $S_8$ — −0.11σ (eDR1like), −0.12σ (CLOE); ensemble −0.04 ± 0.19σ.
  • $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$, $\sigma_8$ — +0.51σ / +0.50σ and −0.49σ / −0.47σ, the same for both and matching the ensemble (+0.55 ± 0.17σ, −0.57 ± 0.18σ). They lie along the $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$–$\sigma_8$ degeneracy, and the ensemble study reads them as a projection effect of the marginal posterior, not a theory bias.
  • Noiseless mock — shown for context; its $S_8$ offset (−0.49σ) is larger than seed 3004's or the ensemble's, and the sources do not explain it.
