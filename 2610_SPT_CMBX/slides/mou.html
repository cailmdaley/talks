Euclid–SPT MoU project: “Cross-correlations between galaxy density, shear and CMB lensing”

  • Joint project approved June 2025, part of the Euclid DR1 key project KP-CMBX-2/3
  • Scope: proposed as 3×2pt (γκ, δκ, κκ); 6×2pt now looks feasible

The Euclid–SPT memorandum of understanding was signed in November 2024. Under it, the joint project “Cross-correlations between Galaxy Density, Shear, and CMB Lensing with Euclid & SPT-3G” was proposed on 3 June 2025 and approved as part of the Euclid DR1 key project DR1-KP-CMBX-3/2. It measures the cross-correlations of Euclid galaxy density and shear with SPT-3G CMB lensing, together with the CMB-lensing auto-spectrum: the CMB-lensing block of the Euclid 6×2pt data vector, on the SPT-3G Main field.

CMB lensing adds a high-redshift source plane with independent systematics, which helps calibrate the shear multiplicative bias and the galaxy bias and tightens the Ωm–σ8 constraint. The project builds on the first Euclid Q1 galaxy density × CMB lensing measurement (Fabbian et al., in Euclid collaboration review) and on DES Y3 shear × SPT-3G D1 CMB lensing (Ouellette, Omori et al., arXiv:2606.26223). Under the MoU, mm-wave analysis is led by SPT members and optical/IR analysis by Euclid Consortium members.

Source: the external-collaborator batch request built from the 3 June 2025 proposal.
