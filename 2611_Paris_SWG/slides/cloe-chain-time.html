Where a full chain's time goes

  • Bars — wall time of one full-vector chain on one Leonardo DCGP node, in minutes.
  • CLOE in JAX — the blinded TR1 × SPT-3G full chain: 32 min (38 parameters, 247 000 likelihood points, effective sample size 2714). 16.4 min are spent in the likelihood and 15.9 min in everything else, mostly nautilus itself. The TR1 × ACT DR6 chain is the same within 1 min.
  • Explore + reweight — the path of the chains in the comparison: JAX exploration (20–25 min) followed by the exact reweighting of 8 500 (data) to 18 700 (mock) points; measured, the range spans the two arms.
  • Hatched — estimated, not run: a nautilus chain sampling CLOE + emulator directly needs about 200 000–250 000 evaluations at 8.0 core-s each, so 4–5 h on one node (11–14 h with CAMB), assuming perfect parallel efficiency.
Reading

With a 4 ms likelihood, nautilus's own work (building its bounds and sampling from them) is now half of the wall time, so further speed-ups have to come from the sampler as much as from the likelihood.
