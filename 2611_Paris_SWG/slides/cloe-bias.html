Both likelihoods recover the GLASS truth equally

  • Black and ochre — one GLASS mock (seed 3004) run with each likelihood: posterior mean minus the input value, with the posterior's own 1σ as the bar (1 in these units). The two agree to 0.02σ in every parameter shown.
  • Grey — the mean of that offset over 20 GLASS mocks run with eDR1like, with its standard error. It says where a typical mock lands, so the single mock sitting on it is expected, not a coincidence.
  • $S_8$ — recovered: −0.11σ (eDR1like) and −0.12σ (CLOE) on the one mock, −0.04 ± 0.19σ over the 20.
  • $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$, $\sigma_8$ — the 20-mock means are +0.55 ± 0.17σ and −0.57 ± 0.18σ, so the marginal means do sit half a σ from the truth, in opposite directions along the $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$–$\sigma_8$ degeneracy. The ensemble study reads this as a projection effect of marginalising a curved posterior, not a theory error; it is the same with both likelihoods (+0.51σ / +0.50σ and −0.49σ / −0.47σ on the one mock).
  • Coverage — over the 20 mocks, the 68 % / 95 % $S_8$ intervals contain the truth in 80 % / 100 % of cases.
