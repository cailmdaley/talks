  • Method — every row except clusters adds theory at a hidden cosmology minus theory at a reference to the measured statistics (Muir et al. 2020).
  • What is blinded — 3×2pt: $C_\ell$, $\xi$ and COSEBIs for shear, positions and their cross. GC spectro: $P_\ell$ and $\xi_\ell$, before and after reconstruction. CMBX: every spectrum of the 6×2pt vector, when it is written. Clusters: the posterior.
  • Drawn from — how the hidden cosmology is chosen. 3×2pt has not fixed this yet (next slide); GC spectro rejects draws that move the BAO or RSD parameters by more than 2× the DR1 tolerance.
  • GC spectro versus 3×2pt — same tool (blindspot, on cloelib) and same formula, different parameters, distribution, reference and seed. GC spectro does not shift the amplitude.
  • Not blinded by the official pipeline — cross-correlations, catalogues, n(z), shear calibration. Nothing blinds the CMB-lensing crosses unless CMBX does.
WL / 3×2pt

The shifts are the blindspot photometric notebook's: 100 points in $(\Omega_c, A_s)$, the rest of the cosmology at the reference, each turned into a shift with cloelib. The prototype draws them from a mock weak-lensing chain's posterior; a 2×2pt-posterior variant and uniform sampling as GC does are also on the table (Felicitas Keil, 10 November; Martin Crocce, 13 November, #dr1-photometric-inference-and-modelling). JC-2 validates the shifts on noiseless synthetic DR1 vectors (nowcast v2, South) and decides at its 24 November telecon (Alessio Spurio Mancini, #dr1-kp-jc-2, 11 November). LE3 then applies the selected shift after its WL production run, which starts the week of 23 November ("Blinding CCB (delivery of shifts) and run", Samuel Farrens, #sgs-ou-le3-wl, 10 November). Earlier: TR1 Cycle 1 shifts delivered (#33161), C1 blinded (#33337), C2 WL power spectra blinded 7 October 2026 (#35269).

GC spectroscopic

DR1 IT2 (Redmine #36596; GC-SWG telecon slides 49–50): 200 draws uniform within $\omega_\mathrm{cdm}$ ±0.0123, $w_0$ ±0.75, $w_a$ ±2.25, $h$ ±0.1, with $w_0 + w_a < 0$, around flat ΛCDM with $\Omega_\mathrm{m} = 0.319$, $h = 0.67$, $\sigma_8 = 0.83$. A draw is rejected if in any redshift bin $|\alpha_\parallel - 1|$, $|\alpha_\perp - 1|$ or $|\Delta f\sigma_{12}|$ exceeds twice the DR1 tolerance. The bias and growth rate are rescaled by $\sigma_{12}^\mathrm{blind}/\sigma_{12}^\mathrm{fid}$, so the broad-band amplitude stays unblinded. The shifts now come from a script port of the blindspot notebook (open blindspot PR #45).

Clusters

CL-SWG Blinding Strategy page (v4, March 2025): run MCMC on unblinded data and shift the posterior before saving, with the shift in a non-human-readable file.

CMBX

Redmine #31813 in dr1-kp-cmbx-3: a cosmology-shift blind of the full 6×2pt vector, drawn uniformly within ±0.1 in $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$ and ±0.075 in $S_8$ of Planck 2018, the chosen index sealed. Code: dr1_cmbx MR !9. Validation criteria and the unblinding procedure are open (#33511, #33512).

Policy

"The actual blinding realization is different for each project. Unless a project explicitly involves a joint probe analysis, comparing results, even if based on the same data, should be avoided." (Redmine BlindingWL, v2, November 2023.) JC-1's blinding deck (Marseille 2025, DMSF 26503): "3x2pt and GCsp-2pts have inconsistent shifts always" and "3x2pt and CMBX have inconsistent shifts". Unblinding is "decided by the Euclid Science Coordination Group after being requested by the relevant key project leads" (DMSF 19229); "The 3x2pt analysis cannot unblind before the individual probes" (BlindingWL). The 3×2pt TR1 analysis plan (31 October 2025) adds: "GC-SWG, TH-SWG, CMBX-SWG, and CG-SWG can potentially unblind WL-SWG. JC-1 provides a natural discussion forum for cross-SWG cosmology activities."
