Timeline to the June 2027 release

The Euclid DR1 lane is the public-facing part of the DR1 plan: the final shear catalogues and redshift distributions in February 2027, unblinding of the two-point measurements by the end of March, and the public DR1 release in June 2027, when the DR1 key-project papers are submitted.

The SPT × Euclid lane is this paper's plan. Blinded TR1 chains exist now (the results slide). The final data products (data vectors, covariance, mocks) are planned for January to February 2027, chains from February to April, the Euclid internal review from April, and submission with the public DR1 in June 2027. The SPT products of the next slide are needed for the covariance, so they sit before the final data products.
