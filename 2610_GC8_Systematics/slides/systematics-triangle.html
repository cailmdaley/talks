Implied contamination stays under half a $\sigma$Cycle 2: CIB-linked proxy reaches $0.83\,\sigma_G$

Built in two steps. First one pair close up, $\gamma_6 \times \kappa$ against the extinction template: the card a click opens on the full triangle. Its top row is the template's measured cross with each of the pair's tracers, $C_\ell^{\gamma_6 S}$ and $C_\ell^{\kappa S}$, each scaled by the integer power $\ell^n$ that makes its error bars most nearly equal across the bands ($\ell^2$ for most crosses, $\ell$ for $\kappa$ and for some source bins, as each axis label says), each with its $\chi^2$ over the 15 bands against zero and PTE (full Gaussian covariance; $\kappa$ diagonal only in Cycle 1, full block in Cycle 2). Its bottom panel is what those two crosses imply for the pair if they were all leakage, $X_\ell^{ab} = C_\ell^{aS} C_\ell^{bS} / C_\ell^{SS}$, in units of $\sigma_{ab}$, the Gaussian error of $C_\ell^{ab}$ in the blinded data vector. Then the card shrinks into its cell and the view is the whole data vector, one cell per pair of tracers.

Every panel spans $\pm 0.75\,\sigma_{ab}$, with $\pm 0.5\,\sigma_{ab}$ shaded; a band beyond the scale is a triangle on the edge. In Cycle 1 that range holds 99.6 % of all bands over every template and variant (99.69 % for the LensMC × SPT-3G state shown here). In Cycle 1 the bars are $\sigma_X/\sigma_{ab}$, first-order error propagation that ignores the covariance between the two crosses and gives the template's auto-spectrum $C_\ell^{SS}$ a Knox error (it treats a fixed map as a Gaussian field), which is least reliable at a tiling-periodic template's harmonics. So the contamination is in units of the data's Gaussian $\sigma_{ab}$, but its own error bar is not purely Gaussian. Their size varies with $\ell$ because the template's own power does; no power of $\ell$ evens them out, since it cancels in the ratio.

In Cycle 1 the lens-bin crosses use $\delta$ divided by the VMPZ test visibility weights, as on the previous slide. Cells outlined in orange are those where both template crosses behind the cell fail, so the pair shares a detectable correlation with the template: each cross's $\chi^2$ PTE over the 15 bands is below $10^{-4}$ (full Gaussian covariance; $\kappa$ diagonal). The PTEs are not calibrated on mocks (about a hundred correlated template crosses per variant), so the threshold is a guide, not a significance. The one flagged cross is $\delta_5 \times$ extinction, so the one outlined cell is $\delta_5\delta_5$ under extinction. Without the visibility weights, extinction also flagged lens bins 2, 4 and 6, and exposures and noise lens bin 1. Inside a card, the failing cross carries its PTE in orange. A shared correlation with the template needs both crosses to be nonzero, so the pair's PTE is the larger of the two crosses' PTEs on its card.

The buttons above the contamination panel pick the template; on the full triangle a click on a cell opens its card. In Cycle 1, where $X$ is resolved (above twice its error), nothing exceeds about $0.4\,\sigma_{ab}$: the largest are noise and exposures in $\delta_2\gamma_3$ at $\ell \approx 440$ (MetaCal, $0.37 \pm 0.16$) and in $\delta_3\delta_3$ at $\ell \approx 550$ ($0.30 \pm 0.14$), both at the tiling harmonic. The nominal maxima above 1 (PSF at $\ell \approx 690$ in pairs with $\delta_1$, and with MetaCal in source bin 2 up to about 2) sit at a dip of the template's own power, where $\sigma_X$ is as large as $X$; they are the edge triangles. The figure here is LensMC $\times$ SPT-3G; the KP2-3 deck has all four shear $\times$ CMB variants, from which the MetaCal numbers above come.

Cycle 2

The Cycle-2 maximum resolved proxy is extinction in $\delta_4\gamma_6$ at $\ell=2148$: $X/\sigma_G=0.828\pm0.137$ (resolved means $|X|>2\sigma_X$, as for Cycle 1). This mostly measures CIB-correlated LSS, not survey contamination (slide 5); the Cycle-1 half-$\sigma$ headline does not carry over. The only other template with resolved bands is exposures, peaking at $0.134\pm0.064$ in $\delta_1\delta_1$ at $\ell=1712$. Noise, PSF, zodiacal light and stars have no resolved bands under this definition.

The worst nominal cell is PSF in $\gamma_1\gamma_4$ at $\ell=349$: $+24.603\pm21.069\,\sigma_G$, unresolved at a deep template-auto dip ($C^{SS}=1.682\times10^{-13}$). Its two crosses are not detections; dividing by the small auto inflates both the proxy and its propagated uncertainty. This is an effect-size proxy with unstable denominators, not a confidence bound on contamination. The nominal per-template maxima are extinction $0.828\pm0.137$ ($\delta_4\gamma_6$, 2148), exposures $0.884\pm0.536$ ($\kappa\delta_5$, 112), noise $1.095\pm0.572$ ($\delta_1\gamma_5$, 112), PSF as above, zodiacal light $0.478\pm0.437$ and stars $0.513\pm0.454$ (both $\gamma_1\gamma_1$, 112), all in units of the science Gaussian error.

Of 8100 nominal bands (6 templates × 90 pairs × 15 bands), 7941 (98.04 %) lie within $\pm0.75\sigma_G$. By template the counts are 1346, 1346, 1338, 1211, 1350 and 1350 out of 1350 in the order above (99.70 %, 99.70 %, 99.11 %, 89.70 %, 100 %, 100 %). The orange outlines use each tracer's full 15-band Gaussian null block, including $\kappa$ in Cycle 2; they are guides, not matched-mock-calibrated family significances.

Inputs are the tr1_v2 systematics/gaussian_null_family.tar crosses and Gaussian blocks, all six per-template archives' contamination.csv autos, and likelihood_product/likelihood_input.tar science Gaussian covariance. The writer calls every template's auto column c_extinction_extinction; each is read from its own archive, not reused from extinction. All template/science window hashes, repeated multipole grids, cross values and pair-major covariance ordering were checked. Cycle 2 conditions on the fixed template auto: no Knox term; $\sigma_X$ propagates Gaussian cross errors but omits cross-cross covariance. The earlier template-auto Knox caveat applies only to Cycle 1. Density is visibility-corrected at the source, comparable with Cycle-1 visibility-weighted density.

The independent extinction_contamination_metric.csv agrees with the triangle's $\kappa$ row to JSON rounding: maxima $+0.32848\sigma_G$ for $\kappa\delta_6$ and $+0.43478\sigma_G$ for $\kappa\gamma_6$, both at $\ell=1712$. These are smaller than the full triangle's $\delta_4\gamma_6$ maximum because that CSV covers only $\kappa\times$ tracers. extinction_deprojection_effect.csv measures a different operation: scalar GC deprojection moves $\kappa\delta$ by at most $0.23827\sigma_G$ ($\delta_4$, $\ell=221.5$, negative shift), with $Q_{2C}=0.00483$–$0.05377$. It is consistent with a sub-$\sigma_G$ change in these $\kappa$ crosses, but is not equal to the leakage proxy; $Q_{2C}$ is an uncalibrated baseline-normalized effect size, not a PTE.
