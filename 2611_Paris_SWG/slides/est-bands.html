Band level: the deficit follows tSZ-like tracers

  • κ × galaxies: unhardened GMV 10.0 ± 1.8 % below hardened
  • κ × Planck $y$: 11.6 ± 1.1 %; κ × CIB: 3.3 ± 0.4 %
  • Unmasking bright sources and clusters: unchanged, 9.6 ± 1.9 %
  • Hardened GMV against pol-only: −6.3 ± 6.1 %

Reading: not a response rescaling; consistent with tSZ, named after the fact

Fractions of the signal over 300 < ℓ < 1500, blinded TR1 × SPT-3G D1
  • What is measured — the difference of κ × tracer bandpowers between two SPT-3G estimators, as a fraction of the signal over 300 < ℓ < 1500, with the joint Gaussian covariance of the difference. Differences of estimators on the same blinded data do not depend on the blind.
  • Galaxies — unhardened GMV sits 10.0 ± 1.8 % below profile-hardened GMV (15.8 ± 3.3 % for the temperature-only pair), with nothing below ℓ 300 and a sign change at ℓ ≈ 2700. Clustering and shear samples agree, with no order in redshift across the 12 bins.
  • Templates — the same difference with the Planck NILC $y$ map and the GNILC 545 GHz CIB map as tracers. A response difference between estimators would give the same fraction for every tracer; κ × CIB is 3.6σ below κ × galaxies (2.9σ when restricted to the TR1 overlap), with the same ℓ shape at about a third of the amplitude.
  • Masking — leaving the sources above 6 mJy and the clusters above ξ = 10 unmasked does not change the shift, so it does not live around the masked objects.
  • Hardened against pol-only — the baseline's own difference from polarization-only is −6.3 ± 6.1 % in κ × galaxies, +1.9 ± 3.3 % in κ × $y$ and 0.0 ± 1.2 % in κ × CIB: no detection at the band level.
Caveats

The tSZ reading was made after seeing the tracer pattern; the criterion stated beforehand (a template exceeding the galaxies) is not met. On the TR1 overlap alone, hardened minus polarization-only in κ × CIB is +10.1 ± 5.1 %, a coherent drift of about 1σ per band with no band beyond 2σ, of the opposite sign to the $S_8$ trend. Chains varying the maximum multipole and with the unhardened estimators were still running when these slides were made.

Not yet possible

Per-estimator response and simulation-calibrated errors need SPT reconstruction simulations with their input κ; foreground injection needs the Agora simulation; tSZ-deprojected, cross-ILC, per-frequency and curl SPT maps have not been delivered.
