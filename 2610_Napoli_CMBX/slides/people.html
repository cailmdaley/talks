The people of DR1-KP-CMBX-2-3, which unites two key projects run as one effort: KP2, CMB lensing cross-correlation with Euclid observables, and KP3, cosmology with combination of Euclid photometric probes and CMB lensing (names as in the KP2/3 paper list, Perpetual Slides DR1-CMBX-KP2/3). The first four are the key-project leads (Carlo Baccigalupi, Giulio Fabbian, Louis Legrand, Margherita Lembo), then the data-vectors and systematics, likelihood and theory, and simulations and covariance workstreams in turn.
