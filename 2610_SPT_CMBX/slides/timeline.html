Timeline to the Euclid DR1 release

The slide opens on where the analysis is now, the blinded TR1 chains, and builds up from there.

The top lane is the DR1 data products, from the Napoli meeting. The DR1 shear catalogues (the R3 reprocessing, with a much smaller PSF-size bias than R2) reach the whole Euclid Consortium at the end of October or start of November, blinded until the end of March like every DR1 shear product. DR1-Foundation is released on 12 November 2026. The n(z) are close to final at the end of November. The final calibrated catalogues, with the final multiplicative shear bias m from SHE-CAL (first run in November), and the final n(z) arrive in February, with the n(z) at the end of February when the products freeze; the bar fades into March because the dates are soft. The two-point measurements are unblinded by the end of March, and the public DR1 release is in June 2027.

The bottom lane is this paper's plan. The DR1 chains start as soon as the n(z) are close to final, around the start of December, and run on the blinded DR1 data, ramping up as the products firm up until they converge on the final products in February and March. Papers go into the Euclid Consortium's internal (ECP) review at about the end of March, so that the roughly two months of review end before the June release, with a few weeks of margin, and are submitted on release day. They go in even if numbers may still shift: the DR1-Foundation strong-lensing papers were written ahead with TBD numbers and needed only a final update. The SPT products listed later in the talk are needed for the covariance, so they come before the final chains.

Sources: Napoli day 1 (DR1 timeline, release papers, ECP review timing: about two months), day 2 (3×2pt pipeline group's DR1 timeline, m bias November to February) and day 3 (roadmap: full non-final chains on blinded DR1 in December).
