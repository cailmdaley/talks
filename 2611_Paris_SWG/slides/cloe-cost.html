The emulator as reweighting target is projected from its per-evaluation cost; a nautilus chain sampling CLOE directly needs about 200 000–250 000 evaluations, so 4–5 h with the emulator and 11–14 h with CAMB, assuming perfect parallel efficiency.

Emulator accuracy

CLOElib's CosmoPower-JAX HMCode-2020 emulator (the Theory WG network) agrees with CLOE + CAMB to 0.006σ per band at five of the six draws and 0.042σ at the sixth. The GLASS mocks use dark-matter-only HMCode-2020, which the emulators do not provide, so the emulator is compared at the theory level on the data configuration, not in a chain.

The reference that never converged

The earlier dr1_cmbx reference, Cobaya with the eDR1like spline likelihood, did not converge on the full vector in more than 17 h on four nodes; it converged on κ-only and κ + galaxy–lensing vectors. That is why the comparison uses the explore-and-reweight path for both likelihoods.
