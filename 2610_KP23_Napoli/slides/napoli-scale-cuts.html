Sources

The CLOE splinter (15 Oct) and the 3×2pt sessions of days 2–3. Positions are the sessions'; individual speakers are not attributed.

  • CLOE splinter, agreed: contamination tests marginalize over nuisance parameters ($T_\mathrm{AGN}$ etc.), KiDS-style.
  • The tolerance (0.5σ on $S_8$ / $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$–$S_8$ for bias, 0.3–0.5σ for baryons, DES/KiDS-style) sets the constraining power and is still pending.
  • The DES-style Δχ² < 1 criterion was judged not to transfer to Euclid (day 3, late session), and the CLOE splinter leaned to dropping that method (M3) for Euclid. Day 2 (Camera): the DES-style per-pair Δχ² is about 5× too loose at DR1 depth. The next slide shows the methods.
  • CLOE splinter: $k_\mathrm{max}$ depends on probe and redshift, larger for shear than for clustering, and the crosses need their own. In the Euclid 3×2pt $w_0w_a$ validation, adding the CMB likelihood forced removing more scales. A public Slack channel / task force will organise the work.
  • Day 2 timeline: final scale cuts Dec–Jan, one set for N+S and one S-only.
