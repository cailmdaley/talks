Bias and the γκ amplitude stay blinded

Two estimators from the same crosses that, unlike the lens ratio, carry the blind; both panels are TR1 × SPT-3G under blind cmbx_dr1_a, and the pale bars show how far a blind-sized cosmology shift (the public envelope, $\pm 0.075$ in $S_8,$ $\pm 0.1$ in $\Omega_{\rm m}$) moves each estimator at fixed truth. Where the pale bars are as wide as the error bars, the number tells us nothing we may read.

Left: galaxy bias per lens bin, $b = (C^{\delta\delta}/T^{\delta\delta}) / (C^{\delta\kappa}/T^{\delta\kappa})$ against unit-bias templates, on linear scales: $\delta\delta$ at $\ell \lt k_{\rm max}\chi$ with $k_{\rm max} = 0.2\,h/$Mpc and $\delta\kappa$ at $0.15\,h/$Mpc, the split that the nonlinear-bias contamination test validates ($0.22\,\sigma_{\rm 2D}$ shift in $(S_8, \Omega_{\rm m})$, against 0.52 with 0.2 everywhere). On linear scales $\delta\delta \propto b^2\sigma_8^2$ and $\delta\kappa \propto b\,\sigma_8^2$, so $\sigma_8$ cancels, but the CMB-lensing kernel carries a factor $\Omega_{\rm m}$ that the clustering auto-spectrum does not: $b \propto \Omega_{\rm m}^{-(0.5\text{–}0.8)},$ and a blind-sized shift moves it by ×0.80 to ×1.41. So the bias is blinded too, and the pitch slide's “bias free of $\sigma_8$” is as far as the claim goes.

Only bins 3 and 6 (filled) are shown as measurements: 1.09 and 1.89 against fiducial 1.32 and 1.71, within 1σ. Lens bin 1 is not measured at this cut: its $\delta\kappa$ keeps a single band at $\ell \approx 112,$ where the mock S/N is about 1, and its point at 0.49 carries a deceptively small, heavy-tailed error bar (on the mocks $b/b_{\rm true}$ in that bin ranges from −1.3 to 7.7 over ten seeds), so it is drawn faint. Bin 2 ($\delta\kappa$ S/N 1.9 on the data) is unconstrained. Bins 4 and 5 come out high, 1.76 and 3.08 against 1.37 and 1.50, and are not results either. Part of that is the estimator: a ratio with a noisy denominator is biased high, and on the mocks it returns $b$ 15–21 % high in bins 4 and 5 at their S/N of 3–4. The rest tracks the $\delta\delta$ auto-spectrum, whose residual against the fiducial rises steadily with ℓ inside the cut (in one of them from +1.4σ to +4.8σ by ℓ ≈ 280). A scale-independent blind cannot make a trend, so nonlinear bias or an estimator effect reaching below ℓ ≈ 300 is the likely cause. The mocks are linear by construction, so they cannot validate the scale cut; the contamination test does that.

Right: the γκ amplitude per source bin over $100 \lt \ell \lt 3000$, relative to the fiducial. It scales as $S_8^{2.6},$ so a blind-sized shift moves it by ×0.75 to ×1.40: as an $m$ or n(z) handle on its own it is useless before unblinding, which is why the lens ratio divides the cosmology out. Source bin 1 is drawn open: on the mocks it comes out $1.25 \pm 0.12,$ 2σ high, for a reason we have not found (possibly the low-z shear excess or source clustering, not established). Bins 2 to 6 recover 0.96 to 1.02 on the mocks.
