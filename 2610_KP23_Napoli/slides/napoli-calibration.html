Calibration: first DR1 m, and n(z) beyond shift + stretch

  • m, incl. selection: LensMC −4.6 ± 0.4 %, MetaCal −1.8 ± 0.7 %
  • n(z), ~5 % errors (DR1-like): shift + stretch misses $S_8$
  • no fix yet; sim realism being checked
  • B-modes on R3 not settled: MetaCal North up to ~5σ; real and harmonic space disagree

Shear calibration (SHECAL, Nicolas Martinet, day 2)

  • Deck p. 10: LensMC m = −0.046 ± 0.004, MetaCal m = −0.018 ± 0.007. Preliminary, on 17 MER tiles (step 1b) with an EXT-DES bypass, and TR1 v2 quality cuts except VMPZ (pp. 11–12). The numbers were new that day and include selection.
  • Martinet's provisional breakdown: ~2 % selection bias, MetaCal measurement bias ~0, and a ~2 % LensMC model bias; not yet verified. The DR1 requirement in SIM is σm ~ 1.5 × 10⁻³ (deck).
  • The likelihood takes a single m (one σ) and c₁, c₂ per tomographic bin. Colour gradients add Δm ~ 2 × 10⁻³ per bin (Csizi+26). The factor-2 gap in the deep sims may be folded in as a ~3 × 10⁻³ m uncertainty.
  • The southern calibration is blocked on reduced EXT-DES data, with no bypass possible (backup slide "EXT photometry in the south").
  • Martin Kilbinger's reading at the CosmoStat group meeting (20 Oct): the requirement is on the uncertainty of m, not its value, and the sims use one PSF for both methods with no PSF error, so the quoted errors are a lower limit.
  • The 3×2pt prior table has $m \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 0.01)$, considered pessimistic in the day-3 session, where ~0.007 was already being reported. Centred on zero, it is far from the SHECAL LensMC value, so the numbers above bear on where an m prior is centred, not only on its width.

R3 B-modes (B-mode Tiger Team Slack, read 21 Oct)

  • Harmonic space (Ruiz Zapatero, 19 Oct, "everything in" masks): LensMC passes in both hemispheres, with both the SGS and the latest time-dependent c-bias correction (≤ 1.9σ with the simulation covariance at ℓmax 2000). MetaCal fails in the North: 3.5σ at ℓmax 1000 and 4.9σ at 2000 with the simulation covariance, 1.1–1.5σ with jackknife; 2.1–2.4σ North + South. The two covariances differ by 2–3×, so the σ depends on which is used.
  • Tessore (20 Oct): the MetaCal North E-mode amplitude is ~1.5× the other three (LensMC N/S, MetaCal S), and the North B-mode shows a J-shaped bias. Under investigation.
  • Real space (COSEBIs, Angus Wright, shown in the Napoli splinter slides): the opposite verdict, LensMC failing and MetaCal passing. Congedo (19 Oct): real and harmonic space give opposite results on the same data, which points to the estimators. Wright's early run was also hit by duplicated per-exposure rows and NaN corrected ellipticities (12 Oct).
  • Splitting the time-dependent c-bias correction by hemisphere takes the LensMC North B-mode from 1.85σ to 0.96σ (Ruiz Zapatero, 15 Oct). Congedo reports LensMC star–galaxy separation under-performing across DR1, worse near the LMC (Redmine #36646).

Redshift parametrisation (William d'Assignies, day 4)

  • Compared shift-and-stretch (n(z) moments 1–2) with DES Y6-style n(z) modes. For cosmic shear the first mode is essentially a mean-z shift.
  • Forecasts with Dumerchat's calibration sims: in a KiDS-like case (sub-1 % systematic error) shifts suffice. In the DES-like case labelled Euclid DR1, with systematic error of order 5 %, correcting the estimated n(z) by the difference in moments does not recover the true cosmology, even with broad priors on shift and stretch (p. 14). In the figure, $S_8$ moves from about 0.83 to about 0.89.
  • Next steps (p. 15): first check the realism of the simulation (the origin of the tails problem); if confirmed, either reweight the wide sample or go beyond moment-1 and 2 corrections, for which the current pipeline has no straightforward solution such as adopting the DES Y6 modes method.
  • The 3×2pt baseline is shift only, no stretch, which was contested in the day-3 session: LSST preparation work found shift-only very optimistic and pointed beyond shift + width.

Context for the κ crosses

κ × γ is linear in $(1+m)$, which the lens ratio checks (later slide). κ × δ depends on the lens n(z) width, so a stretch matters there, and the d'Assignies result says shift + stretch may not be enough at DR1 n(z) errors. The TR1 chains in this talk use shift + stretch per bin.
