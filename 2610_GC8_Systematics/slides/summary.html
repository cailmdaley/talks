The proposed weighting comparison follows the template-fidelity evidence, not an assumption that genuine Galactic extinction has no selection effect. A different diagnostic template cannot undo a catalogue's dereddening or selection history. The VMPZ visibility input set and fitted extinction weight have not been established by the map provenance audit; the question remains open.

Depth is not the only marginal non-extinction cell; see the full heatmap. LensMC B passes every template test in both cycles; MetaCal has a marginal zodiacal-bin-1 exception, not an extinction pattern. Next steps are null-PTE calibration against matched GLASS simulations, followed by recovery tests for weights/deprojection with both Galactic-selection and correlated-CIB injections. Quote bandpower changes against the science vector's Gaussian covariance and inspect worst bands, not only a global statistic.
