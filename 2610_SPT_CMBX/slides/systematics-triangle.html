What is plotted

Euclid TR1 v2 on the official tomographic bins (26 Oct catalogues), LensMC, crossed with the SPT-3G Main bias- and profile-hardened GMV κ. Template crosses are the production results/tr1_v2/systematics archives; $\sigma_{ab}$ is the full NaMaster Gaussian covariance of the blinded Cycle-2 likelihood input (rerun5, 27 Oct). The builder checks that the two share the same field windows, hash for hash. MetaCal and ACT DR6 are not on Cycle 2 in this figure yet.

The close-up's top row is the template's measured cross with each of the pair's tracers, $C_\ell^{\gamma_6 S}$ and $C_\ell^{\kappa S}$, each scaled by the power of ℓ that evens its error bars, with its χ² over the 15 bands against zero and PTE (full Gaussian covariance of the null family). Its bottom panel is $X_\ell^{ab} = C_\ell^{aS} C_\ell^{bS}/C_\ell^{SS}$ in units of the pair's Gaussian error $\sigma_{ab}$.

The five instrumental templates

Exposures, noise, PSF, zodiacal light and Gaia stars: no template cross fails (PTE above 10⁻⁴ everywhere), and over 5 × 1350 bands only three are resolved (|X| above twice its error), the largest $0.19\,\sigma_{ab}$. PSF shows edge triangles at ℓ ≈ 349 and 690: the template's own power dips about a hundredfold there, between its tiling harmonics, so X and its error both blow up ($\sigma_X \approx 12$ and $2\,\sigma_{ab}$); none of those bands is resolved.

Extinction is not a null

The VMPZ extinction template is the Planck 2013 thermal-dust E(B−V) (R1.20), a far-infrared map that carries the cosmic infrared background. Its cross fails with every lens bin (χ² 154–311 on 15 bands), with SPT κ (59) and with the three highest source bins (65, 133, 313), rising with ℓ and positive. Dust obscuration would lower the counts and leave shear and κ alone; a positive cross with δ, κ and high-z shear is large-scale structure in the template. So X here is an upper bound on a contamination the data do not show, not an estimate of one: 257 bands resolved, up to $0.9\,\sigma_{ab}$ in lens × high-z pairs at ℓ ≳ 2000. The template must not be deprojected; it would remove real clustering. GC-8 (28 Oct) carries the detail and the proposals for a dust map with less CIB.

Caveats

The PTEs are not calibrated on mocks yet (about a hundred correlated crosses per template), so the orange threshold is a guide. The errors on X propagate the two crosses' Gaussian errors with the template auto held fixed; the cross-cross covariance is left out.
