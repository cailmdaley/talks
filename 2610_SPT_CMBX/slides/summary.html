Cail Daley · CEA Paris-Saclay

The SPT × Euclid 6×2pt paper is in the CMBX DR1 key-project plan, on the KP2-3 pipeline shared with the Planck + ACT analysis. On TR1 the blinded data vectors with SPT-3G exist and the pipeline is validated stage by stage on GLASS mocks. Finishing the covariance and the mocks needs SPT's noise-bias curves and matched input and reconstructed simulations; access for SPT members runs through Euclid external-collaborator forms, after which the shared NERSC mirror opens.
