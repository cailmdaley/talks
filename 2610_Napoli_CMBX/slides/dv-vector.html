Euclid TR1 data: the full 5×2pt data vector

Data: Euclid TR1 LensMC shear and galaxy density, and the SPT-3G Main winter CMB lensing map; NaMaster bandpowers, 15 bands from $\ell = 100$ to 3000; full NaMaster Gaussian covariance; blinded (cmbx_dr1_a).

From here on the plots are measured on data: the Euclid TR1 release crossed with the SPT-3G and ACT DR6 lensing maps. With CMB lensing the 3×2pt data vector gains a row, shear × κ and galaxy density × κ: 5×2pt (the κ auto-spectrum, the sixth, is not in this vector). These are blinded data, built in three steps along one row of the data vector: the row of cross-spectra with the SPT-3G Main-field CMB lensing map. First its source-bin half: Euclid TR1 shear in six source bins against $\kappa$, $\ell C_\ell^{\gamma\kappa}$ in 15 NaMaster bandpowers from $\ell = 100$ to 3000. Error bars are $\sigma_G$, the square root of the diagonal of the full NaMaster Gaussian covariance. The black curve is the fiducial theory pushed through the same bandpower windows as the data: a fixed reference, not a fit. The strip under each panel is $(\text{data} - \text{fiducial})/\sigma_G$ over $\pm 3$, shaded at $\pm 1$; a band beyond it is marked by a triangle on its edge.

The view then pans along the same row to its lens-bin half, the galaxy-density cross $\delta\kappa$, on one shared vertical scale.

Finally the view pulls back to the whole data vector: 90 spectra and 1350 bandpowers, as a lower triangle of panels. Rows and columns run over the twelve Euclid tracers, source bins $\gamma_i$ then lens bins $\delta_i$, and the CMB lensing row is the base, in the order just panned along. Every panel shows $\ell C_\ell$; the panels of a row share one vertical scale (its shear columns and its density columns separately), so shapes compare along a row. The overview carries no y-axis labels; the close-ups and the detail cards do. The galaxy-clustering amplitudes vary pair by pair: fitting one amplitude per pair against the windowed fiducial (full covariance), the δδ autos sit high (about 1.0 to 1.7), the crosses between non-adjacent lens bins sit low (about 0.4 to 0.6), and δκ sits near 1. Each pair is flat in ℓ; a constant offset only looks like an excess growing with ℓ because σ shrinks. The pattern points at less inter-bin n(z) tail overlap than the delivered n(z) predict, with intrinsic alignments contributing on the source side; magnification at the measured flux slopes does not explain it.
