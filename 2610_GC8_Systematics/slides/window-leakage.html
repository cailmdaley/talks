Cycle 1's fragmented window leaks dust power to high $\ell$

Same map: on the Cycle-2 window, Cycle 1's κ $\bar\rho$ rises 0.082 → 0.150 (Cycle 2: 0.171).

The same extinction source values under different template windows give different measured high-ell power. C1's fragmented support leaks low-ell dust power into high measured bands; its template auto is 40.9 times the rewindowed auto in the highest band. The low-only injection control in window-leakage/lowell.json establishes leakage directly; this plot shows the effect on the saved data instead of the window power. Both original windows are C2-type apodized over 0.15 degrees. The effect is not just a change of error bars: the crosses and template denominator also change.

The left panel is the deconvolved extinction auto (mag²), not an arbitrary rescaling. The right panel shows ell times the raw density-bin-6 cross (mag), with marginal Gaussian error bars on absolute axes. It shows ell times the cross on a linear ordinate, restricted to ell>300 to avoid the low-ell noise dominating the comparison. The horizontal axis remains logarithmic, and every selected point and Gaussian error bar is retained; no division by errors is applied. C1-original, C1 with the C2 template window, and C2 are all overplotted. Sources: results/scratch/gc8/window-leakage/auto.json, c1_rewindowed_g5.json, and rho/rho.json c1/c2/vmpz/g5/raw.

The controlled direction actually measured is C1 tracer data with the C2 template window, not C2 tracer data with the C1 window. The files named c2prep_c1window retain C1 tracer maps and change template preparation; they are not evidence for the latter direction. No new reverse-direction spectrum was computed for this revision. The saved ladder gives κ 0.0815 ± 0.0251 → 0.1504 ± 0.0238 → 0.1712 ± 0.0267. For raw δ6, C1-rewindowed is 0.1702; matching C2's theory normalization gives 0.1364, equal to C2's 0.1364. The κ window shift accounts for 77% of the cycle rise. This does not establish a bias in cosmology or the science vector.
