TR1: Cycle 2 is out, Cycle 3 brings R3

  • Cycle 2 (CosmoHub): v1.1 shear, new tomographic bins, DR1 n(z)
  • Cycle 3, R3 shear, aimed at early Nov:
    • PSF size bias ~10× smaller
    • southern B-mode excess ~5σ → <2σ
  • n(z): close to final end Nov, final end Feb
  • still moving: c-bias (decision Dec), LensMC B-modes

In this pipeline: Cycle 2 data vectors built; Cycle 3 when it ships

What was said

  • Day 2: "Cycle 2 catalogues are on CosmoHub, unblinded over ~500 deg², with analysis guidelines to avoid accidental unblinding." Day 1: TR1 v2 uses the DR1 n(z) (SAS, or Tessore's nowcast kit); the CosmoHub tom_bin_id fix is pending. Cycle 2 (CosmoHub TR1.B v2.0) is the TR1 area cut from the full DR1 photo-z production: the same v1.1 shear measurements and rows, with new tomographic binning and the DR1 n(z) (Farrens and Hartley, 14 Oct; recorded in science/cmbx/data/tr1-v2-cycle2). R3 is the first change to the shear measurement itself.
  • Day 1, R3: "fixed the PSF size bias, which was a few percent and varied across the sky in R2; it is now ~10× smaller", and "took the R2 harmonic-space B-mode excess in the South from ~5σ to <2σ". The TR1 shear in the results shown here is pre-R3.
  • Dates, day 1: EC-wide release aimed at end Oct / start Nov 2026 (Taylor); end Nov 2026, close-to-final n(z) for the Cycle 3 sample selection and DR1 (DR1_WLSOUTH_R3); end Feb 2027, final n(z) with confident uncertainties (Hartley). That Cycle 3 is the R3 selection is the scribe's reading of that timeline.
  • 3×2pt splinter: the static c-bias is out (Tiger Team), final decision December 2026. CLOE splinter: LensMC B-modes are significant again with flipped sign, pending overnight runs.

n(z) and m are still moving

  • Working assumption (Hartley): DR1 won't do much better than Δz ~ 0.02(1+z) on the mean, 0.05(1+z) on the width. Hildebrandt (day 3): ~2 % on mean z is not good enough for DR1 cosmic shear; ~1 % or better is needed. n(z) are NO_WEIGHT only so far.
  • Whether n(z) stretch will be delivered was disputed in the day-3 splinter.
  • First DR1 m (day 2 afternoon, fresh, includes selection, preliminary): LensMC $m \approx -4.6\times10^{-2} \pm 0.4\times10^{-2}$, MetaCal $\approx -1.8\times10^{-2} \pm 0.7\times10^{-2}$. The pipeline takes a single $m$ (one σ) and $c_1, c_2$ per bin.

Where things live

  • Catalogues: CosmoHub. LE3 products: PIC WebDAV (wl-pipeline wiki, Data_Access). n(z) and VMPZ tile products: the internal DR1 SAS. Coverage, mixing matrices and n(z) for the candidate DR1 tile set: PIC (Tessore). Tile list: gitlab.euclid-sgs.uk/PF-LE3-WL/tile-lists. euclidlib PR #154 reads the SAS n(z) product.

In the pipeline behind the results shown here, the Cycle 2 universe (tr1_v2, SPT-3G) is built through blinded data vectors and is being re-materialized. The plan there: move to Cycle 3 when it ships; the LensMC R2 → R3 difference doubles as a PSF-only systematics test of the κ crosses.

Cail's working stance, not a group decision: low-level shear issues (c-bias, bleeding-edge mask, B-mode nulls) stay with OU-SHE and WL, noted, and acted on only where κ brings something in.
