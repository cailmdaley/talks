  • First — Fig. 1 of the Agora paper (Omori 2022): the components of the simulation, from left to right dark matter density, halos above $10^{14}\,M_\odot$, CMB lensing convergence, galaxy shear over galaxy convergence, tSZ (Compton $y$), kSZ, CIB at 353 GHz and radio sources.
  • Second — Fig. 6 of Ouellette et al. (2026, SPT-3G D1 CMB lensing × DES Y3 galaxy lensing): the mean κγ cross-spectrum measured on Agora against the truth (full sky, no noise). Columns are tomographic bins, rows are CMB lensing reconstructions; each panel gives the χ² of the ratio against one and the best-fit amplitude. The simulated shear has no shape noise; error bars are the standard deviation of the mean of 10 Agora patches.
