Shared pipeline

The SPT × Euclid paper crosses Euclid shear and galaxy density with SPT-3G lensing as a 6×2pt analysis. It shares the KP2-3 pipeline with the Planck + ACT 6×2pt paper, so the same data vectors exist for ACT DR6 and SPT-3G and their agreement is a test in itself.

Key-project leads

The coordinators listed on the KP2-3 Redmine wiki: Carlo Baccigalupi for KP2 (with Karim Benabed until his death), and Giulio Fabbian, Louis Legrand and Margherita Lembo for KP3.
