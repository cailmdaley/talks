Why it matters to KP2-3

These are the defaults a joint κ + 3×2pt analysis inherits. Two differ from ours: our n(z) model has a shift and a stretch per bin, and our production binning (15 log bands, ℓ = 100–3000) has about three bands above ℓ = 1500, the resolution problem the √ℓ switch addresses. Scope stays as decided: we build on the 3×2pt choices and act only where κ brings something in.
