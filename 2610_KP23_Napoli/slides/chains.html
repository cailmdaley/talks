TR1 v1.1 LensMC × SPT-3G · shifts from the 3×2pt+κ mean

What is plotted

Blinded (cmbx_dr1_a) posteriors on Euclid TR1 v1.1 LensMC × SPT-3G (the variant control switches to ACT DR6), shown as shifts $\Delta S_8$, $\Delta\Omega_\mathrm{m}$ from the posterior mean of the full 3×2pt+κ vector, so no blinded absolute value appears. Contours are 68 % and 95 %. Built up as κγ + κδ (κ-only), then + γδ, then + γγ + δδ. Sampler: nautilus nested sampling on the production Cobaya model, reweighted to the production Limber integration. 44 sampled parameters: five cosmological ($\ln 10^{10}A_s$, $\omega_c$, $\omega_b$, $h$, $n_s$), $\log T_\mathrm{AGN}$, NLA $A_\mathrm{IA}$ and $\eta_\mathrm{IA}$, per-bin source and lens n(z) shift and stretch, per-bin $m$ and linear bias, on the priors of the previous slide.

Numbers

SPT-3G: $\sigma(S_8)$ 0.056, 0.028, 0.012; κ+GL sits $\Delta S_8 = -0.059$ below the full mean and κ-only +0.015 above. ACT DR6: $\sigma(S_8)$ 0.069, 0.035, 0.013; κ+GL −0.009, κ-only +0.066. Nested shifts in units of $\sigma_n = \sqrt{|\sigma_\mathrm{sub}^2 - \sigma_\mathrm{ref}^2|}$ are in the chains fiber.

Caveats

  • The full vector fails goodness of fit at its best fit on v1.1 ($\chi^2$ 748/605), driven by δδ: autos high, distant crosses low, the v1.1 lens n(z) pattern. On Cycle 2 with the DR1 n(z) the lens n(z) stop narrowing, the γδ band-1 excess vanishes, and a δδ excess remains in the distant crosses.
  • The full-vector runs use a repartitioned proposal; validated on κ+GL, $S_8$ is exact and $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$ comes out about 8 % narrow, so the full contours' $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$ upper side may be short.
  • Exploratory runs outside lc; production chains on Cycle 2 (tr1_v2) go through lc next.
