  • v2: −0.6 ± 0.4 % ShapePipe

Cosmic shear and photo-z led by Klara Bertmann

  • $n(z)$ — not shown: the $n(z)$ for parameters comes from the photo-z group (Klara Bertmann, Bochum, who also leads the tomographic cosmic-shear paper).
  • Shear calibration — first release $m = -0.057 \pm 0.0047$ from image simulations. v2: Martin Kilbinger's ShapePipe metacalibration on Fabian Hervas-Peters' isolated-galaxy grid simulations, $m_1 = -0.0062 \pm 0.0038,$ $m_2 = -0.0057 \pm 0.0046,$ shear plus selection response; Shurui Lin's AnaCal, $m_1 = (+3.1 \pm 7.8)\times10^{-3},$ $m_2 = (+2.0 \pm 7.6)\times10^{-3},$ likely without the selection response, so not yet like for like.
  • Caveat — isolated galaxies only; blending added about −1.7 % in the first release. Multi-band image simulations are in progress.
  • Outlook — the tomographic cosmic-shear analysis, led by Klara Bertmann, targets a June 2027 release, with constraints expected to be competitive with Stage III and with Year-1 Stage IV.
