The TR1 results shown there, and what the week means for KP2-3

Cail Daley

CMBX KP2-3 telecon

22 October 2026

A report to the CMBX KP2-3 group after the joint OU-SHE / WLSWG meeting in Napoli, 12–16 October 2026. The first part repeats the KP2-3 results shown there, on TR1 v1.1 and marked preliminary until this group has discussed them, plus one analysis made during the week: the extinction template and the cosmic infrared background. The second part collects what from the week bears on KP2-3. The full Napoli talk, on behalf of the whole CMBX SWG, is at cailmdaley.github.io/talks/2610_Napoli_CMBX.
