Summary

  1. TR1 × SPT-3G: blinded 5×2pt vector and first posteriors
  2. SPT-3G κ crosses are tighter than ACT DR6 at small scales
  3. Euclid DR2 will overlap well with SPT-3G
  4. Open: covariance with SPT mocks, κκ, foregrounds, inference
  5. To participate: external-collaborator status, by June 2027

Cail Daley · CEA Paris-Saclay

  • TR1 × SPT-3G — the pipeline is validated on GLASS mocks and blinded as in the Euclid 3×2pt analysis; the posteriors use the analytic Gaussian + SSC covariance.
  • Small scales — SPT-3G's error bars in the κ crosses are smaller than ACT DR6's from $\ell \approx 220$ on TR1, ×2.2 at $\ell \approx 2700;$ projected to DR1 the two are on par to $\ell \approx 1700$ and SPT-3G is ×1.6 tighter at $\ell \approx 2700$.
  • DR2 — its southern area will overlap SPT-3G far more than DR1 does.
  • Open — the covariance plan needs SPT's mock reconstructions with their input κ; the scope (cosmologies, κκ, foreground mitigation, inference) is open.
  • Participation — Euclid external-collaborator status, for people who will commit time before the DR1 papers in June 2027.
