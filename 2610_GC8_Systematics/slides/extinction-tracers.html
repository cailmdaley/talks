Correlating extinction with Euclid tracers

$\displaystyle\rho_\ell^{X,\mathrm{ext}}=\frac{C_\ell^{X\,\mathrm{ext}}}{\sqrt{C_\ell^{XX,\mathrm{th}}\,C_\ell^{\mathrm{ext\,ext}}}},\qquad \bar\rho_{X,\mathrm{ext}}:\ \text{GLS mean over } 100\lt\ell\lt3000$

  • $\bar\rho_{X,\mathrm{ext}}$ — each tracer’s correlation coefficient with the extinction map: the cross normalized by the tracer’s theory signal and the template’s measured auto, GLS-averaged over $100\lt\ell\lt3000$.
  • Navy — δ with visibility correction; γE and κ carry no visibility weights. Nearly all positive; κ cannot be obscured by dust, so this is LSS in the map.
  • Cycle 2 (next step) — every tracer correlates at ρ̄ ≈ 0.05–0.17.
  • Orange (last step) — δ before visibility correction. In Cycle 2 the correction moves ρ̄ by ≲ 1σ (δ2: 0.096 → 0.086); toggle to Cycle 1, where it removes most of it (δ4: 0.072 → 0.015, δ6: 0.069 → 0.016).
How $\bar\rho$ is computed

All points compress the same range, $100\lt\ell\lt3000$, into a GLS constant-ρ mean with its error, using the full Gaussian cross blocks divided by the normalization outer product. $C_\ell^{XX,\mathrm{th}}$ is the release-specific fiducial tracer signal model (no shot, shape or reconstruction noise), averaged uniformly over the science bins; $C_\ell^{\mathrm{ext\,ext}}$ is the measured template auto. This model-normalized estimate avoids clipping negative noise-subtracted shear autos; it is not bounded to [−1, 1] in a noisy realization, and the denominator’s uncertainty is not propagated. The GLS mean is a descriptive compression, not a fitted physical model.

What the comparison does and does not show

There is no monotonic-redshift claim. Cycle-1 low-z shear means are mostly within one or two Gaussian errors of zero; high-z shear and κ carry the clearer support. Extinction cannot directly obscure a CMB-lensing reconstruction; the joint sign and the high-z shear support the LSS interpretation (the CIB route, backup, makes it quantitative). This does not rule out real Galactic selection effects or CMB reconstruction foreground response. Only the cycle toggle is active: matched MetaCal/ACT ρ normalizations have not been saved.
