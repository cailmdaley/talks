DR1-KP-CMBX-2-3: Simulations and Covariance

Luchina, Baccigalupi, Calabrese, Carbone, Haridasu, Vielzeuf

  • Following the draft from pre-launch Vielzeuf+: we begin by simulating logNormal, full-sky, mocks of Euclid 3×2 fields correlated with KCMB; marginal distribution calibrated through the DEMNUni-COV simulations. Also possible to constrain logNormal 3×2 fields, anchored to gaussian simulations of KCMB from CMB-collaborations. (3×2) have magnification bias and intrinsic alignment, together with Poisson sampling of galaxies. Concerning KCMB it is possible to either perform lensing reconstruction (if using the fully logNormal mocks) or use the reconstructed KCMB fields from CMB-collaborations (matching their sims). The final covariance is then obtained by computing spectra (with NaMaster) for each simulated sky, and for each field combination on the appropriate footprint (DR1 / DR1×CMB / CMB). Currently investigating Bootstrap resampling of mock skies to increase the sample size.
  • Current results: 400 realizations constrained on ACT gaussian KCMB simulations & 400 fully logNormal realizations with a Planck-like configuration for lensing reconstruction. Soon to be added: constraint realizations on Planck simulations.
  • Parameters: nside=4096, [4.1, 4.1, 4.2, 4.3, 4.4, 4.6] gal/arcmin2 , (23,42) μK noise levels in (T,P) and 7’ beam for Planck-like CMB. Output binned in 20 log-spaced ℓ bins in the range [10, 3000].
