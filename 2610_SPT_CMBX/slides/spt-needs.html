What the analysis needs from SPT

  • First: mock lensing reconstructions with their input κ
  • Lensing noise curves: N0, N1
  • Mean fields

The mocks feed the mock-based covariance; the noise curves and mean fields, the theory and the noise model.

Why the mocks come first

The Euclid side holds the SPT-3G Main winter lensing maps for each estimator and one simulated reconstruction per estimator. With the input κ and the matched reconstruction for each simulation, the SPT block of the mock-based covariance can be built on the constrained route of the covariance slides, and input minus reconstruction gives noise realizations that carry the real reconstruction's structure, for the GLASS validation mocks too. Until then the CMB-lensing noise in the mocks and the covariance is a stopgap: the observed κκ minus theory. A transfer of about 500 input + reconstruction pairs is tracked in Euclid Redmine issue #35882.

Also useful

  • N0 (and the RDN0 recipe for the data) and N1, for each estimator variant used.
  • The mean field, or confirmation of how the released maps and simulations are mean-field subtracted.
  • Masks, the normalisation convention of the released κ, any Monte-Carlo correction, transfer function and L-range used.
  • The simulations' fiducial cosmology, realization count and indexing.
