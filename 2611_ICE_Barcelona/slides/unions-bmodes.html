B-modes inform the scale cuts: CCD and large scales go

  • all scales: suggestive of a repeating CCD-scale additive bias
  • $\xi_\pm^B,$ $C_\ell^{BB}$ and $B_n$ weight scales differently: all three must pass
  • Paper II — B-mode validation (Daley et al. 2026): the gatekeeper for the catalog version, and with PSF and baryon criteria, for the scale cuts.
  • Panels — COSEBI $B_n/\sigma_n$; shaded modes $n \gt 6$, the cosmological information sits at $n \le 6$. Left: over all scales (1–250′) COSEBIs fail, with the oscillating pattern Asgari et al. (2019) associated with a repeating additive shear bias at the CCD scale (a MegaCam CCD is 6.4′ × 14.2′). Real- and harmonic-space COSEBIs agree, so it is the filter weighting, not the basis.
  • Right — with the fiducial cuts, $[12, 83]'$ and $\ell = 300$–$1600,$ the B-modes are consistent with zero; all PTEs above 0.18.
  • Three statistics — over the full range $\xi_\pm^B$ and $C_\ell^{BB}$ pass while COSEBIs fail; each weights scales differently, so all three are required to pass. The origin was not decisively identified.
