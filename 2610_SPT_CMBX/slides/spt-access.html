Taking part needs Euclid external-collaborator status

  • For any Euclid product: data vectors, covariances, mocks, chains
  • In place: a request per person, a NERSC mirror of the products
  • A high bar: each person shown to be critical to the project
  • So: people ready to commit time before June 2027

Euclid data products reach people outside the Euclid Consortium only through external-collaborator status, which Euclid grants person by person. That covers everything derived from Euclid data, not only the catalogues: data vectors, covariances, mocks and chains. Anyone running chains needs it as much as whoever touches the raw data.

The structure is ready. The request goes through the CMBX working group, one entry per person, and once approved the Euclid-side products are mirrored at NERSC (project m5099), readable only by people with access, since the European cluster where they are made is not usable from the US.

Euclid asks that each person be shown to be critical to the project, so the request should list the people who will put real time into the paper before the DR1 papers in June 2027. After this call I'll follow up with everyone on the original sign-on list.
