Building pipelines on simulations is not new

  • SPT: simple simulations to calibrate CMB lensing measurements
  • DES × SPT: simulations with correlated foregrounds
  • UNIONS: image simulations to calibrate shear
  • Euclid × SPT-3G: digital twins from catalogs to cosmology

Cosmology will benefit greatly from open-source, modular simulation frameworks.

  • From simple to complex — the same practice across Cail's work, with simulations that carry more of the instrument and the sky each time.
  • The thesis — keep pushing on open-source, modular simulation and forward-modelling frameworks.
