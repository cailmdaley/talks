Summary

  1. Mocks validate pipelines and characterize systematics; same techniques give forward models for inference
  2. Euclid × SPT-3G: validated on GLASS mocks, blinded on TR1
  3. UNIONS: 191 million galaxies over 5,200 deg²; cosmic shear and 3×2pt with SPHEREx in summer 2027
  4. Mocks give agents a verifiable environment: agents check their own work, scientists specify requirements

Cail Daley · CosmoStat, CEA Paris-Saclay

  • Mocks and forward models — mocks that reproduce a survey's footprint, noise, redshifts and systematics check each step of an analysis; made fast and faithful enough, they serve explicit (field-level) and implicit (simulation-based) inference.
  • Euclid × SPT-3G — the blinded TR1 analysis, its GLASS mocks and blinding; the κ legs of SPT-3G and ACT DR6 differ by 0.07–0.08 in $S_8$ while the full vectors, dominated by the shared galaxy spectra, agree.
  • UNIONS — the UNIONS-3500 release (Papers I–IV), the v2 catalog and its mock checks, Klara Bertmann's tomographic cosmic shear and the 3×2pt with SPHEREx lenses led by Calum Murray and Cail Daley.
  • Agents — agents check their own work against mocks whose answer is known, while scientists set the requirements. Public data and agent-assisted analysis may change how collaborations divide their work; this is a question, not a forecast.
