Timeline to the Euclid DR1 release

  • On “now” — what is in hand: the complete TR1 product suite, the DR1 clustering catalogues (the MER/PHZ R2 sample) and the blinded TR1 chains of the posteriors slide.
  • Top lane — Euclid data products, as presented at Napoli; a bar with a soft edge has no fixed date at that end.
  • Bottom lane — this paper's plan: DR1 chains on blinded data from December, Euclid internal review in April–May, submission on the public-release day.
  • Steps — November–January (shear catalogue, chains starting, scale cuts), then February–March (final products, unblinding), then April–June (review, release, submission).
Euclid data products

The DR1 shear catalogues (the R3 reprocessing, with a much smaller PSF-size bias than R2) reach the whole Euclid Consortium during November, at the earliest at the end of October, hence the soft bar; like every DR1 shear product they are blinded until the end of March. DR1-Foundation is released on 12 November 2026. The n(z) are close to final at the end of November (not drawn: the final n(z) follow). The final selection, the final multiplicative shear bias $m$ from SHE-CAL (first run in November) and the final n(z) arrive in February, the n(z) at the end of February when the products freeze; the bar fades out early in March for that reason. The two-point measurements are unblinded by the end of March, and the public DR1 release is in mid-June 2027.

The analysis plan

The DR1 chains start as soon as the n(z) are close to final, around the start of December, and run on the blinded DR1 data, ramping up as the products firm up until they converge on the final products in February and March. The scale cuts are finalized over December and January, with the 3×2pt group's. The SPT products listed in backup are needed for the covariance, so they come before the final chains.

Papers go into the Euclid Consortium's internal (ECP) review in early April, after unblinding at the end of March, and the roughly two months of review (fast style screening, about two weeks of author revisions, a three-week sign-off window) end in early June. The dashed stretch to the June release is buffer, and the papers are submitted on release day. They go in even if numbers may still shift: the DR1-Foundation strong-lensing papers were written ahead with TBD numbers and needed only a final update.
