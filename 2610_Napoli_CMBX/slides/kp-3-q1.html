KP2-3: Q1-matched DR1 clustering × ACT lensing

Figure mirrored from the contributions deck (12 Oct export, Margherita Lembo's KP2-3 data-vectors slide). Q1 EDFS galaxies with $p_{\rm star} \lt 0.05$, one combined bin, crossed with ACT DR6 lensing: CMB lensing × galaxy density (left) and the galaxy auto-spectrum (right), NaMaster covariance. Three selections: the Q1 redshift bins on the DR1 EDFS footprint, the same with the effective-coverage weighting, and the Q1 EDFS catalogue; theory for the Q1-zbins (solid) and Q1 (dotted) redshift distributions. Grey bands are outside the scale cuts. The slide title is ours, not the contributor's: "Q1-matched DR1" is the DR1 EDFS catalogue cut to the Q1 footprint and redshift bins, compared with Q1 itself. Credit (Giulio Fabbian, 13 Oct): the work of Luca Pagano and Giulio Fabbian, with Margherita Lembo, who computed the spectra; the catalogue cross-match and redshift distributions are Luca and Giulio's. The Q1 analysis itself is Fabbian, Legrand, Lembo, Pagano et al., "Euclid Quick Data Release (Q1): Clustering of photometrically selected galaxies and their cross-correlation with cosmic microwave background lensing" (internal draft 29 Sep 2026, Euclid PUB 741787): tomographic clustering × Planck and ACT lensing over ~60 deg², detected at more than 30σ, through to a cosmological analysis, in which adding the CMB-lensing cross tightens constraints by up to 30 % over clustering alone.
