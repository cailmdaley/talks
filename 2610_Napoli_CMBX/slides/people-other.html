Key-project leads. This talk focuses on the activities that use Euclid weak lensing.

The DR1 key projects of the CMBX working group and their leads, with each science topic as listed in the CMBX splinter introduction in Barcelona: KP1-2, CMB lensing and ISW cross-correlation with Euclid observables (Carlo Baccigalupi); KP3, cosmology with combination of Euclid photometric probes and CMB lensing (Giulio Fabbian, Louis Legrand, Margherita Lembo); KP4, hot gas in the cosmic web via tSZ cross-correlation with GC, WL and clusters (Nabila Aghanim, Laura Salvati); KP5, peculiar velocities from CMB-Euclid cross-correlation (Carlos Hernández‑Monteagudo). Leads as listed by Marina Migliaccio (14 Oct). The additional probes are cross-correlations with AGNs, voids, the FIR CIB and clusters. For the CIB, Han et al. (2026, arXiv:2607.19290, "Euclid: Inferring star-formation history via cross-correlations of photometric clustering and shear with the cosmic infrared background") forecast that Euclid photometric clustering and shear crossed with Planck CIB tighten the bias-weighted star-formation-rate density ⟨b ρ_SFRD⟩ by a factor of about 2 in every redshift bin, reach deeper in redshift, and cut the HOD-parameter uncertainties by about 2.5.

The teams are credited beside their work: the KP2-3 team on the next slide, the KP1 extended-forecast group on the KP1 slides, the KP4 WL × y team on the KP4 slide.
