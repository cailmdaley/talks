Computing changed astronomy in layers

  • the problems we could take on
  • simulations
  • tests and software engineering practices
  • agentic research changes the constraints again
  • expertise goes into scientific environments that improve over time
  • Argument — computing transformed astronomy layer by layer: first which problems could be tackled at all, then simulations, then the software engineering practices the field adopted around them, tests among them. Agentic research redefines the constraints again, and the engineering practices that go with it are only beginning to appear.
  • Environments — things will scale fast. Human expertise is best spent building scientific environments that improve over time (simulations, tests, pipelines an agent can check itself against) rather than answering every question of one analysis by hand. The engineering practices for this are only starting to emerge.
