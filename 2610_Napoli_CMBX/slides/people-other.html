The DR1 key projects of the CMBX working group, each with its science topic as listed in the CMBX splinter introduction in Barcelona: KP1-2, CMB ISW and lensing cross-correlation with Euclid observables; KP4, hot gas in the cosmic web via tSZ cross-correlation with GC, WL and clusters; KP5, peculiar velocities from CMB-Euclid cross-correlation. The additional probes are cross-correlations with AGNs, voids, the FIR CIB and clusters. For the CIB, Han et al. (2026, arXiv:2607.19290, "Euclid: Inferring star-formation history via cross-correlations of photometric clustering and shear with the cosmic infrared background") forecast that Euclid photometric clustering and shear crossed with Planck CIB tighten the bias-weighted star-formation-rate density ⟨b ρ_SFRD⟩ by a factor of about 2 in every redshift bin, reach deeper in redshift, and cut the HOD-parameter uncertainties by about 2.5. KP2-3, cosmology with the combination of Euclid photometric probes and CMB lensing, is the next slide. Someone working on two key projects appears in both.

KP1-2 is led by Carlo Baccigalupi. Guglielmo Frittoli, Fabio Finelli and Angelo Ferrari carried the pre-launch KP1 extended forecasts shown later in the talk (presented by Guglielmo Frittoli at the CMBX general telecon of 1 October 2026). KP4 is led by Nabila Aghanim and Laura Salvati; Giulio Fabbian and Emma Ayçoberry lead its WL × y (tSZ × shear) analysis; with the team shown on the title slide of the KP4 update in Barcelona. Melita Carbone, Carlo Baccigalupi and Matteo Calabrese supervise Davide Luchina's work on the SZ covariances (added at Melita Carbone's request, 13 Oct).
