Open questions

So far the work has gone into the data vectors, the pipeline and its validation. What the paper should constrain has not been thought through. Chains, inference and extensions are where collaboration would help this analysis most. The data vector is the full Euclid DR1 3×2pt plus the CMB-lensing crosses, so this work is DR1-precision Euclid cosmology.

The CMB-lensing auto-spectrum is not in the 5×2pt vector shown in this talk. Adding it raises the question of how: SPT's κκ bandpowers in the same vector, with their covariance against the crosses, or the SPT lensing likelihood run alongside directly.

Foregrounds in the CMB temperature (tSZ, CIB, radio and dusty sources) bias κ × galaxy crosses as well as κκ, through correlations with the Euclid tracers. The map used is profile-hardened, but how much residual bias reaches these crosses, and what mitigation is needed at DR1 precision, is something I haven't thought deeply about yet; others have studied it for κκ and for crosses.

Taking part

Euclid data products reach people outside the Euclid Consortium only through external-collaborator status, which Euclid grants person by person. That covers everything derived from Euclid data, not only the catalogues: data vectors, covariances, mocks and chains. Anyone running chains needs it as much as whoever touches the raw data.

The structure is nearly ready. The request goes through the CMBX working group as one batch form for everyone, being finalized now, and once approved the Euclid-side products will be mirrored at NERSC (project m5099), readable only by people with access, since the European cluster where they are made is not usable from the US.

Euclid asks that each person be shown to be critical to the project, so the request should list the people who will put real time into the paper before the DR1 papers in June 2027. After this call I'll follow up with everyone on the original sign-on list.
