Some signs of systematic contamination

Contamination metric $$X_\ell^f = \ell \, \frac{C_\ell^{\kappa S} C_\ell^{fS}}{C_\ell^{SS}}$$

$f$ = galaxy field, $S$ = systematic map

LensMC and Metacal × ACT DR6 and SPT-3G GMV

For each systematic map S and galaxy field f, the contamination metric X_ℓ^f = ℓ C_ℓ^{κS} C_ℓ^{fS} / C_ℓ^{SS} estimates how much of the galaxy–CMB-lensing cross-spectrum the systematic could imprint. Left column: galaxy density; right column: shear, for LensMC and Metacal crossed with ACT DR6 and SPT-3G GMV.
