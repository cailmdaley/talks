  3. CLOE: testing with CMBX products; debiased κκ bandpowers?
  4. Emulators: Ivan Sladoljev at the 29 Oct call
  5. Results: anything to change before these go further?

Cail Daley · CEA Paris-Saclay

The questions the Napoli week puts in front of KP2-3, in slide order: how to set scale cuts for κ × γ and κ × δ, given the methods 3×2pt is weighing; where the priors, n(z) model and binning of the chains shown here differ from the 3×2pt proposal, and whether shift + stretch is enough for κ × δ at DR1 n(z) errors; testing CLOE with CMBX data products, and asking the CMB-lensing auto-spectrum groups for debiased κκ bandpowers; emulators, with Ivan Sladoljev's talk proposed for the 29 Oct call. The last is why the results are here: they are marked preliminary until this group has discussed them.

Also from the week: Porredon (GC) invited a presentation of the template crosses rerun on the Cycle 2 VMPZ products at the GC-8 call; the Tiger Team's B-mode and VMPZ nulls have no calibrated error bars, which the mock-calibrated PTEs of the template nulls shown here could offer. Calum Murray (day 5) presented a Euclid × SPHEREx lens sample at z < 0.6, ~5.2M galaxies with σNMAD 0.0087 against Euclid PHZ's 0.0354 on the same spec-z matches, with a first galaxy × Planck CMB-lensing cross in six shells; the catalogues are offered for sharing.
