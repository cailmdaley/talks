  • $n(z)$ — not shown: the $n(z)$ for parameters comes from the photo-z group (Klara Bertmann, Bochum, who also leads the tomographic cosmic-shear paper).
  • Figure — the non-tomographic $\xi_\pm$ of the calibrated v2.0.0 sample on all 22,901 run-1 tiles (191.0 million galaxies over 5223 deg², every galaxy, no tomographic selection), concealed, with the first release's error bars and v2's: v2's are 0.67× ($\xi_+$) and 0.64× ($\xi_-$) inside 12–83′, both from OneCovariance (Gaussian, non-Gaussian and super-sample terms).
  • Shear calibration — first release $m = -0.057 \pm 0.0047$ from image simulations. v2 ShapePipe: image simulations rerun on Fir in November (150 interior tiles, isolated galaxies on a grid, processed by ShapePipe as the data are, v2 weights, tile jackknife), $m_1 = (+0.3 \pm 1.5)\times10^{-3},$ $m_2 = (-0.9 \pm 1.7)\times10^{-3};$ the slide quotes the mean of the two components, $(-0.3 \pm 1.1)\times10^{-3},$ with their errors taken as independent. The October grids gave $m_1 = -0.0062 \pm 0.0038,$ $m_2 = -0.0057 \pm 0.0046.$ Shurui Lin's AnaCal, $m_1 = (+3.1 \pm 7.8)\times10^{-3},$ $m_2 = (+2.0 \pm 7.6)\times10^{-3},$ mean $(+2.6 \pm 5.4)\times10^{-3}$ on the slide, likely without the selection response, so not yet like for like.
  • Caveat — isolated galaxies only; blending added about −1.7 % in the first release. Multi-band image simulations are in progress.
  • Outlook — the tomographic cosmic-shear analysis, led by Klara Bertmann, targets a June 2027 release, with constraints expected to be competitive with Stage III and with Year-1 Stage IV.
