Numbers

Blinded $S_8$ and $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$, mean ± σ. SPT-3G: κ-only $S_8 = 0.823 \pm 0.052$, $\Omega_\mathrm{m} = 0.53 \pm 0.19$; κ+GL $S_8 = 0.749 \pm 0.029$, $\Omega_\mathrm{m} = 0.43 \pm 0.12$. ACT DR6: κ-only $0.905 \pm 0.058$, $0.75 \pm 0.24$; κ+GL $0.818 \pm 0.031$, $0.53 \pm 0.17$. With κ alone SPT-3G is about 10 % tighter in $S_8$ than ACT DR6 on the TR1 overlap, despite the smaller overlap (130 against 222 deg² of TR1, footprint slide).

What is plotted

Production chains on Euclid TR1 Cycle 2 (official tomographic bins) LensMC shear and galaxy density, crossed with the SPT-3G Main bias- and profile-hardened GMV reconstruction or the ACT DR6 baseline map. Sampler: nautilus nested sampling on the production Cobaya model, reweighted to the production Limber integration. Covariance: theory-fiducial Gaussian plus super-sample covariance. Scale cut: linear galaxy bias to $k_\mathrm{max} = 0.2\,h$/Mpc for δδ and 0.15 $h$/Mpc for γδ and κδ (arXiv:2203.12439's method). 44 sampled parameters: five cosmological ($\ln 10^{10}A_s$, $\omega_c$, $\omega_b$, $h$, $n_s$), $\log T_\mathrm{AGN}$, NLA $A_\mathrm{IA}$ and $\eta_\mathrm{IA}$, per-bin source and lens n(z) shift and stretch, per-bin $m$ and linear bias.

Why σ is optimistic

The n(z) shift priors (σ 0.013–0.028) come from the Euclid 3×2pt proposal and are not calibrated for TR1; the magnification slopes are fixed; $m_\nu$ is fixed; baryons enter only through $T_\mathrm{AGN}$. The covariance has no connected non-Gaussian term yet (the FLASK covariance earlier in the talk is meant to provide it); on GLASS mocks in this configuration the parameter scatter is ~1.25× the Fisher σ. For scale: DES Y3 × SPT/Planck 6×2pt gives σ($S_8$) = 0.012 on nearly 5000 deg² (arXiv:2206.10824); TR1's weak-lensing area is a few hundred deg², at several times DES's source density.
