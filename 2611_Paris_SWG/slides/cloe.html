CLOE vs dr1_cmbx

The same TR1 chains with two exact likelihoods

  • The question — does replacing the dr1_cmbx exact likelihood (eDR1like with CCL theory) by CLOE change the CMBX posteriors, their bias, or their cost?
  • Short answer — no parameter moves by more than 0.054σ, the bias against a mock's truth is the same, and CLOE is cheaper per evaluation. This is an exploratory comparison, not a key-project product.
