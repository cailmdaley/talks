UNIONS covers the northern sky that Stage III lensing left out

  • different telescopes and different systematics
  • DESI and SPHEREx redshifts on the same sky
  • Globe — the animation opens on the southern Stage III lensing footprints (DES, KiDS, HSC) with Euclid DR1 in violet, travels north to UNIONS, circles the cap once to show its southern, eastern, northern and western edges, and stops on UNIONS and Euclid DR1. Deep orange is UNIONS-3500, the first shear release; light orange is what the v2 catalog adds; the orange line is the final UNIONS footprint.
  • Overlap — small: HSC's HectoMap field (about 100 deg² at Dec +42.5° to +44.5°) lies inside UNIONS, and a few DR6 tiles fall inside DES (Stripe 82, CFHTLS-W1).
  • Significance — a cosmic-shear measurement with its own instrument and its own sky is an independent check on the Stage III results, and the northern sky is where DESI and SPHEREx spectroscopy overlap it.

Drawn

DES: the DES wide-survey envelope (public MOC, 5,155 deg²). KiDS: the KiDS-N/S science-quality mask (public MOC, 891 deg²). HSC: the six HSC Y3 shear fields, digitised from Li et al. 2023 Fig. 1 (572 deg² gross). These three come from the Survey Footprint Explorer and the ADE-talk footprint set. UNIONS-3500: the first-release shear footprint mask (2,894 deg²). UNIONS v2: the 22,901 DR6 tiles in the v2 shape catalog, drawn as whole half-degree tiles before star masks (the masked catalog covers 5,223 deg²). Final footprint: all 23,114 DR6 tiles (about 5,800 deg²), from the public production status page. Euclid DR1: the observed DR1 VIS footprint, recovered from the ADE-talk globe video because the DR1 MOC is internal; its edges are good to a few tenths of a degree, and it covers about 2,550 deg² against the MOC's 2,346 deg². Sky: Gaia EDR3 colour map (ESA/Gaia/DPAC). Generator: make_globe.py in this deck.
