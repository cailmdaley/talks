What has to pass before we unblind?

  • Data vectors: null tests and mock recovery (workflow sheet)
  • Chains: goodness of fit and nuisance posteriors inside priors
  • Consistency: subsets of the data agree given each other
  • Robustness: scale cuts and model variants move results ≪ σ
  • All analysis choices frozen and written down first
  • Not a criterion: agreement with Planck or public κκ
  • Data vectors — the workflow sheet of Margherita Lembo and Louis Legrand (17 November) lists the tests per analysis step: sample statistics against 3×2pt, mask and mode coupling on simulations, map null tests (shear B-modes × κ, rotated shear × κ, lensing curl × galaxies and shear, randoms × κ, visibility templates), spectra against Flagship-2 and between estimators and CMB experiments, systematics deprojection. Its last block, "Final runs / unblinding", has one line so far: criteria defined in advance, goodness of fit, subset consistency.
  • Chains — a blinded chain can still be judged: the χ² of the best fit against the covariance, and whether nuisance parameters (shear and redshift calibration, galaxy bias, intrinsic alignments) sit inside their priors and away from the edges. A shift in cosmology leaves both meaningful.
  • Consistency — beyond comparing two posteriors: given the 3×2pt part of the vector, is the κ-cross part where the model predicts it, and the same for single redshift bins or probes left out. One shift on every spectrum keeps these tests valid while blinded.
  • Robustness — parameter shifts between the baseline and its variants (scale cuts, nonlinear bias, baryons, with and without κκ) can be read while blinded, since the hidden shift cancels in a difference.
  • Frozen — scale cuts, priors, model and the list of tests with their thresholds are fixed and written down before opening; any change afterwards is reported as such.
  • Not a criterion — my view: agreement with Planck or with the published κκ is a result, so it cannot be a condition for opening. Consistency between datasets is a condition for combining them, which is a separate decision.
How many tests may fail

With hundreds of correlated tests some fail by chance. My proposal on 17 November: run every test script on the mock ensemble and read the number of failures on data against that distribution.

Two published examples

ACT DR6 lensing (Qu et al., arXiv:2304.05202, their blinding section) required before unblinding: all baseline analysis choices frozen; no single null-test PTE outside 0.001–0.999; the distribution of PTEs consistent with uniform; the number of tests outside 0.01–0.99 consistent with random fluctuations; and the summed χ² of several types of tests within 2σ of the simulation distributions.

SPT-3G D1 lensing (Omori, Wu et al., arXiv:2608.31136) unblinded in two steps: bandpowers against theory after the simulation-mean, curl, unlensed and multipole-range tests passed, then cosmological parameters after the alternate-cosmology and Agora unbiasedness tests; before fully unblinding they also checked for tension with the primary CMB and BAO, because combining requires consistency. Both papers list the changes made after unblinding.

Who decides

For the primary probes, unblinding is requested by the key-project leads and decided by the Science Coordination Group. Who decides for CMBX is open.
