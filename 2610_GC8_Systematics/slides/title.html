Systematics-template cross-correlations

TR1 template nulls, and the CIB in dust maps

Cail Daley

Euclid GC-8 call

28 October 2026

An update for the galaxy-clustering group on validating the TR1 tracers with cross-correlations against the VMPZ systematics templates. The template crosses were made for the CMBX 3×2pt × CMB-lensing analysis (KP2-3), which needs to know whether any survey property contaminates its data vector; the galaxy-density rows are the ones of most interest here. Everything shown is TR1 v1.1, the Cycle 1 selection and templates, unless a slide says otherwise. Template × data crosses are nulls: their expectation is zero whatever the cosmology, so they are shown as measured, with no blinding.

Earlier versions of these slides were shown at the OU-SHE × WLSWG meeting in Napoli (deck) and at the CMBX KP2-3 telecon of 22 October (deck).
