Shear passes every template; galaxy density does not

  • Shear PTEs look okay: every template, both shear methods
  • κ: clean against every template
  • Galaxy density: extinction (its CIB), and depth in δ1

PTE statistics are not right yet: many correlated tests, not yet calibrated on mocks.

Each cell is a null test: one Euclid tracer (lens bin δ, source bin γ, the rows) or the CMB lensing map κ, crossed with one VMPZ systematics template (the columns). The number is the χ² probability to exceed over 15 bands, $100 \lt \ell \lt 3000$, with the full Gaussian covariance; κ uses the diagonal only. Values below $10^{-6}$ are written as such. These template crosses are blinding-independent. The variant control switches the shear method (γ rows) and the CMB map (κ row); the δ rows are the same in every variant.

Shear passes against every template, for both shear methods: across all 72 shear tests (six templates, six source bins, two methods) the lowest PTE is 0.03, about what the smallest of 72 independent tests gives. κ passes for both maps (lowest 0.17). Galaxy density has two signatures. Extinction fails across the lens bins (PTE below $10^{-6}$ in lens bins 4–6, χ² 70–80 over 15), but its sign is positive in every tracer, the opposite of dust obscuration, and it reaches high-z shear and κ too, with both shear methods and both CMB maps. The Planck E(B−V) map behind the template is built from far-infrared dust emission, and the cosmic infrared background, the integrated emission of dusty star-forming galaxies at z ≈ 1–2, leaks into it; those galaxies trace the same large-scale structure we measure. So it is structure in a foreground map, not contamination of ours. Exposures and noise fail in lens bin 1 (PTE below $10^{-6}$, χ² 87 and 72 over 15), mirror images of each other because pixel noise falls as exposure$^{-1/2}$: one depth dependence of the faintest lens sample, counted once. PSF, zodiacal light and stars are clean apart from single cells near 0.01.

The PTEs themselves are not yet trustworthy as significances: with about a hundred template crosses per variant, correlated with one another through shared tracers and templates, and an analytic covariance not yet checked against the scatter of mocks, the distribution of these numbers under the null is not known. The plan is to run the same null tests on the GLASS mocks and calibrate them, as for the UNIONS B-mode null tests. Read the dark cells as clear detections and the 0.01–0.1 cells as unknown.

Detected in a cross does not mean large in the data vector: the contamination slide puts each template's implied contamination in units of the data's own error bars, $\sigma_{ab}$.
