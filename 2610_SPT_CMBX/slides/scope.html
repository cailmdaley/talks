The scope of the paper is still open

  • Which cosmologies, which extensions?
  • Chains and inference: where I'd most like to collaborate
  • κκ: in the vector, or as its own likelihood?
  • CMB foreground mitigation in the crosses: not yet studied

So far the work has gone into the data vectors, the pipeline and its validation. What the paper should constrain has not been thought through: ΛCDM $S_8$ and $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$ are the baseline of the results slide, and which extensions to run, with which external data, is open. Chains, inference and extensions are where collaboration would help this analysis most.

The CMB-lensing auto-spectrum is not in the 5×2pt vector shown here. Adding it raises the question of how: SPT's κκ bandpowers in the same vector, with their covariance against the crosses, or the SPT lensing likelihood run alongside directly.

Foregrounds in the CMB temperature (tSZ, CIB, radio and dusty sources) bias κ × galaxy crosses as well as κκ, through correlations with the Euclid tracers. The map used is profile-hardened, but how much residual bias reaches these crosses, and what mitigation is needed at DR1 precision, has not yet been studied.
