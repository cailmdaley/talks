Covariance of the 651 fitted bandpowers

  • eventually moving to N‑body‑calibrated FLASK mock-based covariance

Vielzeuf et al., in prep.

  • The matrix — the correlation matrix of the 651 bandpowers inside the scale cut, in blocks by probe family: δκ (22 bands), γκ (90), δδ (92), δγ (132), γγ (315); within a block the bands run by tracer pair and then by $\ell$.
  • Two halves — below the diagonal the Gaussian covariance alone, above it Gaussian plus super-sample covariance. The colour scale is clipped at ±0.20.
  • Features — the super-sample term correlates the shear bands with each other across bins and scales (the warm upper-right block); the Gaussian part is the thin off-diagonal stripes between spectra that share a tracer.
