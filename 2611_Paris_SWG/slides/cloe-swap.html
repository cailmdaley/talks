Same chain with two likelihoods

  • Left — what the two chains share: data vector, covariance, priors, scale cuts and sampler settings.
  • Right — the swapped box, piece by piece. dr1_cmbx: eDR1like with CCL theory (CCL background and Limber projection, CAMB HMCode-2020 $P(k)$), built and evaluated as a Cobaya model. CLOE in JAX: CLOElib v0.11's background, tracers and Limber projection compiled in JAX, the Theory WG's HMCode-2020 emulator for $P(k)$, and a Gaussian $\chi^2$; neither Cobaya nor CCL is installed in the environment its chains ran in.
  • Data — two configurations: the GLASS mock (seed 3004) and blinded TR1 × SPT-3G.
  • Sampler — nautilus nested sampling with 2000 live points, each likelihood sampled directly. The Cobaya likelihood is called one point per MPI worker (447 workers); CLOE in JAX is called on 1000 points at a time.
Inputs

The production Gaussian + SSC covariance and the DR1-document nuisance priors; 37 sampled parameters on the mock and 38 on data ($T_\mathrm{AGN}$ also sampled). The GLASS mocks use dark-matter-only HMCode-2020, the data configuration HMCode-2020 with feedback; on the mock, CLOE in JAX reaches dark-matter-only $P(k)$ through the emulator and a correction table (notes of the JAX slide).

What is not independent

The priors and scale cuts of CLOE in JAX were transcribed from the Cobaya configuration (they agree to $10^{-12}$), and both read the same data-vector file and covariance. The theory code, the background, $P(k)$, projection and likelihood, shares nothing between the two.
