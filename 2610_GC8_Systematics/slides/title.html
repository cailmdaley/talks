Systematics-template cross-correlations

TR1 template nulls, and the CIB in dust maps

Cail Daley

Euclid GC-8 call

28 October 2026

An update for the galaxy-clustering group on validating the TR1 tracers with cross-correlations against the VMPZ systematics templates. The template crosses were made for the CMBX 3×2pt $\times$ CMB-lensing analysis (KP2-3), which needs to know whether any survey property contaminates its data vector; the galaxy-density rows are the ones of most interest here. Template $\times$ data crosses are nulls: their expectation is zero whatever the cosmology, so they are shown as measured, with no blinding.

Two data cycles

The result slides on the template nulls (slides 2–5) carry a Cycle toggle in the bottom-right corner; the choice holds across the deck. Cycle 1 is TR1 v1.1: the v1.1 selection, the VMPZ v1.1 templates, and density maps that are either coverage-only or divided by the VMPZ test visibility weights after the fact (each slide says which). Cycle 2 is tr1_v2: the DR1 R2 templates, the tr1_v2 selection, and density maps that are visibility-corrected at the source, so its $\delta$ rows compare with the Cycle-1 visibility-weighted ones, not the coverage-only ones. Cycle 2 is filled for the Gaussian template null family (slide 2), extinction amplitudes (slide 4) and the separate CIB-route test (slide 5). Extinction has broad positive high-$\ell$ crosses and GC1 retains the exposure/noise signature; a nonzero extinction cross is expected from the CIB, so its failed zero-null does not establish contamination. The contamination triangle (slide 3) is filled: the largest resolved proxy is extinction, $0.828\pm0.137\,\sigma_G$ in $\delta_4\gamma_6$ at $\ell=2148$, measuring mostly CIB-correlated LSS rather than survey contamination. Nominally 98.04 % of bands lie within $\pm0.75\sigma_G$, but the largest unresolved PSF cell reaches $24.6\pm21.1\,\sigma_G$ at a template-auto dip; the Cycle-1 half-$\sigma$ claim is not a Cycle-2 bound. Both cycles show TR1 LensMC shear and SPT-3G CMB lensing; the MetaCal and ACT DR6 versions of the Cycle-1 nulls are in the KP2-3 deck. Slides 6–7 (SFD and CSFD) are Cycle 2 only.
