Euclid DR1 overlaps every CMB lensing survey

The blue region is where Euclid DR1 has VIS mosaics: the union of the MER tiles in the DR1_R1 and R2 releases, as listed in the archive in October 2025. It covers 2341 deg², 1745 in the south and 596 in the north. It is not a shear mask: there are no effective-coverage, star or bright-object cuts, and DR1 shear processing is not finished, so the areas that enter an analysis will be smaller.

The CMB lensing footprints arrive one at a time. Planck PR4 (23 356 deg²) covers all but about 125 deg² of DR1: the overlap is 2215 deg². ACT DR6, with its baseline lensing mask (9646 deg²), overlaps DR1 over 1523 deg², most of it in the DR1 south field down to about −60°. SPT-3G Main, the 1500 deg² winter field (1672 deg² in its lensing mask), overlaps 476 deg² at the southern edge of the DR1 south field. The DR1 north fields are seen by Planck alone; part of the south field is seen by all three.

Conventions: a CMB footprint's area counts mask pixels above 0.5, at the mask's native NSIDE 2048; for these apodized masks this agrees with the weighted area to 0.4 %. The overlap with DR1 is binary, mask above zero and DR1 observed, with DR1 upgraded from NSIDE 1024 to 2048. The maps are drawn at NSIDE 512.
