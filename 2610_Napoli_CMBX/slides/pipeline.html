Catalogues to a blinded likelihood, with mocks at every stage

The KP2-3 pipeline end to end. Euclid TR1 shear and positions are pixelised into maps with their masks and redshift distributions; the CMB-lensing inputs are the Planck PR4, ACT DR6 and SPT-3G Main convergence maps. NaMaster pseudo-$C_\ell$ turns them into bandpowers for all six families: the three Euclid 3×2pt spectra and the three involving CMB lensing.

Two steps run in parallel on the bandpowers. The covariance is analytic and Gaussian, in NaMaster's improved narrow-kernel approximation (iNKA), and covers every block including the cross-blocks between families. The blind is a parameter-shift blind in the style of Muir et al. (2019): a sealed shift in cosmology is turned into a $\Delta C_\ell$ added to the whole six-family data vector, so shapes, signal-to-noise, the covariance and the null tests stay visible while the inferred cosmology is hidden. We run it ourselves because the official pipeline blinds only the Euclid 2pt spectra.

The theory model computes the six families with CCL and passes them through the same bandpower windows as the data. The likelihood varies it with the nuisances: linear galaxy bias, photometric-redshift shifts, shear calibration $m$ and intrinsic alignments. The blinded TR1 likelihood input exists; the TR1 chains come next.

GLASS 6×2pt mocks, with correlated Euclid and CMB-lensing fields, run through the same code and check each stage: bandpower recovery and the Gaussian covariance against the seed-to-seed scatter (10 seeds: the bulk agrees, while the stiffest low-$\ell$ clustering modes scatter about 1.3 times more than the covariance allows), the blind (a rehearsal on a mock) and the likelihood (mock chains that recover the input cosmology).
