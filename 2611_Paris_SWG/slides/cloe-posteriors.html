A second, noise-free check

Over each posterior, the fast JAX likelihood differs from the exact one by a $\log L$ standard deviation of 0.03–0.04 for eDR1like and 0.01–0.02 for CLOE. Both exact likelihoods are measured against the same fast one, so their own difference scatters by at most about 0.06 in $\log L$ across the posterior (inferred from these two numbers, not evaluated directly).
