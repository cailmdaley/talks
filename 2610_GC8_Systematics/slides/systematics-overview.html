Each cell is the zero-null chi-squared PTE for one complete 15-band cross-spectrum, using its full within-test Gaussian NaMaster covariance block. Darker means smaller PTE; values below $10^{-6}$ saturate the colour scale. These are correlated marginal tests, not family significances. All available covariance blocks, including Cycle-1 kappa, use their off-diagonal band terms.

The density rows use visibility-corrected maps and do not change when selecting shear or CMB. Cycle-1 MetaCal B-mode crosses were not run and are explicitly hatched, rather than copied from LensMC. LensMC has no B-template PTE below 0.05 in either cycle. Cycle-2 MetaCal has one marginal B failure, zodiacal light in bin 1 (PTE 0.0260), but no extinction B failure. Its extinction E failures are in bins 1, 4, 5 and 6 (PTE 0.000312, 0.0000248, $1.96\times10^{-15}$, $1.63\times10^{-38}$), reproducing the LensMC pattern. Not every E-mode bin fails: Cycle-2 LensMC E bins 2/3 have PTE about 0.18/0.09.

Depth means exposures/noise, not a uniquely bin-1 effect. Cycle-1 bins 2/3 are more discrepant than bin 1 for these templates; Cycle 2 singles out bin 1 (exposures/noise PTEs 0.00216/0.00313). The heatmap exposes other marginal cells as well; depth is not the only non-extinction marginal rejection.

The kappa test uses the full delivered CMB mask for the kappa field and template support for the template field. Restricting kappa to the common footprint changes extinction chi-squared from 57.7 to 53.0 (SPT), and 32.6 to 31.2 (ACT), still nonzero. The footprint control is in results/scratch/gc8/kappa_common/HEADLINE.md; the displayed ACT rows use its full-window JSONs.

Casey Cragg reported significant B × systematics in DR1-R3, unlike the LensMC TR1 B tests here. The catalogue, area and covariance differ; these results are not a direct reproduction or contradiction.
