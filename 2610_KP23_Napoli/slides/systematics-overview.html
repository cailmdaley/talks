Survey templates leave κ and shear clean

  • κ, shear: clean, the crosses CMBX relies on
  • δ: for GC to validate; δ5 × extinction possibly CIB in the template

δ weighted by the VMPZ test visibility maps (Jan 2026, not yet validated). PTEs not yet calibrated on mocks.

Each cell is a null test: one Euclid tracer (lens bin δ, source bin γ, the rows) or the CMB lensing map κ, crossed with one VMPZ systematics template (the columns). The number is the χ² probability to exceed over 15 bands, $100 \lt \ell \lt 3000$, with the full Gaussian covariance; κ uses the diagonal only. Values below $10^{-6}$ are written as such. These template crosses are blinding-independent. The variant control switches the shear method (γ rows) and the CMB map (κ row); the δ rows are the same in every variant.

No template stands out against shear, for either shear method: across all 72 shear tests (six templates, six source bins, two methods) the lowest PTE is 0.03, about what the smallest of 72 independent tests gives. κ is quiet for both maps (lowest 0.17).

Galaxy density is weighted by the visibility maps. Each lens bin's δ is counts divided by the VMPZ v1.1 test visibility weight, $w = (1 + \mathrm{WEIGHT}) / \langle 1 + \mathrm{WEIGHT} \rangle$ (Odier, Alguero, Macías-Pérez; January 2026, flagged TEST, not yet validated), which is what the visibility maps are for: removing the depth, exposure and extinction modulations of the selection by construction. Same templates, masks, estimator and bands as the production crosses; the Gaussian covariance is recomputed. Without the weights δ shows two signatures: exposures and noise in lens bin 1 (PTE $3\times10^{-12}$ and $2\times10^{-9}$, one depth dependence of the faintest lens sample, mirror images because pixel noise falls as exposure$^{-1/2}$), and extinction across lens bins 2–6 ($10^{-4}$ to $10^{-11}$). With them, δ₁ × exposures and noise go to 0.03 and 0.10 and extinction in bins 2, 3, 4 and 6 to 0.12–0.97. What remains is δ₅ × extinction ($9\times10^{-8}$; +3.5σ at ℓ ≈ 1090), and δ₂, δ₃ × exposures and noise at 0.01–0.03, driven by ±3–4.5σ single bands at ℓ ≈ 440–550, the tiling harmonic where a Gaussian σ for a template cross is least reliable.

Two caveats on the weights. The visibility model is a SOM fit to five survey-property maps that the headers do not name; a template among them is removed partly by construction, so its clean cell checks that the correction was applied, and only templates outside the model are independent tests. And the WEIGHT convention is this pipeline's: the official randoms follow $1 + \mathrm{WEIGHT}$ with slope 0.52, not 1, so the producers need to confirm it. The correction leaves δ × κ within 1σ of the unweighted measurement in every band (largest change 0.96σ, bin 3), so it does not visibly remove signal; an injected-signal mock test would settle that.

The PTEs themselves are not yet trustworthy as significances: with about a hundred template crosses per variant, correlated with one another through shared tracers and templates, and an analytic covariance not yet checked against the scatter of mocks, the distribution of these numbers under the null is not known. The plan is to run the same null tests on the GLASS mocks and calibrate them, as for the UNIONS B-mode null tests. Read the dark cells as clear detections and the 0.01–0.1 cells as unknown.

Is the unweighted extinction signal CIB? Open. The extinction template is documented as Planck 2013 radiance-based E(B−V), which carries cosmic infrared background; of the six templates it is the only one that correlates with the Planck GNILC CIB maps, and κ × extinction is about what the CIB route predicts. A swap to the GNILC CIB-reduced dust map weakens every cross, but it cannot apportion dust and CIB: GNILC dust is CIB-reduced, not CIB-free, and the template-cross estimator leaks the large-scale (ℓ < 100) modes of steep-spectrum maps like dust into the ℓ ≥ 100 bands, so a template cross at a given ℓ is not a measurement at that ℓ. The null tests stand as nulls (an uncorrelated template gives zero in every band whatever leaks); which scale and which component carry a detection is not yet resolved.
