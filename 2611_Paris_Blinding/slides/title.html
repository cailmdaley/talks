Blinding in Euclid DR1

Where the groups stand, and two questions for CMBX

Blinding session

Euclid CMBX SWG meeting, Paris

November 2026

  • The session — day 2 of the CMBX SWG meeting; open to every CMBX key project, not only the 6×2pt analysis.
  • Premise — Euclid blinds each project separately, so CMBX blinds its own data vectors. The open problem is checking them against other groups' while both are blinded.
  • Two questions — how big the CMBX blind should be, and how we compare our blinded data vectors with other groups'.
