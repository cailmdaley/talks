Extinction × tracers, in units of σ

  • Each point — one bandpower of tracer × extinction, divided by its Gaussian error: $C_\ell/\sigma_\ell$. The grey strip is ±1. Density is raw (no visibility correction).
  • Space — the next step switches to Cycle 2; slide 4 shows why its high-ℓ errors shrink.
  • Colours — tomographic bins from low redshift (dark) to high (yellow), same scale for δ and $\gamma_E$; κ has its own colour.
  • Toggles — cycle, shear method (LensMC / MetaCal) and CMB experiment (SPT-3G / ACT DR6); all eight combinations exist.
  • Read — in Cycle 2 the signal grows with ℓ and, in shear, with redshift: what LSS in the template would do.
What is plotted

The ordinate is $C_\ell/\sqrt{\mathrm{Cov}_{\ell\ell}}$, from each saved cross-spectrum and its full NaMaster Gaussian covariance block, before GLS compression. Each error bar is therefore one unit; adjacent bands are correlated, and the ±1 strip is not a simultaneous confidence interval. Density is without visibility correction in both cycles. All panels and states share vertical limits set by the largest plotted band plus padding; nothing is clipped.

Why Cycle 1 looks quieter

Cycle 1’s original template window inflates the high-ℓ errors and changes the crosses; backup B1 isolates that effect (κ ρ̄ 0.082 → 0.150 when Cycle 1 is rewindowed).
