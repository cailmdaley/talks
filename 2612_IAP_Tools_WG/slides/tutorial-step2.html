  • Why quote verification — agents frequently recall paper numbers from training weights with wrong error bars or wrong assumptions. Running astra paper add caches the PDF and --verify-evidence searches the exact text on the cited page.
  • Scientific tension — Suzuki et al. report $\Omega_\Lambda = 0.705^{+0.040}_{-0.043}$; our Step 1 fit gave $0.722 \pm 0.013$ on the same 580 supernovae. Something is missing, and it makes us overconfident by a factor of three.
