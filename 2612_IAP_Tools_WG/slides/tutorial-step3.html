  • Diagnostic — the table's error column matches $\sqrt{\mathrm{diag}(C_{\text{nosys}})}$ to $6.7 \times 10^{-12}$ relative difference: our Step 1 fit was statistical-only.
  • Payoff — switching to the full systematic covariance shifts $\Omega_\Lambda$ by only 0.008 ($0.722 \to 0.714$) while more than doubling the uncertainty ($\pm 0.013 \to \pm 0.030$), bringing our measurement within $0.2\sigma$ of Suzuki et al. (2012).
  • Why ASTRA decisions matter — ranking choices by central value alone would have called this negligible; recording it as a decision preserves both universes side by side.
