SPT-constrained mocks

SPT needs to provide:

  • Lensing masks, with the correct power of the primary mask
  • Pairs of ($\kappa^\mathrm{true}$, $\kappa^\mathrm{recon}$):
    these capture the observing strategy and the details of the lensing estimator

Waiting on Euclid status for external collaborators

  • What it is — mock Euclid fields conditioned on the κ of each SPT simulation, paired with SPT's own lensing reconstruction of that simulation, so the mocks carry SPT's real estimator, filtering, masks and reconstruction noise.
  • Inputs — SPT's lensing masks, which enter the reconstruction at a specific power of the primary (map-level) mask, so the mock analysis has to use the lensing masks themselves and not the footprints of the previous slide; and, for each of SPT's 350–400 simulations of the winter (Main) and summer fields, the input κ with SPT's reconstruction of it.
  • Status — SPT cannot share these products until its external collaborators have Euclid status; the request was circulated on 9 November 2026.
  • Output — covariances for κ × shear and κ × positions with SPT-3G, calibrated in the same way as those built on the ACT and Planck simulations, where about 400 constrained realizations exist.
From the CMBX telecon of 5 November 2026

About 400 realizations were judged too noisy to invert directly. The candidates raised there were shrinkage toward an analytic target (which lacks the reconstruction noise), a jackknife (biased even with delete-two, as found in DES), and a much larger set of own simulations with reconstruction run on each. JC-1's view at that telecon: the 6×2pt and covariance groups should run synthetic chains together.
