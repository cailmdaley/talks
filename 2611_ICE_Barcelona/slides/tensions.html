Tensions in ΛCDM appear when probes are combined

  • ΛCDM fits each dataset across cosmic time with six parameters
  • combining measurements from separate probes shows tensions: expansion rate, clustering amplitude, dark energy
  • new physics or uncontrolled systematics?
  • as we learn more we keep finding that we underestimated our systematic errors
  • Where the story starts — the standard model is very successful dataset by dataset; the tensions of the last decade show up between surveys.
  • Why simulations — telling new physics from systematics needs pipelines whose errors are known, which is what building them on simulations with a known answer gives.
