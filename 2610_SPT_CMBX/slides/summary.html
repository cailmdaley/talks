  2. SPT-3G κ crosses are tighter than ACT DR6 at small scales
  3. Open: covariance with SPT mocks, κκ, foregrounds, inference
  4. Euclid DR2 will overlap well with SPT-3G
  5. To participate: external-collaborator status, by June 2027

Cail Daley · CEA Paris-Saclay

On Euclid TR1 the pipeline produces a blinded 5×2pt data vector with SPT-3G and first blinded posteriors, validated on GLASS mocks and blinded as in the Euclid 3×2pt analysis. SPT-3G's error bars in the κ crosses are smaller than ACT DR6's above $\ell \approx 200$ on TR1 and about 330 projected to DR1, by up to 1.8–2.4 at $\ell \approx 2700$. The covariance plan needs SPT's mock reconstructions with their input κ; the scope (cosmologies, κκ, foreground mitigation, inference) is open. Working on the paper requires Euclid external-collaborator status, for people who will commit time before the DR1 papers in June 2027.
