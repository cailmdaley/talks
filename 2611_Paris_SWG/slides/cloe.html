3. Proposal: a standalone CLOE likelihood

  • The question — does replacing dr1_cmbx's Cobaya likelihood (eDR1like with CCL theory) by a CLOE likelihood change my posteriors or their cost?
  • Short answer — no parameter mean moves by more than 0.09σ on a GLASS mock or 0.06σ on blinded TR1, which is the Monte Carlo noise of the chains, and a chain costs about 40 times fewer node-hours. This is an exploratory comparison, not a key-project product.
  • Names — "Cobaya" on these slides is dr1_cmbx's likelihood, eDR1like with CCL theory, run in Cobaya. "CLOE in JAX" is a standalone likelihood built on CLOElib, with no Cobaya and no CCL.
