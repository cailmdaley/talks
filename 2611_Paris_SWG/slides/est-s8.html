  • −0.030 — $S_8$ from the κ crosses alone (κ × shear and κ × positions) with the profile-hardened GMV estimator, minus the same with the polarization-only estimator, on the blinded TR1 data vectors. A difference of two chains on the same blinded data; no blinded value is shown. With the temperature-only hardened estimator the shift against GMV is a further −0.028.
  • −0.038 — the same difference in Ouellette et al., SPT-3G D1 lensing × DES Y3 shear: 0.795 ± 0.052 (profile-hardened) minus $0.833^{+0.047}_{-0.061}$ (polarization-only), from their results table. Their unhardened GMV is lower still (0.744).
  • ≈1σ — treating polarization-only as a nested subset of GMV, $\sigma_\Delta^2 = \sigma_\mathrm{pol}^2 - \sigma_\mathrm{GMV}^2$ gives $\sigma_\Delta = 0.032$ and a shift of 0.94σ; with the measured correlation of the two κ maps the range is 0.8–1.0σ. The direction is the one a residual temperature foreground would give, and also what the extra noise of polarization-only allows.
  • Full vector — once 3×2pt enters, the three SPT estimators agree to 0.003 in $S_8$.
