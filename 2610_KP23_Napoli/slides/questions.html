The decisions the Napoli week puts in front of KP2-3. The first is the reason the results are here: they are marked preliminary until this group has discussed them. The rest follow the takeaway slides: scale-cut method and the κ crosses' own $k_\mathrm{max}$; whether to adopt the 3×2pt priors, n(z) model and binning; how our data products reach CLOE; and how to organise emulator work with Ivan Sladoljev, whose talk is proposed for the 29 Oct call.
