Reading the table

These are correlated marginal tests, not family significances; with 78 cells per variant a few PTEs below 0.05 are expected by chance. The density rows use visibility-corrected maps and do not change with the shear or CMB toggle. Cycle-1 MetaCal B crosses were not run and are hatched. Cycle-2 extinction E: LensMC bins 1–6 PTE $7\times10^{-4}$, 0.19, 0.09, $3\times10^{-8}$, $5\times10^{-21}$, $10^{-57}$; MetaCal $3.2\times10^{-4}$, 0.34, 0.20, $2.2\times10^{-5}$, $2.1\times10^{-15}$, $1.9\times10^{-38}$. E fails and B passes, which is what LSS in the template predicts.

κ and footprint

The κ test uses the full delivered CMB mask for κ and the template support for the template. On the pre-26-Oct bins, restricting κ to the common footprint moved extinction χ² 57.7 → 53.0 (SPT) and 32.6 → 31.2 (ACT), still nonzero; that control has not been rerun on the official bins.
