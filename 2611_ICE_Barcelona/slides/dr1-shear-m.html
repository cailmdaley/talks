CMB crosses provide an independent check of shear bias $m$

  • Plot — the forecast of the slide before on wider axes, with the priors on $m$ and the source redshift shifts ten times wider: the 3×2pt, then the 5×2pt with SPT-3G; beside it the mean and 1σ of $m$ in each source bin against the ±0.10 prior. $\sigma(m)$ in bins 3–6 goes from 0.041, 0.039, 0.038, 0.039 (3×2pt) to 0.032, 0.030, 0.028, 0.027 (5×2pt); bin 1 hardly moves (0.067 to 0.065).
  • Gain from the crosses — a calibration of shear $m$. With the priors on $m$ and the source redshift shifts ten times wider ($\sigma(m)=0.10$), the 3×2pt $\sigma(S_8)$ is 0.026 and the 5×2pt 0.019, 28 % better; three times wider, 7 %; at the nominal 1 %, 3 %. The 5×2pt then measures $m$ to about 3 % in the four highest bins against 4 % for the 3×2pt; the redshift shifts are set by the 3×2pt itself and do not improve.
