  • Release — four companion papers from one blinded catalog: I, the shape catalog (Hervas-Peters et al.); II, B-mode validation (Daley et al.); III, real-space constraints (Goh et al.); IV, harmonic-space constraints (Guerrini et al.).
  • Whisker — published Stage III cosmic-shear $S_8$, oldest at the top; the band is the CMB. Squares are the two UNIONS results, $\xi_\pm$ (Goh et al.) $S_8 = 0.831^{+0.067}_{-0.078}$ and $C_\ell$ (Guerrini et al.) $0.891^{+0.057}_{-0.084};$ both are consistent with the CMB and with Stage III at about 1σ.
  • Wide error bars — no tomography, and nuisance priors inflated beyond their statistical errors: intrinsic alignments ×2, redshifts $\Delta z$ ×1.4, shear calibration $m$ ×3.
Priors

IA: $A_{\rm IA} \sim \mathcal N(0.83, 0.78),$ the measured width doubled, because the galaxies with reliable redshifts used in direct IA measurements are bright and not representative of the source population, and those measurements come from other surveys. Redshifts: $\Delta z \sim \mathcal N(-0.003, 0.018);$ the ×1.4 is the inflation quoted at Moriond 2026, while Papers III and IV state only the total ±0.018. Shear: $m = -0.057 \pm 0.0047$ from the image simulations, error tripled to 0.014 because three large effects in the image simulations were hard to disentangle.

Numbers

KiDS-1000 $0.759^{+0.024}_{-0.021}$; DES Y3 $0.759^{+0.025}_{-0.023}$; HSC Y3 $0.769^{+0.031}_{-0.034}$; KiDS-Legacy $0.815^{+0.016}_{-0.021}$; DES Y6 $0.798^{+0.014}_{-0.015}$; CMB $0.836^{+0.012}_{-0.013}$. Paper I's catalog: 61.4 million galaxies over 3,648 deg² of imaging, 2,894 deg² effective, $n_{\rm eff} = 4.96$ arcmin$^{-2}$.
