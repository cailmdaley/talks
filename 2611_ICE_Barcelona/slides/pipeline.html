KP2-3 pipeline

  • Steps — one workstream per step of the Euclid CMBX KP2-3 pipeline: data vectors and systematics, then simulations and covariance, then theory, blind and likelihood. The workstream structure follows the Barcelona KP2-3 update by Louis Legrand, Margherita Lembo and Giulio Fabbian.
  • Spectra — the left column is the Euclid 3×2pt, the right column the three families with CMB lensing.
  • Covariance — the chains use the analytic Gaussian (iNKA) plus super-sample covariance; FLASK is the numerical covariance in preparation (the simulations slides).
  • Blind — a step of this pipeline, applied to the five families of the current data vector (κκ not yet included) before the likelihood (backup: the blinding rehearsal).
Data vectors and systematics

Euclid TR1 shear and positions are pixelized into maps with their masks and redshift distributions; the CMB-lensing inputs are the Planck PR4, ACT DR6 and SPT-3G Main convergence maps. The SPT-3G baseline is the bias- and profile-hardened GMV reconstruction (PH-GMV, kGMVbhTTprf), which suppresses the tSZ and foreground bias a temperature-based reconstruction imprints on crosses with large-scale-structure tracers; plain GMV and polarization-only are the variants.

NaMaster pseudo-$C_\ell$ turns them into bandpowers for all six families. The same estimator crosses the data with systematics maps, the templates carried by Euclid's VMPs (extinction, exposures, noise, PSF, zodiacal light, stars) and external CMB foreground maps, which gives the null tests and validation of the data vectors.

Simulations and covariance

GLASS and FLASK lognormal mocks on the TR1 footprint with the delivered n(z) run through the same NaMaster step as the data. GLASS validates the pipeline (the mocks slide); FLASK, calibrated on DEMNUni N-body simulations, gives the numerical covariance. The covariance in use is analytic: Gaussian in NaMaster's improved narrow-kernel approximation (iNKA), evaluated at a theory fiducial, plus super-sample covariance (SSC); it covers every block, including the cross-blocks between families.

Theory, likelihood and the blind

The blind is a parameter-shift blind in the style of Muir et al. (2020): a sealed shift in cosmology is turned into a $\Delta C_\ell$ added to the whole five-family data vector, so shapes, signal-to-noise, the covariance and the null tests stay visible while the inferred cosmology is hidden. The official pipeline blinds only the Euclid 2pt spectra, so this pipeline runs its own blind, a step shared across the workstreams.

The theory model computes the six families with CLOE, the Euclid likelihood code, and its emulators, and passes them through the same bandpower windows as the data. The likelihood varies it with the nuisances: linear galaxy bias $b$, photometric-redshift shifts $\Delta z$, shear calibration $m$ and intrinsic alignments. The blinded TR1 chains are on the posteriors slide.
