κ breaks 3×2pt degeneracies and cross-checks nuisances

  • γκ: an independent handle on shear $m$ and the high-z tail of n(z)
  • δκ vs δδ: bias free of $\sigma_8$ (still tied to $\Omega_{\rm m}$)
  • more $S_8,$ $\Omega_{\rm m}$ and growth out to higher z

DES Y3, free $m$: $\sigma(S_8)$ grows ×4.7 without κ, ×2.3 with it

The figure is from the DES Y3 joint analysis with SPT and Planck CMB lensing (Abbott et al. 2023). Filled contours re-run 3×2pt (orange) and 5×2pt, which adds $\gamma\kappa$ and $\delta\kappa$ (teal), with the shear multiplicative-bias priors widened to flat $m_i \in (-0.5, 0.5)$; the lines are the baseline runs with the image-simulation priors. With $m$ free, 3×2pt loses most of its $S_8$ constraint, a factor 4.7 in $\sigma(S_8),$ because $m$ and $S_8$ both scale the shear amplitude. With the κ crosses the loss is a factor 2.3, and the data alone bound $m_i$ at roughly 5 to 10 % per bin, consistent with the nominal priors. That is self-calibration, as proposed by Das, Errard & Spergel (2013, arXiv:1311.2338) among others: in a joint fit $m$ stays partly degenerate with cosmology, n(z) and IA, so κ is an independent check on the image-simulation calibration rather than a replacement for it.

The handle on $m$ and on n(z) comes mostly from the same place: $\gamma\kappa$ in a bin scales with its $1+m$ and with how much of its n(z) sits at high redshift, where the CMB kernel is largest. Harnois-Déraps et al. (2017), crossing KiDS-450 with Planck, found the two strongly correlated and warned against reading $\gamma\kappa$ as a stand-alone calibration of either. Intrinsic alignments also enter $\gamma\kappa$ and lower its amplitude; in that analysis modelling them raised the fitted amplitude by about 0.1.

For clustering, on linear scales $\delta\delta$ scales as $b^2\sigma_8^2$ and $\delta\kappa$ as $b\,\sigma_8^2,$ so the pair measures galaxy bias free of the amplitude of structure, without galaxy–galaxy lensing. It is not free of $\Omega_{\rm m}$: the CMB-lensing kernel carries a factor $\Omega_{\rm m}$ that the clustering auto-spectrum does not, so the bias inferred this way scales roughly as $\Omega_{\rm m}^{-(0.5\text{–}0.8)}$ and is blinded along with the cosmology. And because the CMB kernel reaches beyond the Euclid sources, the crosses weight structure at higher redshift than cosmic shear alone, adding $S_8$ and $\Omega_{\rm m}$ information and a lever on growth over time. In DES Y3, adding the κ crosses to 3×2pt gave $S_8 = 0.773 \pm 0.016$ and $\Omega_{\rm m} = 0.344 \pm 0.030$ (5×2pt), and with the CMB lensing auto-spectrum $S_8 = 0.792 \pm 0.012$ (6×2pt).
