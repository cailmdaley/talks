The v2 shape catalog holds 191 million galaxies over 5200 deg²

  • $n_{\rm eff} = 6.9$ arcmin$^{-2},$ $\sigma_e = 0.29$
  • area × $n_{\rm eff}$ matches DES Y6
  • 34 core-years in under three weeks
  • Euclid covers this sky by DR3

Led by Cail Daley

  • Animation — the v2 production run, 10–27 October 2026: each DR6 tile fills with the blinded mass map as its shapes complete, beside the completed area against time; it plays once and stops on the full footprint, about 5,700 deg² of tiles before masking.
  • Numbers — the full calibrated v2 sample: 191.0 million galaxies on 5,223 deg², $n_{\rm eff} = 6.91$ arcmin$^{-2}$ (Heymans et al. 2012 definition), $\sigma_e = 0.289$ per component. The first release had 2,894 deg² effective and $n_{\rm eff} = 4.96.$
  • Against DES Y6 — area × $n_{\rm eff}$ is 36.1k for UNIONS v2 and 36.3k for DES Y6 (4,422 deg² × 8.22, Yamamoto et al. 2025); by galaxy count UNIONS v2 is larger, 191M against 152M.
  • Run time — 34 core-years billed over the production run, failed jobs included, wall-clock under three weeks, driven by agents; the catalog behind the first release took more than a year.
Status

Pre-publication: the catalog and its paper come first, with the catalog to be made public quickly. Tomographic photometric redshifts cover 4,582 deg² of it so far (incomplete z band).
