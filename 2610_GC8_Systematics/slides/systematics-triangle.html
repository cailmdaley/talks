Implied contamination stays under half a σ

Built in two steps. First one pair close up, $\gamma_6 \times \kappa$ against the extinction template: the card a click opens on the full triangle. Its top row is the template's measured cross with each of the pair's tracers, $C_\ell^{\gamma_6 S}$ and $C_\ell^{\kappa S}$, each scaled by the integer power $\ell^n$ that makes its error bars most nearly equal across the bands ($\ell^2$ for most crosses, $\ell$ for κ and for some source bins, as each axis label says), each with its χ² over the 15 bands against zero and PTE (full Gaussian covariance; κ diagonal only). Its bottom panel is what those two crosses imply for the pair if they were all leakage, $X_\ell^{ab} = C_\ell^{aS} C_\ell^{bS} / C_\ell^{SS}$, in units of $\sigma_{ab}$, the Gaussian error of $C_\ell^{ab}$ in the blinded data vector. Then the card shrinks into its cell and the view is the whole data vector, one cell per pair of tracers.

Every panel spans $\pm 0.75\,\sigma_{ab}$, with $\pm 0.5\,\sigma_{ab}$ shaded; a band beyond the scale is a triangle on the edge. That range holds 99.6 % of all bands over every template and variant. The bars are $\sigma_X/\sigma_{ab}$, first-order error propagation that ignores the covariance between the two crosses and treats the template's auto-spectrum with a Gaussian error, which is least reliable at a tiling-periodic template's harmonics. Their size varies with ℓ because the template's own power does; no power of ℓ evens them out, since it cancels in the ratio.

The lens-bin crosses use δ weighted by the VMPZ test visibility maps, as on the previous slide. Cells outlined in orange are those where both template crosses behind the cell fail, so the pair shares a detectable correlation with the template: each cross's χ² PTE over the 15 bands is below $10^{-4}$ (full Gaussian covariance; κ diagonal). The PTE statistics are not right yet: there are about a hundred correlated template crosses per variant and the PTEs are not calibrated on mocks (the plan is to run the null tests on the GLASS mocks, as for the UNIONS B-mode null tests), so the threshold is a guide, not a significance. In every variant the one flagged cross is δ₅ × extinction, so the one outlined cell is δ₅δ₅ under extinction. Without the visibility weights, extinction also flagged lens bins 2, 4 and 6 and exposures and noise lens bin 1. Inside a card, the failing cross carries its PTE in orange.

A shared correlation with the template needs both crosses to be nonzero, so the pair's PTE is the larger of the two crosses' PTEs on its card.

The buttons above the contamination panel pick the template; the variant control picks the shear method and CMB map; on the full triangle a click on a cell opens its card. Where X is resolved (above twice its error), nothing exceeds about 0.4 $\sigma_{ab}$: the largest are noise and exposures in δ₂γ₃ at ℓ ≈ 440 (MetaCal, $0.37 \pm 0.16$) and in δ₃δ₃ at ℓ ≈ 550 ($0.30 \pm 0.14$), both at the tiling harmonic. The nominal maxima above 1 (PSF at ℓ ≈ 690 in pairs with δ₁, and with MetaCal in source bin 2 up to about 2) sit at a dip of the template's own power, where $\sigma_X$ is as large as X; they are the edge triangles. All four shear × CMB variants are measured; the δ crosses are the same in every variant.
