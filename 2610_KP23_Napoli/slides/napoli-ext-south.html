SPT-3G × Euclid is all south

What was said

  • 3×2pt splinter, day 3 (Lucie Baumont; redshift topical team with Michel Aguena and Roberto Ingrao; deck pp. 2–12): a low patch in the density/systematics map, too large to blame on the RedMaGiC selection alone. "Red sequences are chaotic in EXT data" (p. 4). The same patches appear comparing galaxies to DES and stars to Gaia, and in the EXT seeing maps. Magnitude differences against RedMaGiC trend with galaxy size, strongly in aperture magnitudes. The OU's coadd PSF-residual postage stamps show dipoles (astrometry, per the OU) plus unexplained structure. Next step: an independent SExtractor run on the EXT TR1 coadds, to tell a MER problem from a PSF problem.
  • Same splinter: the PHZ catalogue is frozen as the DR1 Foundation catalogue, public in weeks. One participant argued the photometry problem may justify an internal 3×2pt PHZ rerun; others answered that a rerun is pointless without fixed photometry.
  • SHECAL (Martinet, day 2): EXT-DES is broken in the SIM chain, with no plan B for DR1; the South is blocked on reduced EXT-DES data, "no bypass possible". The person who ran the DES reduction left.
  • Martin Kilbinger at the CosmoStat group meeting (20 Oct) added numbers not in the notes or deck pages kept here (EXT − DES g-band residuals of ±30 mmag, about twice DES's own scatter against Gaia), and read it as probably worse for clusters than for weak lensing on average.

Why it matters to KP2-3

Every SPT × Euclid cross sits in the south, where Euclid's optical photometry is EXT-DES. Spatially varying photometry gives spatially varying lens selection and photo-z, which is the kind of pattern a κ × δ cross picks up, and DR2 keeps the south on DES (previous slide).
