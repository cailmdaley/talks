Mocks tell how far apart the two analyses may be

  • same catalog, n(z) and blinding: real and harmonic space
  • shared data: independent error bars overstate agreement
  • GLASS mocks: $\Delta S_8 = -0.06$ is 2.2σ
  • Histogram — $S_8$ from real space (Paper III) minus harmonic space (Paper IV) over 341 GLASS lognormal mocks of the UNIONS-3500 footprint; the line is the data, $\Delta S_8 = -0.06,$ with 5 mocks further out (PTE 0.015, 2.18σ).
  • The case for mocks — the two statistics share data, so comparing their independent error bars (0.56σ apart) overstates their agreement; the mock scatter is the right yardstick.
  • Density — the mocks are about 20 % denser than the data, which narrows the mock scatter; the Paper IV authors estimate about 1.9σ for a matched density, in a revision not yet public.
  • Candidate cause — the CCD-scale B-mode feature, localized in θ, spreads over all ℓ in harmonic space, adding power and raising $S_8.$
