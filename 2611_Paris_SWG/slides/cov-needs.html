Running by end February; blinded data early April
  • 3×2pt — the official 3×2pt covariance has to be used, since it cannot be reproduced numerically (CMBX telecon, 5 November 2026); its masks, weights and n(z) are not final.
  • κκ — the κκ × κκ block comes from the CMB experiments. Davide Luchina proposed, and the group leaned toward (5 November): keep the external κκ likelihood and condition the rest of the likelihood and covariance on it through the exact relation, rather than dropping blocks. Decision wanted by the end of November.
  • κ crosses — need simulated κ reconstructions, so that reconstruction noise and its correlations enter. About 400 constrained realizations exist on the ACT and Planck simulations.
  • SPT — SPT has 350–400 simulations; lensing reconstructions on them for the winter and summer fields are the main missing input. SPT cannot share products until its external collaborators have Euclid status (request circulated 9 November 2026).
  • κκ × 3×2pt — Giulio Fabbian expects it negligible for DR1, less clearly at large scales; it may shape the likelihood interface.
  • SSC — lognormal fields lack full super-sample covariance, which matters on the small TR1 footprint.
