What was said

  • Day 3, late session: "The baseline model is Stage-III-like (one linear bias per bin, NLA with z-evolution, magnification, n(z) mean shifts only, one massive ν fixed during scale-cut validation)". "Cosmological parameters are sampled with uniform priors, except n_s, which gets a Gaussian prior 10× the Planck width." "The most up-to-date range is log T_AGN ∈ [7.3, 8.5]." On shear bias: "leave the shear-bias prior as is" (Gaussian, σ = 0.01, called "quite pessimistic").
  • The same session recorded both "identical priors and sampled parameters across probes, for the joint analysis" and "sampled parameters are agreed per KP, at least at this stage"; the $S_8$-vs-$A_s$ sampling basis was unresolved. The action table adds running the CMB with the same priors for the $S_8$ tension.
  • Shared chain configs and scripts move from Cerberus into the CLOE GitHub organisation; whether CMBX gets a folder was not said.
  • CLOE splinter: "Decision: switch to sqrt binning; explore the bin number with chains". ℓ_min = 10 was validated only on unmasked Flagship; leaning to keep 10–3000, not decided.

The TR1 chains in this talk

KP2-3 has not set TR1 priors. The chain profile behind the posteriors in this talk (workflow/scripts/tr1_production_chain_config.json) adopted most of the splinter's table after Napoli, each departure from its earlier choices kept as a named variant. Two differences remain. The n(z) stretch stays, $\mathcal{N}(1, 0.1)$, because on the v1.1 products the lens data pull the stretches to 0.6–0.8 and reject shift-only (variant shift_only fixes it). Magnification uses fixed measured slopes until the $\alpha$ convention is pinned. The table's $T_\mathrm{AGN}$ upper bound is 8.3; the day-3 session called [7.3, 8.5] the most up-to-date range, and the chains follow the table until it is updated.

The n(z) line: d'Assignies, day 4 (from the deck, not transcribed)

  • Compared shift + stretch (n(z) moments 1–2) with DES Y6-style n(z) modes, on Dumerchat's calibration sims. KiDS-like case (sub-1 % systematic error): shifts suffice. "DES-like (Euclid DR1)" case, ~5 % systematic error: "correcting the estimated n(z) by the difference in moments is not enough to recover the true cosmology, even with broad priors on shift and stretch" (p. 14); $S_8$ moves from about 0.83 to about 0.89.
  • Next steps (p. 15): first check the sims' realism (origin of the tails problem); there is "no straightforward solution such as 'let's use DESY6 modes method'". Provisional, no fix on the table. The figure is the backup slide "Calibration".
  • Day 3, late session: one participant said LSST preparation work showed shift-only is "very, very optimistic" and that one needs to go beyond shift + width.

The m line: SHECAL, Nicolas Martinet, day 2

Deck p. 10: "LensMC: m = −0.046 +/− 0.004; MetaCal: m = −0.018 +/− 0.007", preliminary, 17 MER tiles, including selection. The 3×2pt prior $m \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 0.01)$ is centred on zero, so these set where the κ crosses' m prior is centred, not only its width. Details in backup.

Why it matters to KP2-3

These are the defaults a joint κ + 3×2pt analysis inherits, and a starting point for KP2-3's own choice. Two differ from the chains here: their n(z) model has a shift and a stretch per bin, and their binning (15 log bands, ℓ = 100–3000) has about three bands above ℓ = 1500, the resolution problem the √ℓ switch addresses. Cail's working stance is to build on the 3×2pt choices and depart only where κ brings something in; the group has not decided.
