no tomography, and priors inflated beyond the statistical errors:

  • intrinsic alignments ×2
  • redshifts $\Delta z$ ×1.4
  • shear calibration $m$ ×3
  • Squares — the two UNIONS results, $\xi_\pm$ (Goh et al.) $S_8 = 0.831^{+0.067}_{-0.078}$ and $C_\ell$ (Guerrini et al.) $0.891^{+0.057}_{-0.084};$ circles are the Stage III surveys. Both UNIONS points are consistent with the CMB and with Stage III at about 1σ.
  • Why the error bars are wide — conservative nuisance priors. IA: $A_{\rm IA} \sim \mathcal N(0.83, 0.78),$ the measured width doubled. Redshifts: $\Delta z \sim \mathcal N(-0.003, 0.018).$ Shear: $m = -0.057 \pm 0.0047$ from the image simulations, error tripled to 0.014.
  • Robustness — dropping IA moves $S_8$ by $-0.7\sigma,$ a flat $A_{\rm IA}$ prior by $+0.4\sigma;$ every other choice by less than $0.4\sigma$ (backup).
Why ×2 and ×3

IA width doubled because the galaxies with reliable redshifts used in direct IA measurements are bright and not representative of the source population, and those measurements come from other surveys, whose IA amplitude depends on shape algorithm and band. $m$ error tripled because three large effects in the image simulations were hard to disentangle. The ×1.4 on $\Delta z$ is the inflation quoted at Moriond 2026; Papers III and IV state only the total ±0.018 (bootstrap plus a systematic term).
