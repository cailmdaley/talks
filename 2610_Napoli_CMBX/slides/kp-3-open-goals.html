Open goals for KP2-3

  • Scale cuts as an analysis decision, each option validated against theory and mocks
  • Consistency across choices: LensMC vs MetaCal shear, SPT-3G vs ACT DR6 lensing
  • Systematics: template null tests and deprojection on every tracer
  • TR1 chains with the full nuisance model, then DR1

The pipeline runs end to end, so the open work is about choices and validation. Scale cuts are the largest: the data-vector slide shows every band, because where to cut depends on how far the linear-bias model and the nuisance treatment hold, per family and per bin, and that is a decision to make explicitly, with each option tested against perturbation-theory contamination on theory and on the mocks. The data vectors exist for two shear methods and two CMB lensing maps, and their agreement is a check in itself. The systematics battery crosses survey-property templates with the galaxy density and shear maps and measures what deprojection changes. Chains on the TR1 data come after these, followed by DR1.
