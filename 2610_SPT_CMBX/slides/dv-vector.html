TR1 × CMB lensing blinded 5×2pt data vector

Cycle 2. Data: Euclid TR1 v2 LensMC shear and galaxy density on the official tomographic bins (26 Oct catalogues), and the SPT-3G Main winter CMB lensing map, bias+profile-hardened GMV (kGMVbhTTprf); NaMaster bandpowers, 15 bands from $\ell = 100$ to 3000; full NaMaster Gaussian covariance; blinded (cmbx_dr1_a). Data and σ_G are the rerun5 likelihood input (27 Oct). The black curve is still the windowed fiducial of the Cycle-1 run: the Cycle-2 one waits on the theory-fiducial covariance chain. The scale cut drawn is the validated linear-bias cut, the same per-pair ℓ bounds as before (the n(z) are unchanged).

Against Cycle 1 the blinded data barely move. The shear errors are unchanged too: shear noise and σ_G agree with the Cycle-1 product to 0.94–1.01 in every shear auto, and both cycles pass the B-mode null. The clustering errors grow, following the data: the measured galaxy auto-spectra are 1.17× (first lens bin) to 1.35–1.6× (last) Cycle 1's, and the Gaussian covariance is built from the measured spectra. ACT DR6 on Cycle 2 follows (its likelihood input is next in the queue); the ACT button is dashed until then.

From here on the plots are measured on data: the Euclid TR1 release crossed with the SPT-3G and ACT DR6 lensing maps. With CMB lensing the 3×2pt data vector gains a row, shear × κ and galaxy density × κ: 5×2pt (the κ auto-spectrum, the sixth, is not in this vector). These are blinded data, built in three steps along one row of the data vector: the row of cross-spectra with the SPT-3G Main-field CMB lensing map. First its source-bin half: Euclid TR1 shear in six source bins against $\kappa$, $\ell C_\ell^{\gamma\kappa}$ in 15 NaMaster bandpowers from $\ell = 100$ to 3000. Error bars are $\sigma_G$, the square root of the diagonal of the full NaMaster Gaussian covariance. The black curve is the fiducial theory pushed through the same bandpower windows as the data: a fixed reference, not a fit. The strip under each panel is $(\text{data} - \text{fiducial})/\sigma_G$ over $\pm 3$, shaded at $\pm 1$; a band beyond it is marked by a triangle on its edge.

The view then pans along the same row to its lens-bin half, the galaxy-density cross $\delta\kappa$, on one shared vertical scale.

Finally the view pulls back to the whole data vector: 90 spectra and 1350 bandpowers, as a lower triangle of panels. Rows and columns run over the twelve Euclid tracers, source bins $\gamma_i$ then lens bins $\delta_i$, and the CMB lensing row is the base, in the order just panned along. Every panel shows $\ell C_\ell$; the panels of a row share one vertical scale (its shear columns and its density columns separately), so shapes compare along a row. The overview carries no y-axis labels; the close-ups and the detail cards do. The galaxy-clustering amplitudes vary pair by pair: fitting one amplitude per pair against the windowed fiducial (full covariance), the δδ autos sit high (about 1.0 to 1.7), the crosses between non-adjacent lens bins sit low (about 0.4 to 0.6), and δκ sits near 1. Each pair is flat in ℓ; a constant offset only looks like an excess growing with ℓ because σ shrinks. The pattern points at less inter-bin n(z) tail overlap than the delivered n(z) predict, with intrinsic alignments contributing on the source side; magnification at the measured flux slopes does not explain it. Nor can the blind: it adds one smooth cosmology shift to every spectrum at fixed bias, which rescales neighbouring pairs alike and cannot raise the autos while lowering the non-adjacent crosses.
