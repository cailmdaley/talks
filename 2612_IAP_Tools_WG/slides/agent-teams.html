Agent teams coordinate while work is running

  • Agent-to-agent messaging — unlike fire-and-forget sub-agents, a team lets the lead redirect workers mid-flight and lets peers hand off findings without routing every byte through one context window.
  • Cost and context allocation — keep planning and scientific judgment in the lead; offload bulky log reading, codebase search, and independent code review to cheaper or fresh-context workers.
  • Failure mode — more messages do not automatically mean better coordination; clear ownership and explicit done-conditions matter more than team size.
