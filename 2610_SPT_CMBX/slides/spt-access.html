Euclid access for SPT MoU members

  • Euclid external-collaborator status: one form per person
  • Master form ready → CMBX → SPT MoU committee
  • Then per-person forms for the SPT members
  • Then a shared NERSC mirror of the Euclid-side inputs

SPT members of the MoU need Euclid external-collaborator status before Euclid data reach them, and SPT will share its data products once that is in place. The request is one form per person; a master form, on the official Redmine template, is ready and goes through CMBX and then the SPT MoU committee, after which the per-person forms follow. The Euclid-side inputs (TR1 catalogue products, masks, the CMB lensing maps, 216 GB) are mirrored at NERSC under project m5099, and the mirror opens to SPT members once the forms are approved.
