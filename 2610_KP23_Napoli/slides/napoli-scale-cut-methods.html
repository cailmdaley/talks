Three ways to set $\ell_\mathrm{max}$

A screenshot of the shared splinter deck (Napoli, 3×2pt splinter 14 Oct and CLOE splinter 15 Oct; speaker not recorded). The CLOE splinter notes list the full set as M0 fixed $\ell_\mathrm{max}$, M1 Limber $k_\mathrm{max} \to \ell_\mathrm{max}$, M2 at most 5 % of the signal above $k_\mathrm{max}$, M3 per-pair Δχ² removal. The room did not choose between them; one view was to drop M3 for Euclid, since Δχ² < 1 ≈ 0.5σ was a Stage-III heuristic. The right-hand plot shows how differently M2 and M3 behave with redshift.

Our TR1 production profile cuts the lens side at a linear-bias $k_\mathrm{max}$ (δδ 0.2, γδ and κδ 0.15 $h\,\mathrm{Mpc^{-1}}$), validated against one-loop PT contamination (0.22σ in $S_8$–$\Omega_\mathrm{m}$, under the DES Y3 0.3σ criterion).
