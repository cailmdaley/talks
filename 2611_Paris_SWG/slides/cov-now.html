Now: analytic Gaussian + SSC, mock-checked

  • NaMaster Gaussian + SSC, 92 pairs: 73 min, then cached
  • 20 GLASS seeds: shear χ²/band 1.03, clustering 0.77–0.88
  • ACT κ × shear mocks scatter 20 % beyond the covariance
  • δ auto, lowest bin: excess scatter at ℓ ≳ 1000 in mocks

Untested: SSC, and κ reconstruction noise beyond Gaussian

  • Production covariance — analytic Gaussian (NaMaster, with the mode-coupling of each mask) plus super-sample covariance, for the 92 spectra of the 6×2pt vector. Serial it took about 16 h; with parallel block assembly it fills in 73 min and replays from cache in minutes.
  • GLASS check — 20 TR1-footprint GLASS seeds against the model at the truth, with production cuts: χ²/band GG 0.77 ± 0.03, GL 0.88 ± 0.02, LL 1.03 ± 0.02, GK 0.82 ± 0.05, LK 0.93 ± 0.04 (SPT κ). The clustering blocks are over-predicted because the covariance's fiducial is a fit to data whose clustering power sits above the mocks'; shear is right.
  • ACT κ — with ACT DR6 κ the same seeds give LK 1.20 ± 0.03 and GK 1.03 ± 0.07: the κ × shear mocks scatter 20 % more than the ACT covariance allows, so ACT κ × shear posteriors would under-cover.
  • δ auto — in the constrained GLASS mocks, the lowest-redshift galaxy auto-spectrum scatters beyond its Gaussian σ at ℓ ≳ 1000 with no mean offset; conditioning on κ does not cause it. A connected (trispectrum) term of the lognormal field, which a Gaussian covariance omits, is an untested guess.
  • Untested — an inference from the above: lognormal GLASS mocks carry neither full SSC nor the non-Gaussian noise of a real κ reconstruction, so the mock checks validate the Gaussian part only.
