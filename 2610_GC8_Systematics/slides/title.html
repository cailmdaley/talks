  • What this is — a preliminary CMBX KP2-3 test: TR1 density, shear and CMB lensing crossed with the OU-VMPZ extinction map.
  • Cycles — Cycle 1 is the default on every slide; the toggle in the corner switches to Cycle 2 where it exists.

  • Scope — template crosses test the template, not contamination of the science vector.
What changed between Cycle 1 and Cycle 2

Cycle 1 uses TR1 v1.1 products and legacy windows; Cycle 2 uses the tr1_v2 selection and DR1 R2 products. Cycle-1 density visibility weighting is applied after map-making; the Cycle-2 correction is built into the maps. The raw Cycle-2 comparison removes visibility corrections on the already-supported catalogue pixels, without restoring excluded pixels. This is a release comparison, not a controlled change of dust map.

Shear inputs per cycle

Cycle 1 is catTR1v1p1_WL_260623 (TR1 v1.1); Cycle 2 is the 26 October TR1 v2 export with the official tomographic bins and inherited LensMC shears according to the release declaration. Both archived map recipes use the same South WL coverage file and weights-in-mask policy at NSIDE 2048, but the binned maps and weights differ: per-bin support is 319.28–319.68 deg² in Cycle 1 versus 319.24–319.72 in Cycle 2; bin-6 total shear weight rises by 6.90 per cent. Nearly equal areas do not imply identical source selections or shear weights. The dominant template-window effect on the cross errors is tested separately in the cycle ladder (backup B1); these input differences are not a causal attribution of the cycle change.

Status of the measurements

GLASS calibration of the null PTEs is planned after the talk; the Gaussian covariances are not yet trusted as a calibrated significance scale. The shear-B, MetaCal, ACT-window, ρ and window controls are exploratory talk-only measurements and do not replace the production data vector.
