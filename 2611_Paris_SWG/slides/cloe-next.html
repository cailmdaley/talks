What the CLOE likelihood still needs

  • κκ: a term wrapping SPT's candl likelihood is designed but not built
  • Planck and ACT κκ as external likelihoods (?)
  • cloerun as the driver: runs today with three local patches
  • The JAX pieces proposed upstream to CLOElib
  • κκ — the vector compared here is 5×2pt; the lensing auto-spectrum is not in it. For SPT the design is a likelihood term that wraps candl, either as an independent term or with κκ inside the joint vector and candl supplying only the model; neither is built. Adding SPT likelihoods to CLOE as an external module looked feasible at the CMBX telecon of 5 November 2026, and JC-1 welcomed it.
  • cloerun — four chains (the GLASS mock full vector, 3×2pt and κ crosses, and the data model on blinded TR1) were run through cloerun and draw the same samples as the standalone runner to rounding, with wall times within 1–3 %. This needs three local changes to cloerun, which upstream cannot yet load an external or batched probe without; the likelihood is still not CLOElike's. Using cloerun would unify the driver, the configurations and the chain format with 3×2pt, not the theory.
  • Upstream — the JAX call of the CosmoPower emulator is prepared as a change to CLOElib; the projection patches are carried locally and listed in the package README.
  • Above z = 5 — the emulators are trained to $z = 5$; above it CLOElib continues $P(k)$ as a power law in $1+z$. The crosses see nothing above $z = 5$ (≤0.001σ); it matters for κκ, where $z > 5$ is 18–30 % of $C_\ell^{\kappa\kappa}$.
