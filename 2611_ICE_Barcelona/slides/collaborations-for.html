So what are collaborations for?

Exchanging ideas, at the speed of thought rather than the speed of sound.

  • A question, not a proposal — if one person with the spiral can run every phase of an analysis, at what scale does collaboration still work, and for what?
  • Multiplayer science — running the spiral already needs a note system through which agents share notes and hand tasks to one another; it extends to several people and their agents. A meeting's transcript reaches everyone's agents, and by the time it ends the work has moved.
