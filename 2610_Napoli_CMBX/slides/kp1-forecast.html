Contributor's notes

The CMBX project: Fisher forecasts for Euclid photometric 3x2pt alone and combined with CMB lensing (6x2pt), for LCDM, w0waCDM, induced gravity, nmcxp-Delta, K-mouflage and TPM, with Planck and SO lensing, pessimistic and optimistic cuts, Gaussian plus super-sample covariance and a BBN prior on omega_b. Across the six models (full survey, SO, pessimistic) sigma8 tightens by 40-73%, except nmcxp-Delta (12%); n_s 25-44%, sum m_nu 15-59%, the DE/MG parameters 7-41%. The figure (paper Fig. 3 with the BBN overlay): solid bars with the BBN prior, hatched caps = the change without it. Ratio sigma(6x2pt x Planck) / sigma(3x2pt), lower = more gain, for DR1 (red: 6+6 bins, 2500 deg2, per-bin ell_max 140-380 GC / 281-760 WL, Gaussian) and the full survey (blue: 13+13 bins, 14 700 deg2, pessimistic cuts, Gaussian + SSC). Both with Planck lensing, so the comparison is like for like. LCDM, DR1 / full: Omega_m 0.73 / 0.96, Omega_b 0.94 / 0.97, h 0.94 / 0.97, n_s 0.55 / 0.75, sigma8 0.24 / 0.53, sum m_nu 0.47 / 0.69. w0waCDM: sigma8 0.30 / 0.58, sum m_nu 0.41 / 0.76, w0 0.84 / 0.93, wa 0.88 / 0.96. BBN prior: without it the sigma8, sum m_nu and Omega_m ratios change by at most 0.05. n_s changes most (DR1 0.55 with BBN, 0.72 without): without BBN sigma(n_s) is limited by the n_s-Omega_b-h degeneracy (corr(n_s, h) = -0.87), which CMB lensing does not break; once BBN fixes Omega_b h^2, what limits n_s in 3x2pt is the shear bias, which CMB lensing calibrates (with the m_i fixed the 6x2pt gain on n_s vanishes: ratio 0.98). Both 3x2pt and 6x2pt carry the same prior in each ratio. The point: the cross-correlation is worth most where Euclid alone is weakest, the same trend as pessimistic against optimistic cuts, so CMB lensing matters most for early data. The next slide shows the mechanism: CMB lensing calibrates the multiplicative shear biases. Caveats: Fisher forecasts; tau fixed; DR1 Gaussian only, the full survey with SSC.
