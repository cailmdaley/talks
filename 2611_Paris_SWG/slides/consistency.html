Comparing with other groups while blinded

We want our data vector to be consistent with 3×2pt

  1. Get 3×2pt shift?
  2. Coordinate with SAS to apply 3×2pt shift to our data vectors?
  3. Data vectors are simpler to compare than contours
  • The aim — the γγ, δγ and δδ blocks of the CMBX vector should agree with the official 3×2pt measurement of the same galaxies, and that has to be checkable while both are blinded.
  • 1, 2 — two ways to put both on one blind. Either CMBX receives the 3×2pt shift and adds it where its data vectors are made, or the shift is applied to the CMBX vectors on the SAS, as it is to the 3×2pt ones, so that CMBX never holds it.
  • 3 — with one shift on both, the shared spectra can be compared band by band. Comparing contours is harder: the probes differ in constraining power, so the same shift is a different number of σ for each (the Flagship-2 test).
  • The problem today — the γγ, δγ and δδ spectra in the CMBX 6×2pt vector are the official 3×2pt statistics measured on the same galaxies, but blinded with a different draw. A difference between the two blinded copies mixes real measurement differences with the difference of two hidden shifts. Using blindspot for CMBX shifts would not change that.
  • Hold both shifts — if CMBX knew the 3×2pt draw, it could move one vector onto the other's blind and compare. Policy keeps the draws separate, and knowing it would reveal what 3×2pt is meant not to see.
  • Same mocks — run the official LE3 measurement and the CMBX one on the same simulated catalogue and masks. It tests code, masks and weights with no blind; it cannot test choices made only on data.
  • Key holder — a proposal, not in any Euclid document: someone who can see both unblinded vectors computes the χ² of their difference on the shared spectra with a test fixed in advance, and returns only the PTE. It reveals agreement, not cosmology.
  • Joint blind — send the specification of the crosses to the SGS and receive a blinded 6×2pt vector. In December 2025 Samuel Farrens offered this, given a ΔCℓ file including the CMBX spectra (Margherita Lembo, CMBX–cloe-org chat); it was not taken further. It makes the 6×2pt vector consistent in itself, not consistent with the official 3×2pt.
  • After unblinding — blinded data early April 2027, unblinded rerun about a month later.

A shared blinding method does not remove the problem.

Measuring 3×2pt in-house

Giulio Fabbian's first route: measure the 3×2pt statistics from the Level 3 inputs, which are not blinded (catalogues, n(z)), and apply one shift to the whole 6×2pt vector. This is what the CMBX self-blind does now. Comparing its 3×2pt blocks with the official ones then needs one of the routes above.

Whether the comparison should be blind at all

Agreement of the shared spectra between two codes is a measurement question; agreement on $S_8$ between CMB lensing and 3×2pt is a cosmology question. The key-holder and same-mocks routes test the first without revealing the second. The 3×2pt TR1 analysis plan names CMBX among the groups that "can potentially unblind WL-SWG" and points to JC-1 as the forum.
