Blinded TR1 shear × SPT-3G κ follows the fiducial

These are blinded data. Each panel is one cross-spectrum between Euclid TR1 shear in one of the six source bins and the SPT-3G Main-field CMB lensing map: $\ell C_\ell^{\gamma\kappa}$ in 15 NaMaster bandpowers from $\ell = 100$ to 3000, with error bars from the full NaMaster Gaussian covariance (the square root of its diagonal, $\sigma_G$), not a Knox estimate. The gold curve is the fiducial theory pushed through the same bandpower windows and harmonic response as the data. It is a fixed reference, not a fit to these points.

The strip under each panel is the residual $(\text{data} - \text{reference})/\sigma_G$, with the inner and outer bands at $\pm 1$ and $\pm 2$. Over all 90 bands, 3 lie beyond $2\sigma_G$, where about 4 are expected from Gaussian noise. The worst band is $-3.2\,\sigma_G$ at $\ell \approx 870$ in source bin 4, the only one beyond $3\sigma_G$ (about 0.24 expected).

Bands are normalised one at a time here. Neighbouring bands are correlated, so the strips show the size of each deviation, not a goodness of fit.
