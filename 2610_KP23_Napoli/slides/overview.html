The first half brings back what from the Napoli WLSWG × OU-SHE week bears on KP2-3: the DR1 calendar, including when the R3 shear reaches the collaboration, the TR1 Cycle 2 and Cycle 3 data, the 3×2pt discussion of scale cuts and priors, the first DR1 shear calibration and a test showing shift + stretch n(z) may not be enough, and CLOE's readiness for CMBX, with the emulator question. DR2 on DES and the EXT-DES photometry in the south are in backup. The second half repeats the TR1 results shown in Napoli, marked preliminary until this group has discussed them, plus two pieces made since: the extinction template's CIB test, made during the week, and the first blinded TR1 posteriors. These results come from the pipeline in dr1_notebooks (scratch/cdaley/, built on dr1_cmbx); they are not yet group products.
