Lightcone: where the pieces are linked and the spiral is run

  • Lightcone — reproducible, agent-run analysis infrastructure: every result records the decisions, inputs and code it came from, so pipelines, simulations and comparisons can be rerun and checked when anything upstream changes.
  • On screen — Studio, its reading surface, on Paper II of the UNIONS release: the analysis decisions (catalogue variant, blind, PSF-leakage correction, binning) beside the results they produce, with the lineage graph, provenance, data and bibliography a click away.
  • Why it closes the talk — it is the infrastructure that links the forward-model pieces and runs the spiral, for both analyses in this talk.
