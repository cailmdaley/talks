Three levels of a cosmology chain

  • The loop — one evaluation goes once around: the sampler proposes a point $\theta$, the theory predicts the data vector $t(\theta)$ there, the likelihood compares it with the data and returns $\ln L$, and the sampler uses it to propose the next point.
  • Sampler — nautilus (nested sampling) draws a point $\theta$ in the 38 sampled parameters of the TR1 data chains (37 on the GLASS mock, where $\log T_\mathrm{AGN}$ is fixed) and receives $\ln L$.
  • Likelihood — Gaussian: $\ln L = -\tfrac12 (d - t)^\mathsf{T} C^{-1} (d - t)$, with $d$ the measured bandpowers and $C$ the production Gaussian + SSC covariance.
  • Theory — the theory vector $t(\theta)$: the background ($H(z)$, $\chi(z)$) and $P(k, z)$ of the cosmology (CAMB with HMCode, or the emulator), with the nuisances, projected (Limber) to $C_\ell$ and through the bandpower windows.
  • Vector — 5×2pt after scale cuts: shear × shear, shear × positions, positions × positions, κ × shear and κ × positions; 90 pairs, 651 bands. κκ is not included yet.
  • Where a likelihood code sits — the theory and the likelihood levels together are what "Cobaya" (eDR1like with CCL) and "CLOE in JAX" each provide; the sampler is nautilus for both in what follows.
The nuisances

Per tomographic bin: a redshift shift $\Delta z$ for each of the six source and six lens bins, a shear multiplicative bias $m$ for each source bin, a magnification slope and a linear galaxy bias for each lens bin; and NLA intrinsic alignments ($A_\mathrm{IA}$, $\eta_\mathrm{IA}$). The baryonic feedback parameter $\log T_\mathrm{AGN}$ of HMCode-2020 sits with the cosmology. Priors are those of the DR1 photometric-cosmology document.
