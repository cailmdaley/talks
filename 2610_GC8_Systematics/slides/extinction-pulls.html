Every tracer correlates with extinction

Obscuration would lower counts (δ × E < 0) and leave γ and κ untouched.

What is plotted

Each TR1 tracer crossed with the VMPZ GALACTICEXTINCTION_VIS template, documented as built from the Planck E(B−V) map, band by band, as the cross-spectrum divided by its Gaussian (NaMaster) error: six lens bins δi (here the density maps without the visibility weights, which the previous two slides use), six LensMC source bins γi (E-modes), and SPT-3G CMB lensing κ. Shaded: ±1 and ±2σ. Same estimator, masks and 15 logarithmic bands as the null tests on the earlier slides.

The numbers (χ² over 15 bands, full Gaussian covariance; κ diagonal only)

  • δ₁ to δ₆: χ²/15 = 22.9, 45.0, 36.1, 70.4, 79.7, 67.7; the coherent pull Σ pull/√15 is +2.8, +4.8, +4.3, +5.8, +6.5, +5.8.
  • γ₁ to γ₄: 7.6 to 13.7 (coherent +0.2 to +1.5); γ₅, γ₆: 18.9, 19.6 (coherent +3.3, +3.6).
  • κ: 19.9 (coherent +2.9), with the diagonal of the covariance only.
  • Over all 180 δ and γ bands the mean pull is +0.87, and 23 bands exceed 2σ against 8 expected.

The six δ bins share one template, so their significances are correlated, not six independent detections.

Reading

Dust obscuration would lower galaxy counts where E(B−V) is high, a negative δ × E, and could not correlate with shear or with a CMB lensing map at all. Instead every tracer is positive, the δ signal grows with ℓ and is weakest in the lowest-redshift lens bin, and the shear signal appears only in the highest-redshift source bins, which large-scale structure at z ≈ 1–2 lenses most. That is the signature of large-scale structure in the template: far-infrared dust-emission maps carry the cosmic infrared background (CIB) from star-forming galaxies at z ≈ 1–3, which trace the same structure as the Euclid samples. The next slide tests this directly. For κ alone, Galactic dust leaking into the SPT-3G lensing reconstruction would also give a positive κ × dust; it cannot explain the δ and γ rows.

With the visibility weights

Weighting δ by the VMPZ test visibility maps (the version on the first slide) removes most of this: extinction in lens bins 2, 3, 4 and 6 goes to PTE 0.12–0.97, and only δ₅ × extinction remains (PTE $9\times10^{-8}$). If extinction is among the visibility model's inputs, the weights remove the CIB-carried correlation by construction, along with the clustering it traces (inferred; the five SOM inputs are not named in the headers).

Deprojection

Deprojecting this template from δ changes the δδ bandpowers little (Q₂C = 0.007–0.046, at most 0.2σ per band), but in the direction of the CIB-correlated modes: it subtracts real clustering.
