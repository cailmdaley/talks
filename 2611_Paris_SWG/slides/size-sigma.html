The CMBX blind is large in units of σ

±0.075CMBX $S_8$ envelope today
0.016σ($S_8$), blinded TR1 chain
≈0.022σ($S_8$), DR1, scaled from TR1
  • The comparison — the other groups size their blind in units of the expected posterior σ; CMBX uses a fixed envelope. Converting ours to σ units makes the two comparable.
  • ±0.075 — half-width of the uniform $S_8$ envelope of the CMBX self-blind (Redmine #31813), drawn around Planck 2018.
  • 0.016 — posterior width of $S_8$ in the blinded TR1 chain on the full vector (κ crosses and 3×2pt, SPT-3G κ). A width, not a value: the blind moves the posterior's centre, not its width, to first order, unless the draw sits near the envelope edge (see the previous slide's notes).
  • ≈0.02 — an estimate, not a computation: TR1 is about 30 % of the DR1 area, so σ($S_8$) for DR1 is roughly $0.016\sqrt{0.3} \approx 0.009$, and 2σ about 0.018–0.02. The CMBX envelope is then three to four times the ~2σ the other groups use.
  • With κκ — adding the CMB-lensing auto-spectrum is expected to tighten σ($S_8$) substantially (not computed), so a 2σ blind sized on 6×2pt + κκ would be smaller still.
How to make it a number rather than an estimate

The GLASS mock chains of the CMBX pipeline (20 seeds on the TR1 footprint) give the posterior width directly; a DR1-footprint forecast run through the same chain would give the DR1 σ that a σ-based envelope needs. JC-1 and JC-2 draw their shifts from mock posteriors of the probe being blinded, which puts the shift in σ units by construction.

TR1 versus DR1

A blind of 2σ of DR1 (≈0.0175–0.02) is about 1.1–1.25σ of TR1. Whether TR1 needs a strong blind at all is open: the 3×2pt pipeline call of 13 November had "Plans for unblind analysis on TR1 South" on its agenda (outcome not known when these slides were made).
