UNIONS images the northern sky from Hawaiʻi

  • CFHT r, u · Pan-STARRS i · Subaru g, z
  • over 5,000 deg² above Dec ≈ +30°
  • depth comparable to LSST Y1
  • median r-band seeing 0.7″
  • UNIONS — the Ultraviolet Near-Infrared Optical Northern Survey: CFIS (CFHT r and u), WISHES and WHIGS (Subaru Hyper Suprime-Cam z and g) and Pan-STARRS i.
  • Shapes — weak-lensing shapes are measured on the CFHT r band with ShapePipe (Guinot et al. 2022).
Sources

Gwyn et al. 2025 (arXiv:2503.13783) for the bands and the 6,250 deg² core footprint. Depth and seeing are from the same paper: UNIONS is “only slightly shallower in photometric depth than Year 1 of the LSST” (its section on UNIONS science), and the median CFHT r-band image quality is 0.7″.
