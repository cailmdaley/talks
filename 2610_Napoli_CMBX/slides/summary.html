Summary

  1. Five CMBX key projects cross Euclid with CMB lensing, tSZ, kSZ and the CIB
  2. The KP2-3 pipeline runs end to end and recovers GLASS mocks at every stage
  3. On TR1, shear and κ pass every systematics template; clustering's failures are understood
  4. Next: TR1 chains, the FLASK covariance and DR1 clustering × κ

Cail Daley · CEA Paris-Saclay · for the Euclid CMBX SWG

The talk in four lines. The CMBX working group's DR1 key projects cross Euclid with the secondary anisotropies of the CMB: lensing (KP1-2, KP2-3), the thermal Sunyaev–Zel'dovich effect (KP4), the kinetic one through peculiar velocities (KP5), and further probes including the cosmic infrared background. The KP2-3 pipeline goes from catalogues to a blinded likelihood and is validated on GLASS 6×2pt mocks stage by stage. On TR1 data, no shear or CMB-lensing cross with any of six survey-property templates fails, while galaxy density fails against extinction (the CIB in the dust map) and against exposure and noise in the first lens bin (a depth effect). Next come chains on TR1, the numerical covariance from the FLASK simulations, and the move to DR1, starting with galaxy clustering × CMB lensing.
