Timelines: DR1 data products and the analysis

Milestones

  • DR1-Foundation on 12 Nov 2026; full public DR1 in June 2027 (day 1). The SAS freezes at the end of March 2027, so 2PCFs and P(k) must be unblinded by then. About two months of ECP review is assumed, so papers should be essentially final by March, with submission on release day.
  • Papers on DR1-Foundation or DR1 can go to arXiv as soon as the datasets are in the internal SAS; only SHE and LE3 technical papers wait for the public release. Anything used in a paper must be in the SAS. ESA publicises only accepted papers, so the EC plans its own announcement (day 1).
  • Day 3 roadmap: Nov 2026, validate the blinding shifts and the first SHE-CAL m; Dec, full (non-final) chains on blinded DR1; Jan 2027, final scale cuts; Feb, final R3 catalogues and LE3 data vectors; Mar, unblinding; June, release.
  • The 3×2pt splinter plans a full DR1 production run for end Nov / early Dec, taking about a week; day 2 puts the final scale cuts in Dec–Jan.
  • DR2 session: EC-internal DR2 in June 2028, public in June 2029; no WL LE3 before internal DR2; the first DR2 shear is likely MetaCal-only.
  • Papers: drafts go into ECP review at about the end of March, so that the ~2 months of review end by the June release, and are submitted on release day. They go in even if numbers may still shift: the DR1-Foundation strong-lensing papers were written ahead with TBD numbers, and only a final update was needed (day 1, release-papers discussion).

Data products

  • TR1 Cycle 2 (CosmoHub TR1.B v2.0) is the TR1 area cut from the full DR1 photo-z production: the same v1.1 shear measurements, with new tomographic binning and the DR1 n(z) (Farrens and Hartley, 14 Oct). Catalogues are on CosmoHub, unblinded over ~500 deg², with analysis guidelines to avoid accidental unblinding (day 2). The CosmoHub tom_bin_id fix is still pending, so the PHZ bin cuts have to be applied directly.
  • R3 reprocessing (Andy Taylor, OU-SHE, day 1) ran Jun–Aug 2026 and completed in September, with a limited release to the WL-SWG, the B-mode Tiger Team and the KPs for 2-pt validation; the EC-wide release is aimed at end Oct / start Nov 2026. R3 is the first change to the shear measurement itself: the PSF size bias, a few per cent and varying across the sky in R2, is about 10× smaller, and the southern harmonic-space B-mode excess goes from ~5σ to below 2σ. Reading Cycle 3 as the R3 selection follows from the day-1 timeline. The TR1 shear in the results shown here is pre-R3.
  • n(z) (Hartley, day 1): close to final at end Nov 2026 for the Cycle 3 selection and DR1 (DR1_WLSOUTH_R3); final, with confident uncertainties, at end Feb 2027. The working assumption is that DR1 won't do much better than Δz ~ 0.02(1+z) on the mean and 0.05(1+z) on the width; Hildebrandt (day 3) put ~2 % on mean z as not good enough for DR1 cosmic shear, needing ~1 % or better. The n(z) are NO_WEIGHT only so far, and whether a stretch will be delivered was disputed in the day-3 splinter.
  • m bias: the first DR1 SHE-CAL run came in at Napoli (calibration slide); the DR1 m-bias correction lands November to February (day 2) and comes with the final calibrated catalogues in February. No separate final-m date was given.
  • Still moving: the static c-bias is out (Tiger Team), with a final decision in December 2026 (3×2pt splinter); LensMC B-modes came back significant with flipped sign, pending further runs (CLOE splinter).

Where things live

Catalogues: CosmoHub. LE3 products: PIC WebDAV (wl-pipeline wiki, Data_Access). n(z) and VMPZ tile products: the internal DR1 SAS. Coverage, mixing matrices and n(z) for the candidate DR1 tile set: PIC (Tessore). Tile list: gitlab.euclid-sgs.uk/PF-LE3-WL/tile-lists. euclidlib PR #154 reads the SAS n(z) product.

In the pipeline shown here

The Cycle 2 universe (tr1_v2, SPT-3G) is built through blinded data vectors and is being re-materialized; it moves to Cycle 3 when that ships, and the LensMC R2 → R3 difference doubles as a PSF-only systematics test of the κ crosses. Low-level shear issues (c-bias, bleeding-edge mask, B-mode nulls) are left to OU-SHE and WL here, and acted on only where κ brings something in; this is not a group decision.
