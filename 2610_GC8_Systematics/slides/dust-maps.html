Alternative extinction maps on the TR1 sky

All three maps use the Cycle-2 prepared support and the same equatorial TR1 South projection as the first map slide. The absolute Planck13 panel is the prepared A_VIS map with its monopole restored, divided by 2.108 to recover physical E(B−V). For the middle residual only, a local affine fit A_VIS = a·SFD+b converts Planck to SFD calibration: (A_VIS−b)/a − SFD. The final panel is CSFD−SFD with no fitted scale or intercept; both public maps are on the original SFD calibration, without the Schlafly–Finkbeiner 0.86 rescaling. The panels therefore all have reddening units, but the absolute Planck map retains its physical calibration while the middle panel removes the local calibration/zero-point difference before comparison. This is not a literal native-unit A_VIS−SFD subtraction.

Every panel has its own labelled colourbar: absolute E(B−V) 0–0.085 mag, Planck residual ±0.006 mag, CSFD residual ±0.002 mag. The CSFD residual stretch is three times tighter; its smaller amplitude must be read from the colourbar, not the colour saturation. White is outside the prepared support; saturated tails are marked by colourbar extensions. No beam matching is applied, so residuals include sampling and resolution differences. The negative CSFD−SFD field is the removed LSS correction; its correlation with density was checked in round 4 (unweighted pixel Pearson −0.154 to −0.124), not a harmonic significance.
