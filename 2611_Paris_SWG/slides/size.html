How big should the CMBX blind be?

  • Consortium plan: ~5σ of final errors; JC-1, GC spectro: ~2σ of DR1
  • JC-2: shift drawn from the posterior of the probe being blinded
  • CMBX now: $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$ ±0.1, $S_8$ ±0.075, uniform
  • Too small: a shift reads as “the $S_8$ tension”
  • Too large: blinded posteriors change shape

Decide: one envelope, or a large cross-only and a small full one?

  • Consortium — "Blinding at the ~5σ level (where σ is the expected level of errors from the primary probes alone at end of Euclid) seems reasonable"; "Level of blinding: 5σ for GC+WL (Euclid only)" (Science Coordination Group, Copenhagen 2023, DMSF 19230).
  • JC-1 — "run an MCMC (realistic in some way) … and sample two sigma (?) from it in each relevant parameter" (Marseille 2025, DMSF 26503); still a question mark there. The blindspot photometric prototype draws from a weak-lensing chain's posterior.
  • GC spectro — cosmologies "consistent with 2-sigma of the confidence interval estimated for DR1" (blindspot issue #40), enforced by rejection on the BAO and RSD parameters (Redmine #35938, #36596).
  • JC-2 photometric, in practice — 100 draws from a weak-lensing chain posterior on FS2 (two parameters sampled), each turned into a $C_\ell$ shift with CLOElib relative to the FS2 fiducial (Felicitas Keil's slides, JC-2 blinding-test call, 5 November 2026). The validation draw sat about 1σ from the WL best fit; WL recovered it, but 2×2pt was "blinded in the correct direction / much too strongly", hitting the prior, because the same point is several σ of the tighter 2×2pt posterior. The next run draws the 2×2pt shift from a 2×2pt posterior (Keil, Slack, 10 November).
  • JC-2 pass criterion and timeline — on a noiseless synthetic vector, "a recovered shift consistent with the injected one and a posterior of unchanged width" (Alessio Spurio Mancini, #dr1-kp-jc-2, 11 November). Inputs: nowcast v2 South with Davide Sciotti's covariances; shifts regenerated by 17 November; decision at JC-2 on 24 November, a week after this meeting.
  • CMBX — the envelope in Redmine #31813, drawn around Planck 2018; roughly the size of the $S_8$ tension, which the 2023 consortium slides put at ~2σ.
Too small

If the envelope is about the size of the $S_8$ tension, a large blinded offset reads as "that is probably the tension", and that reading leaks into analysis choices. The envelope should be wide enough that a blinded offset says nothing about where the truth sits relative to the tension.

Too large

The shift is computed at fixed nuisances, so it is exact only to first order (Muir et al. 2020, App. A). On a CMBX GLASS mock, a blind at 82 % of the $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$ envelope narrowed the $\Omega_\mathrm{m}$ posterior threefold and halved the bias widths; a blind at 44 % translated cleanly. Blinded widths near the envelope edge can be wrong by ~3×, which matters for any decision that reads σ off a blinded chain. The residual also grows with how wrong the fiducial galaxy bias is: at mock signal-to-noise, a 20 % bias error left $\Delta\chi^2$ of 0.7–1.1 $\sqrt{2\nu}$ at the envelope corners, a 10 % error 0.2–0.3 $\sqrt{2\nu}$. DESI DR1's 3×2pt re-optimizes the bias at each blinding cosmology to avoid this.

Uneven

At fixed bias the clustering amplitude scales as $b^2\sigma_8^2$. On one throwaway draw on a GLASS mock, δδ moved by up to 22σ per band at high ℓ, γγ by at most 1.35σ, and the κ crosses by under 0.7σ. Free biases absorb most of the δδ shift, so the per-band numbers overstate it; the strength of a blind is better measured in parameter space, after marginalizing over nuisances. That calculation has not been done.

Two envelopes

Margherita Lembo (KP2/3 meeting, 8 October 2026): a larger blind for cross-only analyses and "a small one for the full analysis, compatible with the GC and WL blinds."
