Injected PSF leakage shows which fit recovers it

  • 58 realizations on real v2 positions, weights and PSF fields
  • known leakage injected, then refit
  • standard least squares: $\xi_{\rm sys}$ 23–35% low
  • errors in variables: within −2% to +7%
  • Leakage model — a galaxy's ellipticity picks up $\alpha\,e_{\rm PSF} + \beta\,\delta e_{\rm PSF} + \eta\,(\delta T/T)\,e_{\rm PSF},$ with $(\alpha, \beta, \eta)$ fitted to star–galaxy correlation functions (the $\rho$ and $\tau$ statistics). $\xi_{\rm sys}$ is the additive term in $\xi_+$ that the fitted parameters predict; it is what reaches cosmology.
  • Test — each realization keeps the real v2 galaxy positions, weights and PSF fields, rotates the galaxy shapes at random, and injects known leakage near the data's fit, $(\alpha, \beta, \eta) = (0.015, 1.2, 1.0).$ The fits then run as on data.
  • Curves — the mean relative error of $\xi_{\rm sys}$ from the fitted parameters against the injected truth. Standard least squares comes out 23–35 % low from 1′ to 221′: the noisy $\rho$ design dilutes the fit, and the galaxy and star fields were centered inconsistently. Every errors-in-variables variant is within −2 to +7 %, against a floor of about +3 % from noise.
Caveats

No cosmic shear in the realizations, so they do not reproduce the data's $\chi^2$ growth with patch count; a rotated GLASS shear field is the next step. The injected $\beta$ is constant by construction. The errors-in-variables fit reports $\alpha$ and $\eta$ errors 0.55–0.65 times the true scatter, so those two need profile-likelihood or mock-calibrated intervals; $\beta$'s errors are calibrated. No cosmology was fitted.
