Galactic dust messes with galaxy counts

  • VMPZ extinction: Planck 2013 dust map
    $A_\mathrm{VIS}=2.108\,E(B{-}V)$
  • Fluxes: dereddened magnitudes
  • Visibility maps: no residual correlation of counts with dust
  • The map — VIS-band extinction $A_\mathrm{VIS}$ in magnitudes on the TR1 South footprint; white is outside the prepared support.
  • Planck 2013 — the EBV column of the Planck 2013 thermal-dust model (file HFI_CompMap_ThermalDustModel_2048_R1.20; “R1.20” is just the file’s release tag).
  • 2.108 — $A_\mathrm{VIS}$ = $R_V$ × ($A_\mathrm{VIS}/A_V$) × E(B−V) = 3.1 × 0.68 × E(B−V).
  • Two corrections — dust dims and reddens galaxies, so the photometry is dereddened, and the GC visibility map then removes any residual dependence of the galaxy counts on dust. That second step assumes the dust map holds no large-scale structure.
  • Space — the next step switches the map to Cycle 2: the support changes, the dust values do not.
Where 2.108 comes from

E(B−V) is a reddening: the colour excess between the B and V bands. Turning it into an extinction in a given band takes two factors. $R_V$ = $A_V$/E(B−V) = 3.1 is the standard Milky-Way total-to-selective ratio, giving the V-band extinction. The VMPZ generator then scales to the Euclid VIS band with $a_\mathrm{VIS}$ = $A_\mathrm{VIS}/A_V$ = 0.68. So $A_\mathrm{VIS}$ = 3.1 × 0.68 × E(B−V) = 2.108 E(B−V), in magnitudes. It is one global constant: the map’s spatial structure is entirely Planck’s.

Which map the photometry used

Which dust map the MER/PHZ dereddening used is not yet confirmed (open question from the 26 October discussion with Sam Farrens). If it is also Planck R1.20, CIB could modulate the selection through the fluxes as well. The visibility map is built offline, so its dust input can be changed for DR1.

How the map is sampled

The OU-VMPZ-ID GalacticExtinction InfoMap samples the Galactic-coordinate Planck parent (Planck Collaboration XI 2014; nominal 5-arcmin resolution) at each equatorial tile-pixel centre by nearest-parent-pixel lookup, without additional smoothing or interpolation.

Why it matters for GC

These templates feed the offline GC visibility maps. The delivered VMPZ extinction InfoMaps are frozen for DR1; changing the offline visibility input is a separate proposal (discussion with Sam Farrens, 26 Oct: gc8-systematics-update/meetings/2026-10-26-1532-sam-vmpz-csfd).
