Alternative extinction maps

  • Left — the VMPZ (Planck 2013) map in E(B−V).
  • Middle — VMPZ minus SFD, after fitting out their calibration difference: what Planck sees that SFD does not.
  • Right — CSFD minus SFD: the extragalactic (CIB) correction Chiang (2023) removes from SFD. This is the LSS SFD carries.
  • Colourbars — each panel has its own; the right one is three times tighter than the middle.
How each panel is made

All three use the Cycle-2 prepared support and the same equatorial TR1 South projection as slide 2. Left: the prepared $A_\mathrm{VIS}$ map with its monopole restored, divided by 2.108 to recover E(B−V). Middle: a local affine fit $A_\mathrm{VIS}$ = a·SFD + b converts Planck to SFD calibration, then ($A_\mathrm{VIS}$ − b)/a − SFD. This is not a literal native-unit subtraction. Right: CSFD − SFD with no fitted scale or intercept; both public maps are on the original SFD calibration, without the Schlafly–Finkbeiner 0.86 rescaling. No beam matching is applied, so residuals include sampling and resolution differences.

Colour stretches

Absolute E(B−V) 0–0.085 mag; Planck residual ±0.006 mag; CSFD residual ±0.002 mag. White is outside the prepared support; saturated tails are marked by colourbar extensions.

Is the CSFD correction really LSS?

The negative CSFD − SFD field is the removed LSS correction; its unweighted pixel Pearson correlation with the pre-26-October Cycle-2 density maps was −0.154 to −0.124 across bins (round 4; not recomputed on the official bins), not a harmonic significance. The correlations on slides 5–6, not visual cleanliness, test the residual extragalactic imprint.
