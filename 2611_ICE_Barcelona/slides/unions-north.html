UNIONS adds weak lensing in the north

  • different telescopes and different systematics
  • excellent overlap with DESI
  • Globe — the animation opens on the southern Stage III lensing footprints (DES, KiDS, HSC) with Euclid DR1 in violet, rises to the smaller UNIONS patch in the southern Galactic cap (globe centre RA 2°, Dec +32°), then turns in right ascension alone, Dec held at +32°, to the main UNIONS area in the northern Galactic cap (RA 193°), and stops there on UNIONS, Euclid DR1's northern patch and, along the equator, HSC and KiDS-North. Orange is UNIONS, the v2 shape catalog. The dashed pale line with no fill is the DESI spectroscopic footprint.
  • Overlap — small: HSC's HectoMap field (about 100 deg² at Dec +42.5° to +44.5°) lies inside UNIONS, and a few DR6 tiles fall inside DES (Stripe 82, CFHTLS-W1).
  • Significance — a cosmic-shear measurement with its own instrument and its own sky is an independent check on the Stage III results, and the northern sky is where DESI and SPHEREx spectroscopy overlap it.

Drawn

DES: the DES wide-survey envelope (public MOC, 5,155 deg²). KiDS: the KiDS-N/S science-quality mask (public MOC, 891 deg²). HSC: the six HSC Y3 shear fields, digitised from Li et al. 2023 Fig. 1 (572 deg² gross). These three come from the Survey Footprint Explorer and the ADE-talk footprint set. UNIONS: the 22,901 DR6 tiles in the v2 shape catalog, from the public production status page, drawn as whole half-degree tiles before star masks (the masked catalog covers 5,223 deg²). Euclid DR1: the observed DR1 VIS footprint, recovered from the ADE-talk globe video because the DR1 MOC is internal; its edges are good to a few tenths of a degree, and it covers about 2,550 deg² against the MOC's 2,346 deg². DESI: the main-survey footprint, the union of 1.605°-radius discs about the 9,929 dark-time tiles flagged IN_DESI in tiles-main.ecsv from the public DESI DR1 surveyops tag 1.0 (data.desi.lbl.gov/public/dr1/survey/ops/surveyops/tags/1.0/ops/), smoothed over 1.2° to round off the tile edges; about 14,900 deg². Sky: Gaia EDR3 colour map (ESA/Gaia/DPAC). Generator: make_globe.py in this deck.
