SPT covariance

1. SPT-constrained mocks for accuracy
2. SPT-like mocks for as many samples as needed
3. The two combined, with control variates (CARPool-type)

  • Scope — what the SPT × Euclid 6×2pt analysis needs from the covariance effort (Pauline Vielzeuf, Davide Luchina) for its κ blocks, as data products: what goes in and what should come out.
  • Plan — constrained realizations on SPT's own lensing reconstructions carry the real estimator and noise but are limited to SPT's 350–400 simulations; SPT-like mocks with a generic quadratic estimator can be made in any number. The idea is to use the first for accuracy and the second for sample count, combining them with a control-variate (CARPool-type) estimator. A plan to discuss with the covariance team, not a settled method.
  • Timeline — the κκ likelihood and covariance design is to be decided by the end of November; everything running by the end of February; blinded data early April 2027 (CMBX telecons, 5 and 12 November 2026).
