Resolved contamination stays under half a σ

Built like the data-vector slide: first the CMB lensing row close up (its shear half, then its galaxy half), then the whole triangle, cell for cell as on that slide, but each panel now shows how much a systematics template S could move that pair if its measured cross with each tracer were all leakage: $X_\ell^{ab} = C_\ell^{aS} C_\ell^{bS} / C_\ell^{SS}$, in units of $\sigma_G$ of $C_\ell^{ab}$. Each panel spans $\pm 1.5\,\sigma_G$, with $\pm 1\,\sigma_G$ shaded; the bars are $\sigma_X/\sigma_G$, first-order error propagation that ignores the covariance between the two crosses and treats the template's auto-spectrum with a Gaussian error, which is least reliable at a tiling-periodic template's harmonics.

Framed cells, in ochre, are those where at least one of the two template crosses behind the cell fails: its χ² PTE over the 15 bands is below 0.01 (full Gaussian covariance; κ diagonal), or its bands lean one way, with Σpull/√15 beyond ±3, a same-sign shift that χ² misses. Inside a card, the failing cross carries its PTE in ochre. With LensMC × SPT-3G, extinction frames every pair touching lens bins 2–6 and source bins 5–6, which is its CIB signal. Exposures, noise and PSF frame the pairs touching lens bin 1, a depth effect. Zodiacal light and stars frame lens bin 3, both by a one-way lean (+3.5 and −3.2), not by χ². Extinction × κ leans +2.9 (SPT-3G) and +2.2 (ACT DR6), below the threshold.

The dashed lines in exposures, noise and PSF cards mark where each template's own power peaks: the bands at ℓ ≈ 438, 551 and 1088, the same in all three. A fundamental between the first two bands (ℓ ≈ 400–620) is a pattern repeating every 360°/ℓ ≈ 0.6–0.9°, consistent with the ≈0.7° Euclid field of view stepping across the survey, and ℓ ≈ 1088 is its second harmonic. The bands are too coarse to pin the period more tightly. At these multipoles the template is far from a Gaussian field, so the Gaussian σ of its crosses is least trustworthy there.

The buttons at the top right pick the template; the variant control picks the shear method and CMB map; a click on a cell (on the full triangle) opens it with the two template crosses that make it, each with its χ² over 15 bands against zero and PTE (full Gaussian covariance; κ diagonal only). Where X is resolved, nothing exceeds about 0.4 $\sigma_G$: the largest are exposures in δ1δ1 ($0.35 \pm 0.15$) and extinction in δ5δ5 ($0.24 \pm 0.12$; δκ at most $0.18 \pm 0.10$). The nominal maxima above 1 (exposures δ6δ6 at $\ell \approx 349$, PSF δ4γ1 at $\ell \approx 691$) sit at dips of the template's own power, where $\sigma_X$ is as large as X. The lens-bin-1 depth signature costs δ1δ1 about a third of $\sigma_G$ per band. All four shear × CMB variants are measured; the δ crosses are the same in every variant.
