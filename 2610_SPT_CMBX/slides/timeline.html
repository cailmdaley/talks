Timeline to the June 2027 release

The Euclid DR1 lane is the DR1 plan from the Napoli meeting. DR1-Foundation is released on 12 November 2026. The blinded DR1 R3 shear catalogues reach the whole Euclid Consortium at the end of October or start of November (R3 is the reprocessing with a much smaller PSF-size bias). The multiplicative shear bias m comes from a first SHE-CAL run in November, with final values in February. The final calibrated catalogues follow in February, with the final n(z) at the end of February, when the products freeze. The two-point measurements are unblinded by the end of March, and the public DR1 release is in June 2027, when the DR1 key-project papers are submitted.

The SPT × Euclid lane is this paper's plan. Blinded TR1 chains exist now (shown later in this talk). Full but non-final chains on blinded DR1 follow in December, as in the DR1 roadmap. The final data vectors and chains come in February and March, on the frozen products. Papers go into the Euclid Consortium's internal (ECP) review at about the end of March, so that the roughly two months of review end by the June release, and are submitted on release day. They go in even if numbers may still shift: the DR1-Foundation strong-lensing papers were written ahead with TBD numbers and needed only a final update. The SPT products listed later in the talk are needed for the covariance, so they come before the final data vectors.

Sources: Napoli day 1 (DR1 timeline, release papers, ECP review timing), day 2 (3×2pt pipeline group's DR1 timeline) and day 3 (roadmap).
