How CMBX can help weak lensing

  • Source n(z) × m: lens ratio, no image simulations; TR1 at ±15 %
  • Intrinsic alignments: lens ratios give $A_{\rm IA} = 0.05 \pm 0.7$, cosmology-insensitive
  • Survey-property maps: extinction template likely carries the CIB

Three places where the CMB-lensing crosses touch the weak-lensing and OU-SHE work in this room, offered as openings for collaboration rather than results.

Source redshifts and shear calibration: the lens ratio $C^{\gamma_i\delta}/C^{\kappa\delta}$ measures each source bin's $(1+m_i)$ times its lensing efficiency against the CMB's, with galaxy bias and $\sigma_8$ cancelling. On TR1, with intrinsic alignments in the templates, the shared amplitude is consistent with 1 in every variant: 0.85 to 0.95, with errors of about 15 %, so 0.3 to 1σ low. The four variants share galaxies (and, per CMB map, the same κ), so sitting low together is one fluctuation counted several times, not four. Per bin the errors are 15 to 40 %, against the 0.3 to 1 % the Euclid requirements on $m$ and the bin mean redshifts allow (the lens-ratio slide); it is a consistency check now, independent of image simulations, and its precision grows with area and with the CMB map's depth.

Intrinsic alignments: the IA slide. From the $\gamma\delta/\kappa\delta$ ratios alone, with cosmology entering only through distances, $A_{\rm IA} = 0.05^{+0.66}_{-0.69}$ (LensMC × SPT-3G; the other variants 0.15–0.28, same width). In the joint fit to the full blinded data vector, adding NLA intrinsic alignments improves the fit by $\Delta\chi^2 \approx 13$–20 with $A_{\rm IA} \approx 0.4$–0.6 (blinded data; about 1–2σ above the mock baseline).

Survey-property maps: the Planck E(B−V) extinction template correlates positively with every TR1 tracer, the opposite of dust obscuration. It most likely reads as cosmic infrared background leaking into the far-infrared dust map, so the template carries large-scale structure and must not be used to deproject the galaxy maps; swapping in a CIB-cleaned dust map (Planck GNILC) is the direct test, not yet run.
