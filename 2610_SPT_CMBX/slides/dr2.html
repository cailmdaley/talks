Euclid DR2 will overlap well with SPT-3G

  • DR2 south: 3570 deg²
  • internal DR2: June 2028
  • the DR1 analysis is the DR2 pipeline

Where this comes from

Hervé Aussel, “Toward DR2”, Euclid WL-SWG × OU-SHE meeting, Napoli, 16 October 2026: Euclid observed 7234 deg² for DR2, 3570 deg² of it in the south and 3664 deg² in the north. DR2 data acquisition runs to June 2027; the internal DR2 release is planned for June 2028 and the public one for June 2029. The southern survey stays close to DES, which Euclid needs for its photometric redshifts, and SPT-3G Main lies inside the DES footprint.

For SPT × Euclid

The DR2 southern area grows Euclid's overlap with the SPT-3G fields well beyond DR1's, and the DR1 analysis is the pipeline DR2 would run. The DR2 × SPT-3G overlap area has not been computed yet; this slide claims only the direction.
